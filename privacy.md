## Information Collection and Use

### Location Data
**aTrainingTracker** collects location data to enable workout tracking, route recording, and real-time performance metrics (speed, distance, ascent) even when the app is closed or not in use (background recording). Location data is collected only while a tracking session is active and is stored exclusively on your device.

### Sensor and Biometric Data
The app is designed to collect and store data from paired physical sensors via ANT+ or Bluetooth LE (Low Energy). This includes, but is not limited to:
*   Heart Rate
*   Cycling Power and Cadence
*   Running Speed and Cadence
*   Ambient Temperature
*   Barometric Altitude

### Data Storage and Sharing
All tracked data is stored locally in a private database on your device. **I do not collect, store, or have access to your personal workout data on any external servers.** 

Your data is only shared with third-party services if you explicitly choose to:
1.  **Upload to Strava**: If enabled, your workout metrics and GPS paths are sent to Strava.
2.  **Upload to Dropbox**: If enabled, workout files (TCX, GPX, etc.) are synced to your private Dropbox account.
3.  **Manual Export**: If you manually export a workout file (TCX, GPX, etc.) to your device storage or share it with another application.

## Log Data

In the event of an error in the app, anonymized data called "Log Data" is collected through third-party products (Firebase Crashlytics). This may include your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, app configuration at the time of the crash, and other statistics.
