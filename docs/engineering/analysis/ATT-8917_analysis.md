# Stage 1 Analysis: ATT-8917 - Detect Triathlon, Duathlon, and Brick Sport Transitions with Retroactive Dismount/Mount Anchoring and Automatic Named Laps

**Ticket**: `ATT-8917` (`3 SP`, `Feature`) | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-5908` | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0`  
**Active Sprint**: `2026-11-19_01_Früh` | **Branch**: `feature/ATT-8917` (base `05096023` / post-`ATT-8891` `410240a8`) | **Author**: AI Agent 1 (Implementer) | **Date**: 2026-11-19

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
* During triathlon (`Swim -> T1 -> Bike -> T2 -> Run`), duathlon (`Run -> T1 -> Bike -> T2 -> Run`), and cycling-running brick workouts, manual lap/sport switching with wet hands or while racking a bike is error-prone and frequently missed.
* `ATT-8890` (`PhoneCarriageClassifier`, `PhoneCarriageMode`) and `ATT-8891` (`KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator`, `BANALService` Tier 3 inferred `BSportType`) now classify 1-Hz carriage modes (`STATIONARY`, `WALKING_TRANSITION`, `BIKE_*`, `RUN_*`) and infer live `BIKE`/`RUN` sport identity, yet no multisport state machine tracks dismount/mount boundary timestamps (`t_exit_sport1`, `t_enter_sport2`), anchors retroactive named laps (`Bike`, `Transition`, `Run`) in `Laps.db`, or transitions the live cockpit (`BIKE <-> RUN`) cleanly across hardware and kinematic signals.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
* **Existing Pipeline**:
  * `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` (`1,417` LoC) classifies `PhoneCarriageMode` at 1 Hz (`:800-898`) and invokes `sportDiscriminator.onCarriageMode(mode, timestampMs)` (`:818`) + `publishInferredSportTelemetry()` (`:1278-1290`).
  * `BANALService.java` (`1,019` LoC) resolves `getBSportType()` (`:531-535`) as `Tier 1 (mHaveUserSelectedSportType) > Tier 2 (DeviceManager.getSportType()) > Tier 3 (mInferredBSportType)`, maps `getActivityType()` (`:646-682`) into `BANALServiceRepository.activityType` (`:467-470`) -> `TrackingTabsScreen` (`:373-492`) for sport-specific tabs/units (`km/h` vs. `min/km`, cycling `RPM` vs. running `strides/min`), and broadcasts `LAP_SUMMARY` in `newLap()` (`:563-617`).
  * `LapsDatabaseManager.java` (`612` LoC, `DB_VERSION = 3`) and `WorkoutRepository.kt` (`4,931` LoC, `:1434-1449`) already support `Laps.NAME` and `Laps.TIME_START`; `WorkoutLaps.kt:473`, `LapSplitCalculator.kt:74`, `LapData.getDisplayName()`, `FitFileWriter.kt:821-893`, `TCXFileWriter.kt:132`, and `GCFileWriter.java:149` already consume `LapData.name`/`timeStart`.
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification (7 Live-Code Blind Spots Found & Resolved)**:
  1. **Retroactive Accumulator Offset Bug (`MyAccumulatorSensor.java:138-174`, `ClockDevice.java:170-176`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java:282-284`, `BANALService.java:444-468`)**: `MyAccumulatorSensor.reset()` sets `mInitialValue = sub(mZeroValue, mValue)` *without* zeroing `mValue`; subsequently calling `BANALService.setInitialSensorValue(delta)` would overwrite `mInitialValue = delta`, emitting `delta + mValue` (`1880 s` instead of `15 s`!) AND polluting `MySensorManager.mInitialValues` globally for future sensors. **Mitigation**: Add `MyAccumulatorSensor.resetTo(N targetValue)` setting `mInitialValue = sub(targetValue, mValue)` (notifying listeners with `targetValue` and zero `MySensorManager` global mutation), invoked via a dedicated `BANALService` helper after `deviceManager.newLap()` to anchor `TIME_LAP` and `DISTANCE_m_LAP` to `(now - t_enter)` deltas while preserving the ongoing leg's anchored `timeStart` and `name` for `TrackerService.createNewLap()` at stop.
  2. **Manual / External Lap Interleaving & Boundary Clamping (`TrackerService.java:745-768`)**: If the athlete presses manual Lap between `t_exit` and `t_confirm` (or resumes an existing workout), raw `t_exit` may predate the current lap's start. **Mitigation**: `MultisportLapCoordinator` tracks `currentLapStartAnchor` (`timestampMs`, `activeTimeS`, `totalDistanceM`), updated on workout start/resume and every non-pause `BANALService.LAP_SUMMARY`; before persisting, clamp `effectiveExit = max(t_exit, currentLapStart)` and `effectiveEnter = max(t_enter, effectiveExit)`, skipping any closed leg whose clamped effort is `<= 0`.
  3. **Pause / Auto-Pause Gating & Persistable-Lap Evaluation Before `LAP_NR` Increment (`ClockDevice.java:108`, `LapsDatabaseManager.java:139-143`, `TrainingApplication`)**: `TIME_ACTIVE` and `TIME_LAP` freeze while `PAUSED` or `AUTO_PAUSED`. Confirming during pause or incrementing `LAP_NR` unconditionally by `+2` when Leg 1 or `Transition` is rejected by `REQ-TRK-002` (`lapTime <= 0 && lapDistance <= 0.0`) creates `1 -> 3` `LAP_NR` gaps and orphaned sample tags. **Mitigation**: Gate the `15 s` confirmation timer strictly on active unpaused tracking (`isTracking() && !isPaused() && !isAutoPaused()`, never confirming while `PAUSED`/`AUTO_PAUSED`), and evaluate persistable laps (`hasLeg1`, `hasTransition`) under `REQ-TRK-002` *before* deciding the `LAP_NR` advance (`+2` if both persist, `+1` if only one persists, `+0` if neither) and assigning sample `LAP_NR` labels.
  4. **Post-Workout ERTS `LAP_NR` Sync with ZERO Edits to `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.java` (`1,533 / 1,533` LoC Ceiling, `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt:236,729`, `FitFileWriter.kt:849-856`)**: `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.java` sits at its hard `1,533 / 1,533` LoC ceiling (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt:132`), and its existing `updateLapNumberForSampleRange()` (`:686-711`) takes `_id` row bounds rather than timestamps. **Mitigation**: Make **`0` edits** to `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.java`; have `MultisportLapCoordinator` execute the `LAP_NR` range update asynchronously on `mDbExecutor` (chained after `mTableInitializationFuture`) via `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.getInstance(context).getDatabase()` + `getTableName()` (or `WorkoutSamplesRepository.kt`, `128` LoC) filtering by `TIME_TOTAL` (or `CAST(strftime('%s', time) AS REAL)`) across the exact persisted `[effectiveExit, effectiveEnter)` and `[effectiveEnter, t_confirm]` windows.
  5. **Hardware `CONFLICT`, Parked Bike Sensor in `T2` & `PhoneCarriageClassifier` `cyclingPrior` Masking (`BANALService.java:511-535,646-682`, `DeviceManager.java:1043-1056`, `PhoneCarriageClassifier.kt:398`, `ActivityType.java:34-39`)**: (a) When both `BIKE_*` and `RUN_SPEED` sensors are active, `DeviceManager.getSportType()` returns `BSportType.CONFLICT`, blocking Tier 3 fallback. (b) A parked bike sensor can remain BLE-connected in `T2` emitting `0 W`/`0 RPM`; worse, `PhoneCarriageClassifier.kt:398` applies `cyclingPrior += 2.5` from `getAuthoritativeBSportType()`, which can mask running in `T2` when `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR` is unavailable. (c) `ActivityType` ordinals are `GENERIC_HR(0) < RUN_SPEED_AND_CADENCE(1) < BIKE_SPEED_AND_CADENCE(2) < BIKE_POWER(3)`, so `mActivityTypeMax` (`:672-678`) latches `BIKE_*` when `!changeSportWhenDeviceGetsLost()`. **Mitigation**: Fuse `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator`'s permission-free 50-Hz accel footstrike inference alongside `PhoneCarriageMode` and live non-zero hardware sensor activity in `MultisportTransitionDetector`; on confirmed transition (when `!mHaveUserSelectedSportType`), publish confirmed `BSportType` to `BANALService` overriding `CONFLICT`/silent-bike Tier 2, reset `mActivityTypeMax` to the new leg's `ActivityType`, and call `handleSportTypeChanged()` without firing `SPORT_TYPE_CHANGED_BY_USER_INTENT`.
  6. **Spurious `LapSummaryDialog` Popups & Pre-Tracking Guard (`BANALServiceRepository.kt:286-304`)**: Raw `BANALService.LAP_SUMMARY` (`IS_PAUSE=false`) pops up modal dialogs. **Mitigation**: Dispatch transitions via dedicated package-scoped `BANALService.MULTISPORT_TRANSITION_INTENT` handled by `TrackerService`/`MultisportLapCoordinator`, gated strictly on active tracking, and reset cleanly on `RESET_ACCUMULATORS_INTENT`, workout start/stop, and `onDestroy()`/`shutDown()`.
  7. **Strict Monolith Ceilings & 9-Locale Named Laps (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt:116-134`, `Rule 9.4`)**: `TrackerService.java` (`3,540 / 3,559`, **`+0`**), `DeviceManager.java` (`1,707 / 1,708`, **`0` edits**), `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.java` (`1,533 / 1,533`, **`0` edits**), `WorkoutRepository.kt` (`4,931 / 5,117`, **`+0`**), `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` (`1,417 / 1,500`, keep **`<= 1,455`**), `BANALService.java` (`1,019`, keep **`<= 1,075`**). Add localized `lap_name_bike`, `lap_name_transition`, `lap_name_run` across all 9 locales in `values*/strings_cockpit.xml` (English fallback `"Bike"`/`"Transition"`/`"Run"` in JVM tests; zero new settings UI screens per `ATT-1250`).

## 3. Scope Grounding & Non-Goals (`3 SP` Verified — No Split Needed)
