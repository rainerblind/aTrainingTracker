## Context & Problem Statement
Field failure on Pixel 10 (`5.0.0-dev-b956b379`, run `_id = 2422`, `2,742 s`) with **Polar H10** (`polarLiveImuEnabled = 1`) and **Polar Verity Sense** (`polarLiveImuEnabled = 1`) after `ATT-9176` landed:
1. `HR (Polar Verity Sense)` emitted **`79 bpm` identically for `2,738 / 2,742` s** (even resuming at `79 bpm` right after the `17:53:55` crash restart at `t = 924`!), and `RR_INTERVAL (Polar Verity Sense)` emitted **`659 ms` identically for `919 / 2,742` s** (`HRV_ARTIFACT_RATE` = `100%`, mean `99.82%`), while `CADENCE (Polar Verity Sense)` (`80.75 spm`) and `HR (Polar H10)` (`95–159 bpm`, avg `146.5 bpm`) streamed normally.
2. Canonical primary `HR` selected Verity Sense's frozen `79 bpm` over Polar H10's live `146.5 bpm`, corrupting `ExtremumValues` (`AVG = MIN = MAX = 79.0`), HR zones, and `EFFICIENCY_FACTOR`.

## Root Cause & Forensics
### Independent Peer Review & Verification (`Rules 69 & 70`, Live Codebase Audit @ `41a1764f`)
1. **Root Cause 1A — Unconditional Verity Sense `startPpiStreaming()` During Active Workouts (`PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt:993, 1002, 1007-1022`)**: `ATT-9176` launched `api.startPpiStreaming(sessionId)` unconditionally on Verity Sense across all 3 readiness hooks — including during active `TrackingMode.TRACKING`/`PAUSED` workouts alongside `ACC (52 Hz) + GYRO (52 Hz) + HR` (and offline HR arming). Per Polar BLE SDK docs, optical PPI mode is stationary-only and motion-intolerant: during running, Verity Sense flags ~100% of PPI beats with `blockerBit = true` (zero usable workout HRV data, since `ATT-9176` already excluded optical PPI from `HRV_DFA_A1` and `WorkoutRr.db`) while its optical HR pipeline stalls/freezes at pre-run standing HR (`79 bpm`) across BLE reconnects.
2. **Root Cause 1B — Infinite Last-Value Latching & One-Shot Silent Watchdog (`PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt:1095-1156`, `MySensor.java:80-103`, `BleHrmSilentConnectionWatchdog.kt:68-73`)**:
   - `MySensor.mValue` latches its last `newValue(...)` forever unless cleared with `newValue(null)`. In `processPpiData()` (`line 1147`), when all subsequent beats are `ARTIFACT_GAP` (`100%` artifact rate), `mRrIntervalSensor` stays permanently latched at the single pre-run clean beat (`659 ms` for all `919 s` until crash).
   - In `processHrData()` (`line 1097-1102`), `silentHrWatchdog.onHrPacketReceived()` disarms permanently on ANY non-empty `data.samples` (even `sample.hr <= 0` or lost skin contact!), `if (sample.hr > 0)` silently drops `0 bpm` without clearing `mHeartRateSensor`, and there is ZERO mid-session staleness (`> 15 s`), zero/no-contact (`> 10 s`), or constant-value flatline (`>= 60 s` identical BPM) monitor/recovery! `BTLEHeartRateDevice.java:389-396` similarly disarms on `heartRate == 0` and lacks stale clearing.
3. **Root Cause 2 — First-Connected-Wins Primary `SensorType.HR` Selection (`MySensorManager.java:142-168, 379, 528-561`)**: `MySensorManager` defines priority lists for Speed, Cadence, Altitude, Location, and HRV — but **NONE for `SensorType.HR`**. Both Polar H10 (ECG) and Polar Verity Sense (optical PPG) register under identical `DeviceType.HRM`; `getFirstSensor(SensorType.HR, DeviceType.HRM)` returns `sensorList.peekFirst()` permanently. Unlike `DeviceManager.java:525-547` (`ECG (0) > ANT+ (1) > PPG (2)` for RR beats), primary `HR` ignores ECG vs. optical modality and ignores sensor health (stale/null/flatlined/`>= 95%` optical artifact rate), locking onto Verity Sense whenever it connects first.
4. **Monolith LoC Ceilings & Extraction Strategy (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt`)**:
   - `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt`: `1,492 / 1,500` LoC (**8 LoC headroom** — extract pure-JVM `PolarHrStreamHealthMonitor.kt` + move helper wiring to `PolarPmdStreamCoordinator.kt` [`497/1,500`] to keep `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt <= 1,495` LoC with unchanged public JVM signatures).
   - `MySensorManager.java`: `700 / 1,500` LoC (**800 LoC headroom**) — delegate `SensorType.HR` arbitration to new pure-JVM `PrimaryHrSensorArbitrator.kt` (`~140` LoC) + `HrSensorHealthProvider` interface.
   - **Untouched Monoliths (`0` edits)**: `DeviceManager.java` (`1,707/1,708`), `TrackerService.java` (`3,551/3,559`), `DevicesDatabaseManager.java` (`1,669/1,671`), `PolarVeritySenseOfflineController.kt` (`1,483/1,500`).

- **In Scope**: (1) Pure-JVM `PolarHrStreamHealthMonitor.kt` tracking valid HR freshness (`15 s` stale clear to `null`), zero/no-contact clearing (`10 s`), RR/PPI artifact-only staleness clearing (`10 s`), constant-value HR flatline detection (`>= 60 s` identical BPM -> degraded flag + cooldown-bounded Verity PPI stop + HR slot re-subscribe), and triggering `MySensorManager.reevaluatePrimaryHrSensor()`; (2) Gate Verity Sense `StreamSlot.PPI` strictly to non-tracking resting modes (`READY`/`WAITING_FOR_START`) via `TrainingApplication.TRACKING_STATE_CHANGED` + `startPpiCollection` check (stopping `PmdMeasurementType.PPI` when entering `TRACKING`/`PAUSED`, resuming on `READY`); (3) Pure-JVM `PrimaryHrSensorArbitrator.kt` + `HrSensorHealthProvider` wired into `MySensorManager.java` (`getBestSensor(SensorType.HR)` & `reevaluatePrimaryHrSensor()`) enforcing Health Tier (`HEALTHY > DEGRADED > STALE_OR_NULL`) then Modality Tier (`ECG (0) > ANT+ (1) > Optical PPG (2) > Other (3)`), plus `heartRate > 0` watchdog/health guard in `BTLEHeartRateDevice.java`; (4) Update `docs/architecture/detailed_design/my_btle_device.md` (`REQ-PRO-114`).
