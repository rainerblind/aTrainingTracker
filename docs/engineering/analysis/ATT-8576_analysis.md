# Stage 1 Analysis: ATT-8576 - att_fused Initial GNSS Jump, Low-Speed Slope Extremes & Standstill Freeze

**Ticket**: `ATT-8576` (Recommended `2 SP`, Epic `ATT-166`) | **Branch**: `feature/ATT-8576` | **Date**: 2026-11-17

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
* During Pixel 10 ride `Zum Bäcker #56` (`2026-11-17_055721`, `workoutID=2182`, `5.0.0-dev-c4795aee`), `att_fused` (`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` + `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused`) exhibited three coupled artifacts:
  1. **46.3 km/h Start Spike (`t=7 s`)**: With `SPEED_mps (GPS) = 0.00 m/s` and GNSS accuracy tightening `25.1 m -> 16.0 m`, `att_fused` jumped `25.2 m` in `1 s` (`DISTANCE_m`: `3.0 -> 28.2 m`), emitting `SPEED_mps_att_fused = 12.87 m/s` (`46.3 km/h`), dropping altitude `-4.37 m`, hitting `-3000 m/h` VAM clamp, and logging `-31%` slope.
  2. **Frozen Slope at Stop (`t=200–213 s`)**: During a `13 s` bakery stop (`speed = 0.00`), `SLOPE` stayed frozen at `-21%`.
  3. **+29% Low-Speed Slope & Ascent Inflation (`t=219–222 s`)**: Rolling away at `0.82–0.99 m/s` over flat `442 m` DEM, `SLOPE` spiked to `+29%` (`+1040 m/h` VAM) and cumulative `ASCENT` reached `26.00 m` (vs `6.13 m` on `VerticalSpeedAndSlopeDevice`).

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
* **Cause 1 — Cold GNSS Anchor, Velocity Leak & Accuracy Masking (`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt`, `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java`)**:
  * `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.onLocationChanged()` (`:238-241`) anchors `refLat/refLon` on the very first fix even when `acc = 25.1 m > MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS` (`20.0 m`, `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:62, 304`), and still mutates `location` and invokes `onNewLocation()` (`:309-318`) despite rejected GNSS updates.
  * `ekf.updateGnssPosition()` (`:250`, `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:298-361`) runs **before** `ekf.updateSpeed(0.0, speedVar)` (`:253-260`, weak `speedVar = (acc*0.25)^2 = 16.0`), has no Mahalanobis innovation gate or ZUPT guard, and leaks position jumps into `x[2]` (`speedMps`) via `p[0][2]`/`p[1][2]` cross-covariance built during 50-Hz `predict()` (`:185-192`).
  * `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:316` overwrites `location.accuracy = sqrt(ekf.positionVariance)` (`~5–7 m` instead of `16.0–25.1 m`), shrinking `escapeRadius = max(3.0, 1.5 * accuracy)` in `SpeedAndLocationDevice.onNewLocation()` (`:586, 620`). Thus a `25.2 m` fix-tightening jump unpins the stationary anchor (`:620-622`), sets `delta_distance = 25.2 m` (`:632`), triggers `REQ-TRK-056.2` geometric fallback `calcSpeed = 25.2 m/s` (`:637, 646-648`), averages `mSpeed = (mSpeed + 25.2)/2 = 12.87 m/s` (`46.3 km/h`, `:743`), and feeds `12.87 m/s` into `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.getCurrentSpeed()` (`:351-356`), driving `predict(1.0, 12.87)` to drop altitude `-4.37 m`!
* **Cause 2 — Standstill Slope Freeze (`AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt:386-395`, `KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt:155-161, 215-228`)**:
  * `publishAltitudeAndMetrics()` skips `mSlopeSensor?.newValue(...)` whenever `speedMps < minSpeed` (`DEFAULT_MIN_SPEED_MPS = 0.5`), and `KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf` never decays `slope` during standstill (`predict()` returns early, `updateBarometer(..., isStandstill=true)` leaves `slope` untouched). Both Cockpit (`-21%`) and `ekf.slope` stay frozen across stops (`SensorFilterDefaults.kt:65` 5s moving average also retains the stale value).
* **Cause 3 — Unbounded Low-Speed Slope Coupling (`KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt:59, 146-185, 230-272`)**:
  * `standstillSpeedThresholdMps = 0.3 m/s` (`:59`) unlocks full slope updates at `0.82 m/s` (`deltaDist = 0.82 m`), while 5-Hz `handlePressureMeasurement()` (`AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt:264`) updates `slope` (`k1`) between 1-Hz `predict()` steps without low-speed damping or physical slope bounds. Tiny `~0.24 m` baro noise over `0.82 m` differentiates to `+29%`, which `predict()` (`altitude += slope * deltaDist`, `:164`) integrates across `2.0 m` hysteresis (`:371-383`) into `26.00 m` bogus `ASCENT`.
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification (Live `d7dec447`)**:
  * **Base-Class Invariant Check (`SpeedAndLocationDevice.java:620-648`)**: `SpeedAndLocationDeviceStationaryJitterTest.kt:342-437` (`REQ-TRK-056.1`/`.2`) mandates geometric speed fallback when `rawSpeed <= 0.1` and displacement exceeds `escapeRadius`; therefore `ATT-8576` MUST fix `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` / `UnicycleOdometryEkf` (valid-fix origin anchoring, accuracy preservation `>= acc`, ZUPT/zero-speed & Mahalanobis gating) rather than breaking `SpeedAndLocationDevice` fallback.
  * **Sibling & Downstream Audit**: `VerticalSpeedAndSlopeDevice.java:83, 234-241` enforces `VAM_LOW_SPEED_DRIFT_THRESHOLD_MPS = 1.2` for low-speed drift; `TrackingTabsScreen.kt:384` already zeroes `SLOPE` below `slopeMinSpeedMps` for auto-brightness. `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.kt:248` / `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt:75` are post-workout offline paths (out of scope).
  * **Existing Test Constraints**: `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFusedTest.kt:142-181` uses `3.0 m -> 2.5 m` fixes with `50 m` displacement and `5.0 m/s` speed; `KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkfTest.kt:73-88` asserts `ekf.slope == 0.0` at standstill; `ATT-8575` (`REQ-FIL-022`, `:204-343`) tests 3-state baro/ref anchoring and `p22 >= p00` standstill preservation.

## 3. Scope Grounding & Non-Goals (`ATT-1250`)
* **In-Scope**: (1) `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` & `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt`: defer/re-anchor `refLat/refLon` until first valid GNSS fix (`acc <= MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS`), gate/ignore degraded initial fixes, preserve GNSS uncertainty (`location.accuracy >= acc`), order speed/ZUPT before GNSS position update, and add Mahalanobis/zero-speed jump gating; (2) `KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt` & `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt`: decay/reset `slope` to `0%` on sustained standstill (`speedMps < minSpeed`), add low-speed slope damping (`0.3..1.2 m/s`) and physical grade bounds to stop `+29%` spikes and `ASCENT` inflation; (3) Unit tests in all 4 `*Test.kt` suites.
* **Out-of-Scope**: Modifying `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java` `REQ-TRK-056` fallback, `VerticalSpeedAndSlopeDevice.java`, `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.kt`, `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt`, UI/strings, DB schema, or `ANT-Android-SDKs`.
