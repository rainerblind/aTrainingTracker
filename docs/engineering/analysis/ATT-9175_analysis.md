## 1. Problem Statement & Observed Failure Modes
On physical Pixel 10 (`66020DLCR002FL`) with Polar Verity Sense (`24825632`, MAC ending `82:56`), tapping *"Jetzt synchronisieren"* on `PolarPendingRetrySyncSection` failed with `Polar Verity Sense connection timed out after 45000 ms for 24825632` despite `BluetoothGatt.onConnectionState(status=0, connected=true)` -> `onConfigureMTU(232)` -> `requestConnectionPriority(1)` -> `discoverServices()` succeeding within ~500 ms. Every `5,000 ms`, `PolarOnlineImuBridge` logged `Search timed out for Polar sensor 24825632 after 5000 ms` and executed `BluetoothGatt.disconnect() + refresh() + close()`.

## 2. Independent Peer Review & Verification (`Rules 69 & 70`)
Audited against live `f7ed7d94` and cached `polar-ble-sdk:8.6.0` (`BDBleApiImpl$startDevicesStateMonitor$1$1` bytecode):
1. **Verified — `withGattYieldAndPolarApi()` bypasses `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge`**: `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.kt:1079-1124` pauses only `deviceManager?.getBtleDeviceByAddress(macAddress)` (`MyBTLEDevice`), which returns `null` for `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` (`extends MyRemoteDevice`). Thus `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge`'s `reconnectJob` (`line 445`) and `onSearchTimeout()` (`line 469-486`) keep calling `polarApi?.disconnectFromDevice(sessionId)` on the shared singleton (`REQ-SET-118.1`), killing the offline controller's in-flight link.
2. **New Blind Spot Found — Disconnected bridges are absent from `activeBridges`**: `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.activeBridges` (`line 120`) adds on `markBridgeConnected()` (`line 516`) and removes on `deviceDisconnected()` (`line 576`). During Aftermath retry sync, the bridge is disconnected and NOT in `activeBridges` (and `DeviceManager` may be `null` in pure-JVM tests). Yield state MUST therefore live in `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator` as a per-device alias-aware ref-counted lease + registered bridge pause/resume registry (registered in `init`/session start, unregistered in `shutDown()`).
3. **New Blind Spot Found — Interaction with `ATT-9155` `silentHrWatchdog`**: If the bridge IS connected when offline PFTP sync runs (`>= 12 s`), `onSilentHrWatchdogTimeout()` (`PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt:364-401`) lacks an offline-yield check (unlike `BTLEHeartRateDevice.java:264` `isGattPaused()`) and would escalate to `forceDisconnectDevice(..., clearRegistrations = false)` mid-backfill. Pausing must cancel/defer `silentHrWatchdogJob` under `silentHrWatchdogLock` without leaking a timer.
4. **Verified — `deviceConnecting` (`SESSION_OPENING`) ignored during 8-feature GATT handshake**: `BDBleApiImpl` fires `deviceConnecting()` on `SESSION_OPENING` (not `SESSION_OPEN_PARK`), and `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.multiplexerCallback` (`line 188-195`) already dispatches it, but `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.sdkCallback` (`line 526-691`) never overrides `deviceConnecting()`. Thus `searchTimeoutJob` (`DEFAULT_BTLE_SEARCH_PERIOD_SEC = 5 s`) fires mid-`discoverServices()` and invokes `disconnect() + refresh() + close()`, clearing Android's GATT cache and causing an infinite 5 s death loop.
5. **Verified — Zero edits needed in offline controllers or `DeviceManager`**: All Verity Sense (`PolarVeritySenseOfflineController.kt:484,540,595,756,794`) and H10 (`PolarH10OfflineController.kt:292,499,686`) offline operations already route through `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.withGattYieldAndPolarApi()`.

## 3. Root Cause Summary
1. **Unpaused `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` During Offline GATT Yield**: `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.withGattYieldAndPolarApi()` does not pause matching `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` instances (reconnect loop, search timeout teardown, and `12 s` silent-HR watchdog), causing concurrent `disconnectFromDevice()` teardowns against both Verity Sense and H10 offline PFTP sessions.
2. **Unextended 5 s Search Watchdog During Live `SESSION_OPENING` Handshake**: `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` ignores `deviceConnecting()`, aborting live `onConnectionState(connected=true)` -> `discoverServices()` -> 8-feature Polar SDK handshakes at `5 s` instead of granting a single bounded handshake grace window (`15 s`) while preserving `5 s` fast-timeout when no peripheral responds.
