# Stage 1 Analysis: ATT-8999 - Stop 1-Hz Heap/OOM Cascade on WorkoutRepository._allWorkouts During Live Tracking

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
* During a 93-min active workout (`2,196` historical workouts), swiping back over `Nur Karte` crashed `com.atrainingtracker.debug` with `OutOfMemoryError` (`512 MB` Java heap, `<1%` free after GC; `SharedLabelerJni` hit the final 40-byte allocation); post-restart heap hit `322.8 MB` in 18 min.
* **Expected**: Live cockpit telemetry flows strictly via `BANALServiceRepository`; `TrackerService` persists 1-Hz SQLite samples/extrema asynchronously via `recordLiveSample` without 1-Hz `WorkoutRepository` reloads, `_allWorkouts` cloning, or historical re-aggregations.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
* **3-Stage 1-Hz Heap Churn Cascade Verified Against Live Code**:
  1. **Per-Sensor List Clones (`TrackerService.java:2687, 2693, 2702, 2789` -> `WorkoutRepository.kt:3982-4020, 4274-4305`)**: On every active `sampleAndWriteToDb` tick (`:2588`), changed `MIN`/`MAX`/`AVG` sensors invoke `updateExtremaValueInMemory`, cloning the `2,196`-item `_allWorkouts` list (`currentList.map`) 6–12x/sec and emitting on `allWorkouts`.
  2. **1-Hz Full SQLite Reloads & `PolyUtil.decode` (`TrackerService.java:2855` & `:2620` -> `WorkoutRepository.kt:1882-1895, 4314-4347, 4457-4513`)**: `TrackerService` broadcasts `WORKOUT_UPDATED_INTENT` at 1 Hz; `workoutUpdateReceiver` (`:1887`, registered `:3004`) runs `reloadWorkoutData` -> `WorkoutDataMapper.fromCursor` (`WorkoutDataMapper.kt:79-315`: 40+ SQLite extrema/laps/cluster/location queries + `PolyUtil.decode(mapPolyline)` at `:102, 1152` + `decodeDoubles(encodedAltitudes)` at `:109, 1210`) and clones `_allWorkouts` via `addOrUpdateWorkout`.
  3. **1-Hz Downstream Historical Rebuilds**: `PeriodsRepository.kt:179-187` maps `allWorkouts` via `toPeriodProjectionKey()` (`:1195-1211`, tracking `!finished` `activeTimeSec`/`totalTimeSec`/`totalDistance`/`ascentMeters`/`polylineHash`), triggering `loadFromDatabase(forceIncremental = true)` (`:932-1050`: 4 DB queries + `precalculateGroups` 4-way grouping `:955-963` + `enrich` `:974-1145` allocating `PeriodSummary`/daily maps; `workoutIdToPathMap`/`workoutIdToAllPolylinesMap` stay `emptyMap()` per `REQ-PER-039.1` at `:1004-1005, 1131-1132`, and `calculateWorkoutBounds` `:912-930` has 0 callers). Activity-scoped `AthleteProfileViewModel` (`:395-405`, `ATrainingTrackerApp.kt:629`) re-queries cardio/load SQLite tables and `KnownLocationsViewModel` (`:161-189`, `ATrainingTrackerApp.kt:431`) re-runs $O(\text{locations} \times N)$ geofence counts on every emission.
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification (Blind Spots & Guardrails)**:
  * **Paused 1-Hz Broadcast & Edge Gap (`TrackerService.java:2603-2623`)**: `TrackerService.java:2620` ALSO sends `WORKOUT_UPDATED_INTENT` at 1 Hz while paused (`SCRUM-97`), asserted by `TrackerServiceStateMachineCharacterisationTest.kt:329-365`.
