## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
* When tracking without manual sport pre-selection (`BANALService.mHaveUserSelectedSportType == false`) and without paired sport-specific ANT+/BLE sensors (`DeviceManager.getSportType() == BSportType.UNKNOWN`, `GCData = TDS----AG--`), `BANALService.getBSportType()` (`:484-491`) remains `BSportType.UNKNOWN` for the entire session, preventing live sport-specific features (`STRIDES` registration, notification sport label, `BANALServiceRepository.bSportType`, auto-pause sport thresholds, pace/speed cues) from activating.
* At workout completion, `TrackerService.BanalFinalizationSnapshot.capture()` (`:658-668`) and `finalizeLiveSession()` (`:3384-3420`) invoke `EquipmentAndSportTypeDiscoveryManager.resolveSportType()` / `resolveIdentity()` / `getCandidateBSportTypes()` using session-wide `averageSpeed = mDistanceTotal_m / mTimeActive_s`. Unpaused shopping/bakery stops dilute cycling sessions (`Metzger, Bäcker, und Knittel`: `1.35 m/s` avg vs. `p90 = 6.34 m/s`, `416 s` coasting; `Norma #9`: `2.34 m/s` vs. `p90 = 6.66 m/s`, `343 s` coasting; `Zum Bäcker #57`: `3.19 m/s` vs. `p90 = 6.65 m/s`, `46 s` coasting) into walking/running speed brackets (`SportTypeDatabaseManager.java:835-883`, `TrainingApplication.java:242-245`), whereas true running (`Kurze Runde #52`) has `0 s` coasting and continuous step-coherent footstrikes ($L_{\text{step}} = v / f_{\text{step}} \in [0.60, 1.75]\text{ m}$).

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
* **Existing Pipeline**:
  * `BANALService.java` (`980` LoC) resolves `getBSportType()` (`:484-491`) and `getActiveBSportType()` (`:329-340`) strictly from Tier 1 (`mHaveUserSelectedSportType -> mUserSelectedBSportType`) and Tier 2 (`DeviceManager.getSportType()`), with no Tier 3 kinematic inference slot or moving-speed summary.
  * `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` (`1,371` LoC) already runs 50-Hz `TYPE_ACCELEROMETER`, optional `TYPE_GYROSCOPE`/`TYPE_ROTATION_VECTOR`/`TYPE_PRESSURE`/gated `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR`, `PhoneCarriageClassifier` (`:108`), and `SmartphoneImuCadenceEstimator` (`:116`) backed by `ImuCadenceDsp.kt`.
  * `EquipmentAndSportTypeDiscoveryManager.kt` (`342` LoC) resolves `SportType` / `Equipment` / `candidateBSportTypes` solely from `(activeDeviceIds, bSportType, averageSpeed)` via `SportTypeDatabaseManager.getSportTypesIdList(bSportType, avgSpd)` (`:132-162`).
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification (5 Live-Code Blind Spots Found)**:
  1. **Circular Feedback Loop (`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:801-802,1254-1276`, `PhoneCarriageClassifier.kt:398-399`)**: `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.updateCarriageClassification()` feeds `BANALService.getBSportType()` into `carriageClassifier.setSportContext(hint)` (`+2.5` score prior). If `BANALService.getBSportType()` returns Tier 3 kinematic inference, feeding it back into `PhoneCarriageClassifier` creates a self-reinforcing latch that blocks `RUN -> BIKE` recovery (e.g., jogging to a bike then riding). **Mitigation**: Expose `BANALService.getAuthoritativeBSportType()` (Tier 1 + Tier 2 only, `UNKNOWN` when sensorless/unselected) for `PhoneCarriageClassifier.setSportContext()`, while `getBSportType()` / `getActiveBSportType()` returns Tier 1 > Tier 2 > Tier 3 for live UX and `STRIDES` gating.
  2. **Budget & Permission-Denied Degradation Gap (`PhoneCarriageClassifier.kt:347-415`)**: `PhoneCarriageClassifier` does NOT ingest `TYPE_ACCELEROMETER`. On devices lacking `TYPE_GYROSCOPE`, `TYPE_GAME_ROTATION_VECTOR`, and `TYPE_PRESSURE` with `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` denied (`TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR` unregistered), `PhoneCarriageClassifier` falls back to `v >= 5.5 m/s -> BIKE_HANDLEBAR` and `v in 2.2..4.6 -> RUN_WAIST_HIP`, misclassifying fast `5.6 m/s` interval runners as `BIKE` and slow `2.0–2.5 m/s` steep uphill MTB climbs as `RUN`. **Mitigation**: The new `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator` (`banalservice/odometry/`) MUST ingest permission-free 50-Hz `TYPE_ACCELEROMETER` (`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:329-334`) through `ImuCadenceDsp` (`UniformGridResampler` + `BiquadBandPass` `2.4–3.6 Hz` vertical/magnitude impact periodicity) to detect running footstrikes and compute $L_{\text{step}} = v / f_{\text{step}} \in [0.60, 1.75]\text{ m}$ even when gyro, barometer, rotation vector, and `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR` are all absent.
  3. **Strict Monolith & God-Class LoC Ceilings (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt:116-134`)**: `TrackerService.java` is `3,556 / 3,559` LoC (3 lines headroom -> target `<= 3,556`, `0` net growth), `DeviceManager.java` is `1,707 / 1,708` LoC (`0` edits), and `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` is `1,371` LoC against `MAX_MONOLITH_LOC_THRESHOLD = 1,500` (only `129` lines below the net-new God-class tripwire!). **Mitigation**: Place all kinematic discrimination, coasting detection, permission-free footstrike DSP, and stop-filtered moving-speed quantile tracking in a cohesive pure-Kotlin class `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator.kt` under `banalservice/odometry/` (keeping `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt <= 1,420` LoC, `BANALService.java <= 1,035` LoC, `TrackerService.java <= 3,556` LoC, `DeviceManager.java` untouched).
  4. **Finalization Speed Dilution vs. `SPEED_mps` Extrema Invariant & Explicit `WALK`/`OTHER` Selection (`TrackerService.java:643-684,2259-2277,2465-2472,3247-3249,3383-3420`)**: `BanalFinalizationSnapshot.capture()` and `finalizeLiveSession()` currently pass session-wide `effectiveAvgSpeed` into `resolveSportType()`, `resolveIdentity()`, and `getCandidateBSportTypes()`. Passing stop-filtered moving speed (`v > 0.8 m/s` moving mean/percentile summary) into `EquipmentAndSportTypeDiscoveryManager` resolves multi-stop errand rides cleanly to `BSportType.BIKE` (`Endurance` / linked bike gear) while **strictly preserving** `effectiveAvgSpeed = mDistanceTotal_m / mTimeActive_s` for `WorkoutSummaries.SPEED_AVERAGE_mps` (`:2790`) and `SensorType.SPEED_mps` `ExtremaType.AVG` (`:3247-3249`, `SCRUM-108` / `REQ-TRK-030.3`). Furthermore, Tier 1 precedence MUST check `mHaveUserSelectedSportType` (not `mUserSelectedBSportType != UNKNOWN`), because `WALK` and `OTHER` map to `BSportType.UNKNOWN` (`SportTypeDatabaseManager.java:796,800`).
  5. **Thread Safety, Zero Main-Thread SQLite, Lifecycle Reset & `RUN <-> BIKE` Transitions (`BANALService.java:454-465,749-751`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:1279-1319`)**: High-rate IMU callbacks (50 Hz) and `BANALService.getBSportType()` MUST remain lock-free/volatile with zero SQLite queries. `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator` must use bounded evidence with hysteresis (activating within `< 30 s` of active running/cycling while permitting `RUN -> BIKE` or `BIKE -> RUN` recovery on sustained counter-evidence), reset cleanly on `onAccumulatorsReset()` (`RESET_ACCUMULATORS_INTENT`) and `BANALService.onDestroy()`, and NEVER fire `SPORT_TYPE_CHANGED_BY_USER_INTENT` (which implies manual user override and triggers `startSearchWhenUserChangesSport()`). When inferred `BSportType` updates on `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused`, trigger `handleSportTypeChanged()` internally so `BSportType.RUN` activates `STRIDES` automatically.

## 3. Scope Grounding & Non-Goals (`3 SP` Verified — No Split Needed)
* **In-Scope Goals**:
  * Pure-Kotlin `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator` (`banalservice/odometry/`) combining: (1) coasting/freewheeling ($v > 3.0\text{ m/s}$ for $\ge 3\text{ s}$ with near-zero step rate & low cyclic oscillation $\to$ `BIKE`), (2) `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR` (`2.0–3.6 Hz`) + permission-free 50-Hz `TYPE_ACCELEROMETER` (`2.4–3.6 Hz` impact periodicity via `ImuCadenceDsp`) with step-length coherence $L_{\text{step}} = v / f_{\text{step}} \in [0.60, 1.75]\text{ m}$ ($\to$ `RUN`) vs. $f_{\text{step}} \approx 0$ or apparent cycle distance $> 3.5\text{ m}$ at $v > 5.0\text{ m/s}$ ($\to$ `BIKE`), (3) `PhoneCarriageMode` voting (`BIKE_*` vs. `RUN_*`), and (4) stop-filtered moving speed ($v > 0.8\text{ m/s}$) distribution (`movingMeanMps`, `p50Mps`, `p90Mps`, moving sample count) with kinematic precedence over speed thresholds for fast `5.6 m/s` runners and slow `2.0 m/s` uphill MTB cyclists.
  * `BANALService` strict 4-tier precedence (`mHaveUserSelectedSportType` > `DeviceManager.getSportType()` > high-confidence kinematic `BSportType` > speed-based fallback), `getAuthoritativeBSportType()`, volatile inferred sport + moving-speed summary exposure via `BANALServiceComm`, and reset on `RESET_ACCUMULATORS_INTENT`/`onDestroy()`.
  * `EquipmentAndSportTypeDiscoveryManager` moving-speed-aware overloads (`resolveSportType`, `resolveIdentity`, `getCandidateBSportTypes`) + minimal zero-bloat wiring in `TrackerService` (`BanalFinalizationSnapshot` / `finalizeLiveSession`, plus `LiveWorkoutSession` 1-Hz moving-speed fallback for GPS-only sessions without `ATT_FUSED`).
