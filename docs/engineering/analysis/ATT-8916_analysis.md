## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
`ATT-8890` (`REQ-FIL-028`) persists 1-Hz `SensorType.ACTIVITY_AND_CARRIAGE_MODE` codes (`UNKNOWN(-1)`, `STATIONARY(0)`, `WALKING_TRANSITION(1)`, `BIKE_HANDLEBAR(2)`, `BIKE_JERSEY_BACK(3)`, `BIKE_FRONT_POCKET(4)`, `RUN_WAIST_HIP(5)`, `RUN_BICEP_ARM(6)`, `RUN_HANDHELD(7)`) into `WorkoutSamples.db`, yet Workout Details currently surfaces none of this phase history. Users cannot inspect multi-modal transitions (e.g., `Bike Trouser Pocket -> Walking / Transition -> Stationary -> Walking / Transition -> Bike Trouser Pocket`) or per-mode duration and distance totals post-workout.

* **Independent Peer Review & Verification** (live audit on `sprint/2026-11-18_02_Spät` @ `fee3e805`):
  * *Non-Existent Ticket Symbols*: Ticket cites `WorkoutDetailsViewModel.kt` and `WorkoutDetailsScreen.kt`, which do not exist. Authoritative owners are `TrackOnMapAftermathViewModel.kt` -> `TrackOnMapScreen.kt` (5 callers: `WorkoutSummariesTabbedScreen.kt:313`, `WorkoutSummariesListFragment.kt:188`, `WorkoutClustersScreen.kt:163`, `WorkoutClusterHeatmapScreen.kt:403`, `PeriodMapScreen.kt:249`) -> `WorkoutDetailTabbedContent.kt:873-935`.
  * *Decimation & Trackless Blind Spot*: `TrackOnMapAftermathViewModel.kt:207` simplifies map tracks to `<= 800` points and `WorkoutRepository.kt:2149` decimates trackless points to `<= 800`. Timeline coalescing MUST run on the full-resolution 1-Hz cursor inside `loadWorkoutDetailTelemetry` (also guarding `hasCarriageMode` at `L2472` and preserving `telemetry?.activityCarriageTimeline` in the `else` trackless branch at `L259-271`) so brief dismounts are never dropped and indoor/trackless sessions render cleanly.
  * *All-UNKNOWN / Warmup Blind Spot*: `ActivityType.java:227` adds `ACTIVITY_AND_CARRIAGE_MODE` to every new table even when `ATT_FUSED` is off, producing all-`NULL`/`UNKNOWN(-1)` rows, and `PhoneCarriageClassifier` emits `UNKNOWN(-1)` during `1.8s` warmup (`MIN_WARMUP_MS`). Pause gaps (`rawDt > 5s`) must clamp per-row `dt` (`<= 5s`, matching `ZoneDistributionCalculator.kt:32`) and `deltaDist >= 0.0`.

## 6. Architectural Strategy & High-Level Solution
1. **Pure-JVM Coalescer**: Accumulate `(timeSec, distanceM, rawModeCode)` during `WorkoutRepository.loadWorkoutDetailTelemetry`'s single cursor pass into contiguous `ActivityCarriageSegment` spans (preserving transition order, clamping pause gaps `<= 5s`, skipping warmup `UNKNOWN`) plus per-mode `ActivityCarriageModeSummary` totals.
2. **State & Host Wiring**: Add default-null `activityCarriageTimeline: ActivityCarriageTimeline? = null` to `WorkoutDetailTelemetry`, `AftermathMapUIState`, `TrackOnMapScreen`, and `WorkoutDetailTabbedContent`, forwarding across all 5 callers.
3. **Compose Ribbon & Breakdown UI**: Add `ActivityCarriageTimelineCard` under `WorkoutDetailSectionType.DETAILS` (rendering only when `timeline != null && timeline.segments.isNotEmpty()`) featuring a `ZoneDistributionStackedBar`-styled proportional timeline bar (by duration/distance), summary mode chips/rows (icon, `PhoneCarriageMode.labelResId`, duration, distance, share %), and expandable chronological phase breakdown.
