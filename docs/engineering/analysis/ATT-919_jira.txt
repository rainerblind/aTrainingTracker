h1. Feature ATT-919: Known Start Locations Management, Interactive Map Geofences & Manual Altitude Correction UI

h2. 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context
*aTrainingTracker* maintains geographical start locations in {{StartLocation2Altitude.db}} (Schema V5) to calibrate hardware barometric altimeters upon workout start. With *ATT-1278* (Internet Digital Elevation Model ground truth) and *ATT-1366* (elimination of running mean altitude drift), the data layer provides thread-safe, robust reference elevations.

However, there was zero user interface for this database:
# *Zero Visibility*: Athletes could not view stored locations, reference altitudes, or workout start frequencies.
# *No Manual Calibration*: Athletes knowing their exact cadastral or surveyed ground truth could not manually enter it.
# *No Location Naming*: Discovered locations were labeled with technical placeholders (e.g. "Internet DEM start") and could not be named or edited.
# *No Spatial Context*: Athletes could not inspect the 200m spatial geofence circles on an interactive map.

h2. 2. Architecture & Solution Design
* *Navigation Drawer*: Entry under {{drawer__maps}}: {{R.id.drawer_start_locations}} -> {{NavRoutes.START_LOCATIONS}} ("Start Locations" / "Startorte").
* *Dual-Perspective Screen (KnownLocationsScreen.kt)*:
** *List Tab*: High-density Material 3 cards rendering Location Name, Altitude (m/ft), Provenance Source badge ({{INTERNET_DEM}}, {{MANUAL_USER}}, {{AUTO_LEARNED}}, {{GPS_FALLBACK}}, {{LEGACY_RAW}}), Hit Count badge, coordinates (5 decimals), and Action overflow menu (Edit, Center on Map, Delete).
** *Map Tab*: Google Map rendering pin markers and 200m circular geofence overlays with viewport bounds culling ({{visibleRegion.latLngBounds}}).
* *Meaningful Location Naming & Editing*:
** *Reverse Geocoding on Discovery*: Queries Android {{Geocoder}} in the background to assign a human-readable place name (locality, sublocality, thoroughfare).
** *Legacy Healing Enrichment*: Unnamed or placeholder records are enriched with place names during healing.
** *Custom Name Protection*: Names edited by the athlete are strictly preserved in SQLite ({{KnownLocationsDbHelper.NAME}}).
* *Seamless Altitude Protection*:
** In {{EditKnownLocationDialog.kt}}, manual altitude edits automatically persist with {{source = MANUAL_USER}} and {{is_locked = 1}}, protecting calibrations without requiring manual lock management.
** "Fetch from Internet (DEM)" queries online elevation on demand and persists {{source = INTERNET_DEM}} with {{is_locked = 0}}.
* *Concurrency & Invariants*:
** Single-threaded SQLite dispatcher ({{KnownLocationsDB-Thread}}) prevents coroutine deadlocks.
** Repeat starts at locked/manual locations increment {{hitCount}} while preserving reference altitudes ({{REQ-DAT-007}}).
** Complete 9-language localization parity (EN, DE, ES, FR, IT, JA, NL, PL, PT).
