# Stage 1 Analysis: ATT-9244 - Stop PebbleService When appMessages Unsupported & Close PebbleView.db Cleanly

**Ticket**: `ATT-9244` (`1 SP`, `Bug`, `severity-major`) | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-235` (*No Crashes & App Stability*) | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0`  
**Active Sprint**: `2026-11-20_03_Nacht` | **Branch**: `feature/ATT-9244` (base `a88a0672`) | **Author**: AI Agent 1 (`Flash`) | **Date**: 2026-11-21

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
* On Pixel 10 ride `Zum Bäcker #59`, `PebbleService` launched at workout start, failed `com.getpebble.android.provider` lookup (`WTF: appMessages are not supported`), yet stayed alive as `START_STICKY` running the 1-Hz `mUpdatePebbleReceiver` loop for the entire 10-minute ride and leaking 4 `SQLiteConnection` objects on `PebbleView.db` at shutdown.
* **Expected**: `PebbleService` stops immediately via `stopSelf()` (`START_NOT_STICKY`) without registering 1-Hz receivers, binding `BANALService`, or opening `PebbleView.db` when `PebbleKit.areAppMessagesSupported(this)` is `false`; when Pebble IS supported, `PebbleView.db` uses the singleton `PebbleDatabaseManager` and closes cleanly on destroy with zero `SQLiteConnectionPool` leak warnings.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
* **4 Verified Failure Mechanisms on `sprint/2026-11-20_03_Nacht`**:
  1. **Missing `stopSelf()` & Early Receiver/Service Bind (`PebbleService.java:350-386, 425-432`)**: `onCreate()` unconditionally binds `BANALService` (`mBanalConnection`) and registers 8 `BroadcastReceiver`s (`mUpdatePebbleReceiver`, `mConfigurePebbleWatchAppReceiver`, `mPebbleConnectedReceiver`, pause/resume/lap/search). `onStartCommand()` returns `START_STICKY` and `startPebbleWatchApp()` logs `// TODO: close this service` (`L431`) without calling `stopSelf()`.
  2. **Android `onCreate -> onStartCommand` Lifecycle Trap**: Even if `onCreate()` calls `stopSelf()`, Android still executes `onStartCommand()` before `onDestroy()`. Without checking `areAppMessagesSupported` at the top of both `onCreate()` and `onStartCommand()`, `onStartCommand()` still executes `PebbleDatabaseManager.getInstance(this).getFirstViewId(...)` (`L380`) and `startPebbleWatchApp()` (`L381`).
  3. **Unguarded `unbindService` in `onDestroy()` (`PebbleService.java:399`)**: `onDestroy()` calls `unbindService(mBanalConnection)` unconditionally. Skipping `bindService()` on early exit without an `mIsBanalBound` guard would crash `onDestroy()` with `IllegalArgumentException: Service not registered`. (`LifecycleUtils.safeUnregisterReceiver` already guards unregistered receivers; gating on `mBaseReceiversRegistered` avoids 8 noisy `Log.w` warnings.)
  4. **Unclosed Per-Call `PebbleDbHelper` & Unclosed Singleton (`PebbleService.java:313`, `PebbleDatabaseManager.java:51-78`)**: `updateSensorTypeList()` (`L313`) opens `(new PebbleDbHelper(this)).getReadableDatabase()` on every `startPebbleWatchApp()`/`configurePebbleWatchApp()` call and never closes the `PebbleDbHelper`/`SQLiteDatabase`, leaking a `SQLiteConnectionPool` connection per call—even though `PebbleDatabaseManager.getSensorTypeList(long)` (`L206-231`) already exists. Furthermore, `PebbleDatabaseManager` lacks a `close()` method and `isOpen()` re-open guard (`TrackingViewsDatabaseManager.getDatabase()` pattern).
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification**:
  * Verified `PebbleKit.areAppMessagesSupported(Context)` (`pebblekit:4.0.1`) synchronously queries `com.getpebble.android.provider` inside `try/finally` closing its own `Cursor`, returning `false` when Pebble is absent.
  * Verified `TrainingApplication.java` (`2,135` LoC) sits at its exact `MAX_MONOLITH_FILE_LOC_BASELINE` (`2135` in `ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt:123`)—leaving `TrainingApplication.startPebbleWatchapp()` (`L369-385`) untouched preserves the `2135` LoC ceiling and guarantees `PebbleService` self-guards regardless of start caller.
  * Confirmed `PebbleService` has zero existing unit tests in `app/src/test/`; `TryWithResourcesCursorAuditTest` scans `smartwatch/pebble/`, and `LifecycleServiceReceiverSafetyAuditTest` enforces `LifecycleUtils.safeUnregisterReceiver`.

## 3. User Scope Grounding (ATT-1250) & Complexity Audit
* **In-Scope Goals (`1 SP` Verified)**: (1) Immediate `stopSelf()` + `START_NOT_STICKY` in `PebbleService` when `areAppMessagesSupported == false` without binding `BANALService`, registering receivers, or opening `PebbleView.db`; (2) `mIsBanalBound` / `mBaseReceiversRegistered` / `mReceiversRegistered` teardown guards in `onDestroy()` / `stopPebbleWatchApp()`; (3) Route `updateSensorTypeList()` through singleton `PebbleDatabaseManager.getSensorTypeList(mViewId)`, add `isOpen()` reopen safety + synchronized `close()` to `PebbleDatabaseManager`, and close cleanly on `PebbleService.onDestroy()`; (4) Robolectric lifecycle + SQLite leak-free tests.
* **Out-of-Scope Non-Goals**: `PebbleServiceBuildIn.java` (parallel `// TODO: close this service`, no `PebbleView.db` access), `DatabaseSchemaRegistry.kt:42` (`"Pebble.db"` naming discrepancy), `DatabaseConnectionManager.kt` additions, and deleting legacy Pebble support (`ATT-40` revival).

## 6. Architectural Strategy & High-Level Solution
* Add package-private/testable `isAppMessagesSupported()` helper in `PebbleService` delegating to `PebbleKit.areAppMessagesSupported(this)`. Check early in `onCreate()` (skip `bindService` + 8 receivers, call `stopSelf()`) and `onStartCommand()` (skip `PebbleDatabaseManager` + `startPebbleWatchApp()`, call `stopSelf(startId)`, return `START_NOT_STICKY`), plus early-return + `stopSelf()` inside `startPebbleWatchApp()`.
* Guard `onDestroy()` with `mIsBanalBound`, `mBaseReceiversRegistered`, and `mReceiversRegistered` (`stopPebbleWatchApp()` resets `mReceiversRegistered = false` and skips `closeAppOnPebble()` when never started); invoke `PebbleDatabaseManager.closeIfInitialized()` on destroy.
* Update `PebbleService.updateSensorTypeList()` to call `PebbleDatabaseManager.getInstance(this).getSensorTypeList(mViewId)` (eliminating raw `new PebbleDbHelper(this)`); add `synchronized` `isOpen()` reopen check in `getDatabase()` and `close()` / `closeIfInitialized()` in `PebbleDatabaseManager`.

## 7. System Invariants & Risk Assessment
* **Core Invariants**: (1) Supported Pebble (`areAppMessagesSupported == true`) behavior, receivers, and `START_STICKY` unchanged; (2) `ConfigPebbleViewsActivity`/`ConfigPebbleViewFragment` seamlessly reopen `PebbleView.db` after `PebbleService` closes; (3) `TrainingApplication.java` stays `<= 2,135` LoC; (4) Zero unshielded `startService`/`unregisterReceiver` or unclosed `Cursor`s.
* **Risk Rating**: **LOW** — strictly scoped 2-file Java change in `smartwatch/pebble/` with deterministic Robolectric verification.
