# Stage 1 Analysis: ATT-8726 — Prevent `RadarEdgeOverlay` Targets from Jumping Back to Bottom When Crossing 140m on V1 or ANT+

**Ticket**: `ATT-8726` (`1 SP`, `Bug`, `severity-major`, `tier-a`, Epic `ATT-1286`) | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0` | **Branch**: `feature/ATT-8726` | **Date**: 2026-11-24

## 1. Problem Statement & Forensic Root Cause Analysis
When a cycling radar streams over BLE V1 (`6a4e3203`, including unbonded BLE sessions and `tools/ble_radar_simulator.py` / `tools/replay_workout.py`) or ANT+ (`0x28`), `RadarTarget` entries carry `vehicleSizeCategory = VehicleSizeCategory.UNKNOWN_OR_LIGHT` and `lateralOffsetMeters = null`. When an approaching target spawns beyond `140.0 m` (e.g., Fast Car at `160.0 m`, Truck at `150.0 m`, Convoy at `160.0 m`), it travels smoothly upward along the `175.0 m` rail scale (`160/175 = 0.914 -> 140.2/175 = 0.801`), then **jumps backward to the bottom of the rail (`~1.0`)** the instant `rangeMeters <= 140.0f`.

- **Exact Defect Location**: `RadarOverlayMath.resolveRailMaxRange(radarState)` (`app/src/main/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/tracking/radar/RadarEdgeOverlay.kt:190-197`) is stateless across frames:
  - When `rangeMeters > 140.0f` is the sole predicate selecting `MAX_RADAR_RANGE_V2_METERS` (`175.0f`), crossing `<= 140.0f` snaps the returned range immediately back to `MAX_RADAR_RANGE_METERS` (`140.0f`).
  - Both production consumers — `RadarFullScreenThreatOverlay` (`RadarEdgeOverlay.kt:624`, feeding `RadarFullScreenVehicleMarker` and `RadarTopDownVehicleMarker`) and `RadarVerticalRail` (`RadarEdgeOverlay.kt:1000`, feeding `LEFT`/`RIGHT` edge dots) — call `resolveRailMaxRange(radarState)` directly on every frame, causing `normalizeRadarRange` to jump from `0.801` to `1.000` (`+19.9%` downward reversal).

## 2. Independent Peer Review & Verification
Auditing `sprint/2026-11-24_03_Nacht` confirms the ticket hypothesis and surfaces 4 critical design details:
1. **Exact Call-Site Coverage**: `grep` across `app/src/main/` confirms `resolveRailMaxRange` has strictly 2 call sites (`RadarEdgeOverlay.kt:624` and `RadarEdgeOverlay.kt:1000`); `RadarTopDownVehicleMarker.kt:152` receives `maxRangeMeters` as a parameter from `RadarFullScreenThreatOverlay`. Hoisting a single session latch into `RadarEdgeOverlay` covers `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `FULL_SCREEN` (`COMPACT_BADGE` & `TOP_DOWN_VEHICLES`), and mid-approach `edgeSide`/style switches from one source of truth.
2. **All-Clear & Disconnect Lifecycle**: `RadarFullScreenThreatOverlay` (`L390`) and `RadarVerticalRail`'s target block (`L998`) unmount when `hasActiveThreats == false`, whereas `RadarEdgeOverlay` stays mounted across `CONNECTED` (clear & active threats) and `DISCONNECTED_ERROR` (`TrackingTabsScreen.kt:1028-1066`). Hosting the latch inside `RadarEdgeOverlay` ensures immediate reset to `140.0f` on `targets.isEmpty()` (All Clear) OR `connectionState != RadarConnectionState.CONNECTED` (disconnect / error / reconnect). Both `BTLERadarDevice.java` and `ANTBikeRadarDevice.kt` emit `targets = emptyList()` strictly on All Clear or disconnect.
3. **Multi-Target & Mixed-V2 Bonus Protection**: When a closer target is already approaching at `140 m` scale and a second target enters at `> 140 m`, upward expansion `140 -> 175` occurs immediately; once at `175 m`, scale never shrinks back to `140 m` while any target remains active (also protecting a remaining `UNKNOWN_OR_LIGHT` target if a closer V2 `CAR`/`TRUCK` passes first).
4. **Compose Snapshot & Contract Compliance**: Mirroring `RadarThreatHysteresisSession` (`RadarEdgeOverlay.kt:377,504`), the latch MUST be a plain non-Compose holder inside `remember { ... }` (zero `MutableState` writes during composition per `coding_standards.md` §1.10 / Rule 66), while keeping `RadarOverlayMath.resolveRailMaxRange(radarState)` stateless and unchanged for `TST-TRK-073.1`.

## 3. Scope Grounding, Non-Goals & Complexity/Tier Audit
- **In Scope**: Pure `RadarOverlayMath.resolveLatchedRailMaxRange(previousMaxRangeMeters, radarState)`; private `RadarRangeLatchSession` hoisted in `RadarEdgeOverlay`; passing `maxRangeMeters` to `RadarFullScreenThreatOverlay` and `RadarVerticalRail`; non-invasive `RadarRailTarget_${target.targetId}` testTag in `RadarVerticalRail` for behavioral Compose Y-monotonicity assertions; unit + Compose UI tests in `RadarEdgeOverlayTest.kt`; `REQ-TRK-073.1` / `TST-TRK-073.1` refinement.
- **Out of Scope**: Modifying BLE/ANT+ radar packet decoders (`BTLERadarDevice`, `ANTBikeRadarDevice`), `RadarStateRepository`, `RadarTopDownVehicleMarker.kt` kinematics, rail widths/colors/badges (`REQ-TRK-073.2`, `REQ-TRK-089`), or localized strings (`0` string changes).
- **Sizing & Tier Check**: `1 SP` (canonical Fibonacci `<= 5 SP`, no split needed); `tier-a` retained per Jira label (`Rule 8.3`, no downgrade).

### Requirement Archaeology & Chesterton's Fence Audit
- **Original Requirement ID & Target**: `REQ-TRK-073` (`REQ-TRK-073.1` *Dynamic 175m Rail Scaling*) in `docs/requirements/req_tracking_cockpit_part2.md:1570-1607`, targeting `RadarEdgeOverlay.kt` (`RadarOverlayMath`, `RadarEdgeOverlay`, `RadarFullScreenThreatOverlay`, `RadarVerticalRail`) and `RadarEdgeOverlayTest.kt`.
- **Historical Origin & Commit Trace**: Introduced in `ATT-5096` (`f2c2e15e`: *Render vehicle size (car vs. truck) and lateral lane-change offsets in live radar overlay*) and extended to `FULL_SCREEN` in `ATT-7537` (`3206a504`), `ATT-8733` (`22853065`), `ATT-8958` (`5e26c348`), and `ATT-8959` (`1b62150e`).
- **Root Reason for Existing Formulation**: `ATT-5096` added `175.0 m` dynamic rail scaling so long-range V2 radars (RearVue 820) and targets beyond `140.0 m` would not clip at the bottom edge while preserving full vertical travel resolution (`140.0 m`) for standard legacy V1/ANT+ units. Because `REQ-TRK-073.1` did not specify monotonic per-approach range latching, `resolveRailMaxRange` was implemented statelessly per frame.
- **Preservation of Core Invariants**: Preserves stateless `RadarOverlayMath.resolveRailMaxRange(radarState)` (`140.0f` for standard V1 `<= 140 m`, `175.0f` for `> 140 m` or V2), `140.0 m` full-height resolution for normal V1/ANT+ approaches that never exceed `140 m` and after every All Clear / disconnect, 100% gesture pass-through (`REQ-TRK-062.3`), `LEFT`/`RIGHT` dot geometry (`REQ-TRK-073.2`), `FULL_SCREEN` marker layout & threat hysteresis (`REQ-TRK-089`, `REQ-TRK-098`), and zero localization/DB/decoder regressions.
