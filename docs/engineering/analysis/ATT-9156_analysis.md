## 1. Problem Statement & Field Evidence
* Pixel 10 easy run `Kurze Runde var 2 #53` (`2026-11-19_184953`, `_id = 2414`) exposed severe live DEM-induced elevation/slope corruption in `att_fused_altitude_slope` (`KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf` + `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused`):
  1. **2.7× Ascent/Descent Inflation & Extreme Slope Swings**: Live `att_fused_altitude_slope` reported **`167.1 m` ascent / `166.0 m` descent** and swung `SLOPE` between **`-35%` and `+34%`** (`VERTICAL_SPEED -2790..+2269 m/h`), whereas true loop gain is **`~58–64 m`** (ERTS canonical `63.75 m / 58.00 m`; cluster `1493` history `55–69 m`).
  2. **DEM Grid-Cell Step Blowup (`253` samples `|Δalt/dt| > 0.5 m/s`)**: `ALTITUDE (Topografische Höhe (DEM))` jumped **13–21 m in 1–2 s** across coarse DEM cells (e.g., `18:58:05–07`: `440 → 427 → 440 m` while barometer stayed flat `413.7–414.0 m`), pulling live `att_fused_altitude_slope` down `-1.73 / -1.52 / -1.38 m/s` and emitting `-28%` slope.
* **Complementary to `ATT-9063`**: `ATT-9063` (`REQ-FIL-033`) fixed DEM slope blowup in the offline post-workout ERTS smoother (`ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt`); this ticket hardens the **live EKF** (`KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt` + `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt`).

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Verification
* **Verified Root Causes on `sprint/2026-11-20_01_Früh` (`bf7eed5e`)**:
  1. **Over-Confident `DEFAULT_VAR_DEM = 4.0` (`±2 m` 1σ) (`KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt:58`, `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt:362, 416`)**: `PhoneCarriageMode.kt` scales `rBaroM2` to `1.5–2.0 m²` (walking/handheld/pocket) and `updateAdaptiveJitterVariance()` inflates `effectiveVarBaro` up to `25.0 m²`. Because `rDem = 4.0` is comparable to or smaller than `rBaro`, `k0 = p00 / (p00 + 4.0)` pulls live `altitude` aggressively toward noisy SRTM/DEM grid steps, rectifying DEM cell chatter across the `2.0 m` hysteresis deadband (`AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt:475-483`) into `167.1 m` false ascent.
  2. **Un-Decoupled DEM-to-Slope Gain & Non-Joseph Covariance Update (`KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt:459-479`)**: Unlike `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt:282` (`ATT-9063`, which sets `k1 = 0.0` whenever `hasBaroData || isStandstill` and applies 3×3 Joseph SPD covariance updates), live `updateReferenceAltitude()` still applies `k1Eff = (p10 / s) * gainScale` to `slope` even when `hasBarometerSample == true`, injecting every `13–21 m` DEM step directly into `SLOPE` (`±34%`) and `VERTICAL_SPEED`. Once `k1Eff` is zeroed/damped, simplified `(I - KH)P` (`:470-478`) can also lose SPD without Joseph-form covariance updates.
  3. **No Baro-vs-DEM Step-Discrepancy Gating or Variance Inflation (`KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf.kt:454-465, 493-502` & `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt:405-448`)**: `checkReferenceAltitudeFromSensors()` feeds raw DEM values directly into `updateReferenceAltitude()` without comparing `Δdem` against recent `Δbaro`. Even with `DEFAULT_VAR_DEM = 25.0 m²` (`1σ = 5 m`), linear `applyHuber()` (`huberK = 3.0`, `limit >= 15 m`) lets a `13 m` 1-second DEM jump pass **completely unclamped** and still shrinks `p00` via `(1 - k0) * p00`.
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification (Blind Spots, Mathematical Invariants & Call-Site Audit)**:
  * **Blind Spot 1 (`DEFAULT_VAR_GPS` vs `DEFAULT_VAR_DEM` Hierarchy & `testThreeStateGpsBeforeDem`)**: Raising `DEFAULT_VAR_DEM` from `4.0` to `25.0 m²` (`±5 m`) while leaving `DEFAULT_VAR_GPS = 16.0` (`±4 m`) inverts the physical accuracy hierarchy (`REQ-FIL-020.1`: DEM > GPS vertical) AND breaks `KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkfTest.testThreeStateGpsBeforeDem_recoversFromCoarseGpsAnchorWithoutBiasFreeze` (`:274-311`, posterior error `15 * (1/(1/16 + 9/25)) / 16 = 2.22 m > 1.0 m`!). Retuning `DEFAULT_VAR_GPS` coherently (`64.0 m²`, `±8 m` vertical GPS 1σ > `DEFAULT_VAR_DEM = 25.0 m²`) satisfies both physics and `testThreeStateGpsBeforeDem` (`~0.62 m < 1.0 m`!).
  * **Blind Spot 2 (`REQ-FIL-022` Initial Anchor Re-Anchoring Preservation)**: Step-discrepancy gating MUST apply strictly to **post-anchor** updates (`hasReferenceAnchor == true`), preserving the initial `Baro-before-Ref` snap (`:418-434`) that absorbs `+130.4 m` synoptic weather offsets into `baroBias` and triggers `resetAscentDescentAnchor()` (`0 m` phantom ascent/descent) as well as `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFusedTest.testFinding4_gpsElevationIngestion_whenGpsActive` (`:309-338`).
  * **Blind Spot 3 (Transient DEM Bounce vs. Permanent Bias Correction & Barometer-Free Mode)**: Comparing `stepDiscrepancy = |Δref - Δbaro|` between consecutive reference updates + quadratic Huber variance inflation (`effectiveVarRef`) rejects transient `440 → 427 → 440 m` bounces on both edges (`0%` slope spike, `0 m` ascent) while allowing sustained plateau offsets (`Δref ≈ 0` on subsequent ticks) and gradual weather drift (`|Δref - Δbaro| ≈ 0`) to smoothly anchor `baroBias` and loop closure to MSL. When `!hasBarometerSample` (barometer-free DEM-only mode), `k1` remains active and baro-step comparison is bypassed.
  * **Call-Site & Monolith LoC Audit (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt:116-134`)**: Sole production caller of `KinematicAltitudeSlopeEkf` (`503 / 1,500` LoC, `997` headroom) is `AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt` (`513 / 1,500` LoC, `987` headroom). `PhoneCarriageMode.kt` (`256` LoC, uses `DEFAULT_VAR_BARO` only), `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt` (`547` LoC), and `AltitudeFromPositionDevice.java` (`284` LoC) need `0` edits. All protected monoliths remain **`0` LoC touched**.
