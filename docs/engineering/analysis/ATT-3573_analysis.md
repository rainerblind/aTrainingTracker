## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation

In `aTrainingTracker`, turn-by-turn route navigation prompts (`REQ-MAP-028`, `REQ-UI-324`, `REQ-UI-360`, `REQ-UI-361` / `ATT-1450`, `ATT-3455`, `ATT-3493`) visually guide athletes along route polylines. When a sharp turn is detected ($120^\circ \le |\Delta\theta| < 155^\circ$), `TurnDirection.SHARP_RIGHT` and `TurnDirection.SHARP_LEFT` are displayed in the cockpit prompt banner (`TurnPromptBanner.kt`) using `ic_turn_sharp_right.xml` and `ic_turn_sharp_left.xml`.

During active cycling and running sessions, athletes glance at cockpit guidance prompts for a fraction of a second under high ambient brightness, glare, or high speeds. 
However, in production use, athletes report that `ic_turn_sharp_right` and `ic_turn_sharp_left` are frequently misread as **U-turns** (`TurnDirection.U_TURN`). This causes athletes to falsely conclude that they made a routing error or missed an intersection and must turn around $180^\circ$, when the route actually instructs them to take an acute fork or sharp turn (~$135^\circ$).

### 2.2 Three Structural Flaws Producing U-Turn Resemblance

1. **Full $180^\circ$ Semicircular Dome Top**:
   The inner curve in `ic_turn_sharp_right` consists of `c0,-2.21 1.79,-4 4,-4` (a $90^\circ$ arc from $x=8, y=9$ to $x=12, y=5$) followed by `c2.21,0 4,1.79 4,4` (a second $90^\circ$ arc from $x=12, y=5$ to $x=16, y=9$).
   The outer curve mirrors this with radius $6$ from $x=18$ across to $x=6$.
   This produces a full $180^\circ$ circular semicircle spanning $12\text{dp}$ across the canvas, identical to the dome of `ic_turn_u_turn`.
2. **Vertical Downward Exit Shaft**:
   After completing the $180^\circ$ turn, the right shaft descends vertically (`v2.59` and `V11.59`) along $x=16$ and $x=18$. It does not diverge diagonally.
3. **Vertical Downward-Pointing Arrowhead ($180^\circ$, South $\downarrow$)**:
   The arrowhead tip is generated by `l4,4l4,-4` with the apex at $(17.0, 15.41)$. The vector from arrow base $(17.0, 11.41)$ to apex $(17.0, 15.41)$ points $(0, +4)$, which is strictly vertical South ($180^\circ$).

Consequently, both `ic_turn_sharp_right` and `ic_turn_u_turn` share:
- Vertical left stem
- Wide $180^\circ$ circular loop at the top
- Vertical right shaft
- Arrowhead pointing straight down ($\downarrow$)

In athletic glance conditions, human visual perception quantizes both icons as an inverted "U" with a downward arrow—the universal symbol for a U-turn.

## 3. Angular Progression & Design Invariants

According to `TurnCueDetector.kt` and `docs/design_guidelines.md` (Section 5.5), navigation cues form a strict angular sequence:
- **`STRAIGHT`**: $0^\circ$ (heading North, $\uparrow$)
- **`SLIGHT_RIGHT`**: $+45^\circ$ (heading North-East, $\nearrow$)
- **`RIGHT`**: $+90^\circ$ (heading East, $\rightarrow$)
- **`SHARP_RIGHT`**: $+135^\circ$ (heading South-East, $\searrow$)
- **`U_TURN`**: $+180^\circ$ (heading South, $\downarrow$)

Every other icon in the family aligns its arrowhead direction with its exit trajectory:
- `ic_turn_slight_right`: 45° exit shaft, 45° diagonal arrowhead ($\nearrow$).
- `ic_turn_right`: 90° horizontal shaft, 90° rightward arrowhead ($\rightarrow$).
- `ic_turn_u_turn`: 180° vertical shaft, 180° downward arrowhead ($\downarrow$).

Therefore, for `ic_turn_sharp_right`:
1. The turn at the top MUST execute an acute deflection of $\sim 135^\circ$, ending with tangent vector $(+1/\sqrt{2}, +1/\sqrt{2})$ (South-East).
2. The exit shaft MUST extend diagonally down-right along $(+1, +1)$ at $135^\circ$, NOT vertically.
3. The arrowhead MUST point diagonally down-right ($\searrow$ at $135^\circ$), NOT vertically downward ($\downarrow$).
4. The loop MUST NOT span $12\text{dp}$ across to $x=18$ like a U-turn dome, but remain compact and acute.

## 4. Geometric Architecture & Candidate Evaluation

Four candidate geometries were prototyped and evaluated side-by-side across the full 8-icon family:

| Candidate | Geometry Description | Arrowhead Orientation | Visual Distinction from U-turn | Family Harmony |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Current (`39610f62`)** | $180^\circ$ semicircle dome, vertical shaft, downward chevron | $180^\circ$ ($\downarrow$) | ❌ Extremely Poor (looks identical) | ❌ Inconsistent (sharp turn points south) |
| **Candidate B** | Acute $135^\circ$ arc, short diagonal shaft, asymmetric barb | $135^\circ$ ($\searrow$) | ✅ High | ⚠️ Asymmetric wing stroke |
| **Candidate C** | Thin tapered bend, diagonal arrowhead | $135^\circ$ ($\searrow$) | ✅ High | ❌ Non-uniform stroke (< 1.5dp at apex) |
| **Candidate D (Selected)** | Uniform 2dp acute $135^\circ$ bend, diagonal shaft, symmetric Material arrowhead | $135^\circ$ ($\searrow$) | ✅ **Outstanding** | ✅ **100% compliant with Rule 23 & Section 5.5** |

### Selected Geometry Specification (Candidate D)

* Center of curvature: $(c_x, c_y) = (10.5, 8.5)$
* Inner radius: $r_{in} = 2.5\text{dp}$ (from inner stem $x=8$ to $c_x=10.5$)
* Outer radius: $r_{out} = 4.5\text{dp}$ (from outer stem $x=6$ to $c_x=10.5$)
* Uniform stroke width: $r_{out} - r_{in} = 2.0\text{dp}$ throughout stem, bend, and shaft.
* Grounded baseline: starts at $(6, 20)$ with `h2` to $(8, 20)$, closes at $(6, 20)$ with `V20z`.
* Acute $135^\circ$ arc: decomposes into a $90^\circ$ primary arc and a $45^\circ$ deflection arc to achieve tangent vector $(+1/\sqrt{2}, +1/\sqrt{2})$.
* Arrowhead: canonical Material Design arrowhead rotated by $45^\circ$ below horizontal, centered on the diagonal shaft, with symmetric barbs perpendicular to the trajectory.
