# Stage 1 Analysis — ATT-9601: Stationary Zero-Speed Publication & Post-Pause Velocity Spike Bounding

- **Ticket**: `ATT-9601` (`2 SP`, `Bug`, `tier-a`, Epic `ATT-166`) | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0` | **Risk Rating**: `HIGH`
- **Problem Statement**: Real-world cycling telemetry (`2026-11-22_081402`, bakery stop) exposed two defects in `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused`: (1) when entering an indoor stop with poor GNSS accuracy (`acc = 45.9 m > MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS`), `mSpeedSensor` stayed frozen at `1.536 m/s` for `14 s` until the `10 s` inactivity watchdog decayed it, delaying Auto-Pause; and (2) upon exiting after a `159 s` stop (`row 228 -> row 229`, `acc = 12.75 -> 11.73 m`, `+19.1 m` coordinate snap, Doppler `3.51 m/s`), `SPEED_mps_att_fused` spiked to `10.85 m/s` (`39.1 km/h`).

## 1. Independent Forensic Root-Cause Verification (4-Augen-Prinzip)

1. **Defect 1 — Unpublished Zero Speed on Stationary Rejected GNSS Fixes**:
   - In `app/src/main/java/com/atrainingtracker/banalservice/devices/SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` (`L626-628`) and pre-anchor `L554-559`, `ekf.applyZeroVelocityUpdate(timestampMs)` zeroes `ekf.speedMps = 0.0`, but the early `return` on `!gnssAccepted && (isStationary || fusedSpeed <= SPEED_ZERO_THRESHOLD)` skips updating `mSpeed`, `mSpeedSensor`, and `mPaceSensor`. Thus `mSpeedSensor` remains latched at the last moving value until `INACTIVITY_TIMEOUT_MS` (`10 s`) + exponential decay (`~4 s`).
   - **Lock-Order Constraint**: `shutDown()`/`onAccumulatorsReset()` are `@Synchronized` on `this` and acquire `synchronized(ekf)` inside; any `synchronized(this)` helper (`publishStationaryZeroSpeed()`) MUST be invoked strictly outside `synchronized(ekf)` to prevent AB-BA lock inversion.
2. **Defect 2 — Dual Compounding Root Cause Behind the Exact `10.85 m/s` Post-Pause Spike**:
   - **Cause 2A (`UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:439-628`)**: After heading is anchored once (`coldStartHelper.hasAnchoredHeading == true`), `coldStart.decouplePositionVelocity` is permanently `false` (`AttFusedColdStartHelper.kt:213`). During a `159 s` stop/outage, 50-Hz `ekf.predict()` builds `P[pos, v]` cross-covariance and inflates `P[pos, pos]`. When outdoor fixes resume (`row 228` -> `row 229`), `mahalanobis <= 3.0` (`GNSS_HUBER_GAMMA`), so `confirmedPostOutage` never triggers and `k[2][0] * y0 + k[2][1] * y1` leaks the `+19.1 m` coordinate snap into `x[2]`.
   - **Cause 2B (`SpeedAndLocationDevice.java:675,719,738,774` — Independent 4-Augen Catch)**: Even when `ekf.speedMps` stays cleanly at Doppler `3.51 m/s`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice.onNewLocation()` computes `calcSpeed = 19.09 m / 1.0 s = 19.09 m/s`. Because `rawSpeed (3.51) < 0.2 * calcSpeed (3.82)` (`REQ-TRK-056.2` Google-Fused damped-Doppler fallback), `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java` **discards `rawSpeed = 3.51 m/s`**, replaces it with `speed = 19.09 m/s`, and averages `mSpeed = (2.61 + 19.09) / 2 = 10.85 m/s` (`39.1 km/h`) — reproducing the field spike to 2 decimal places!

## 2. Change-Request Impact Analysis & Chesterton's Fence

- **Affected Requirements**: Net-new `REQ-FIL-041` (`.1`–`.3`, `Derived (SW-only)`), refining `REQ-FIL-023.1`, `REQ-FIL-036.2`, and `REQ-TRK-056.2`.
- **Affected Architecture Elements**: `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java` (`841 -> ~860` LoC), `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt` (`850 -> ~875` LoC), `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` (`1,495 -> 1,497 / 1,500` LoC — strictly under the `1,500`-LoC cap). `0` SQLite schema changes, `0` UI/Compose changes, `0` `strings*.xml` edits (`Localization Parity: N/A`).
- **Verification Impact & Test Surface**: `TST-FIL-041` (`.1`–`.3`) across `UnicycleOdometryEkfTest.kt`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFusedTest.kt`, plus regression coverage on `SpeedAndLocationDeviceStationaryJitterTest.kt`, `SpeedAndLocationDeviceDecayTest.kt`, `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmootherTest.kt`, `OdometrySimulationTest.kt`, `OfflineTelemetryReplayHarnessTest.kt`, and `ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt`.
- **Risk Assessment & Invariants**: Over-decoupling could delay genuine fast starts (`0 -> 9.0 m/s`), or weakening `REQ-TRK-056.2` could regress `SpeedAndLocationDevice_GoogleFused` damped-Doppler recovery. Mitigated by (a) decoupling ONLY GNSS position-to-velocity gain `k[2][0..1]` for `POST_PAUSE_DECOUPLE_FIXES = 3` moving fixes (never clamping `ekf.updateSpeed()` for Doppler or wheel speed), and (b) gating the base-class geometric override behind `protected boolean trustsFusedLocationSpeed() { return false; }`, overridden to `true` strictly in `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused`.
- **Chesterton's Fence (`REQ-TRK-056.2`, `REQ-FIL-023.1`, `REQ-FIL-036.2`)**:
  - `REQ-TRK-056.2` (`196b3e67`, `ATT-6256`) was built specifically for `SpeedAndLocationDevice_GoogleFused` reporting damped speed `< 0.2 * calcSpeed` during desk/mock replays; keeping `trustsFusedLocationSpeed() == false` by default preserves `SpeedAndLocationDevice_GPS` and `SpeedAndLocationDevice_GoogleFused` bit-for-bit.
  - `REQ-FIL-023.1` (`8b0eba41`, `ATT-8576`) & `REQ-FIL-036.2` (`97f49453`, `ATT-9158`) invariants (`MIN_DOPPLER_SPEED_MPS = 3.0`, `ZUPT_SPEED_VARIANCE = 2.0`, `MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS = 20.0`, `coldStartHelper` 180° heading seed, `confirmedPostOutage` recovery, stationary anchor pinning) remain 100% preserved.
