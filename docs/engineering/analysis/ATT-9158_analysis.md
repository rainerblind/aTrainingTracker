## 1. Problem Statement & Field Evidence
* Pixel 10 easy run `Kurze Runde var 2 #53` (`2026-11-19_184953`, `_id = 2414`, `~8:15/km`) exposed three coupled cold-start & ERTS defects:
  1. **23 s Cold-Start Pinning Freeze (`t = 1..23 s`)**: `att_fused` pinned `SPEED_mps`, `DISTANCE_m`, and `LATITUDE`/`LONGITUDE` at `0.0` even while raw GPS (`acc = 9.2–18.9 m`, `1.0–2.3 m/s`) and Google Fused (`acc = 3.4–4.8 m`) recorded `30–38 m` of steady running.
  2. **Teleport Unpin & ERTS `5.73 m/s` (`20.6 km/h`) Catch-Up Spike**: At `t = 24 s` and `t = 31 s`, `att_fused` jumped `9.34 m` and `12.20 m`; post-workout `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer` then synthesized an artificial `5.73 m/s` peak (`t = 30–34 s`) overwriting `ExtremumValues.MAX(SPEED_mps)` when all normal live speeds were `< 3.3 m/s`.
  3. **Distance Shortfall (`119–177 m`)**: Canonical ERTS `DISTANCE_m` (`5,364 m`) fell `120 m` below `att_fused` (`5,484 m`) and `158–177 m` below GPS (`5,522 m`) / Google Fused (`5,541 m`), starting with `~35–40 m` lost during the `t = 1..23 s` freeze.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Verification
* **Verified Root Causes on `sprint/2026-11-20_01_Früh` (`6b4ac2c5`)**:
  1. **Unconditional `distFromAnchor > deadbandRadius` Pin Lock (`SpeedAndLocationDevice.java:584, 614-628, 658` & `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:699`)**: Initial fix pins `mIsStationaryPinned = true` (`:658`). Because `ATTFused` preserves `location.accuracy = max(acc, sqrt(fusedPosVar))` (`:699`), `deadbandRadius = max(3.0, 0.6 * acc)` reaches `11.34 m` at `18.9 m` cold-start accuracy (`9.0 m` at `15 m`). `onNewLocation()` refuses to unpin until EKF-smoothed `distFromAnchor > deadbandRadius` even when `!mIsIndoorMode && !extStationary && !accelAtRest` and sustained Doppler speed (`> 1.0 m/s`) + active running IMU/steps confirm motion. Upon release (`t = 24 s`), `:632` dumps `delta_distance = distFromAnchor` (`9.34 m`) in 1 tick (and `:644-648` can even override speed with geometric `calcSpeed`).
  2. **EKF Cold-Start Lag, Wrong Default Heading (`PI/2`), Speed Crushing & Secondary Snap (`UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:53-55, 207-228, 510-597` & `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:574-650`)**:
     * Before heading anchors (`MIN_DOPPLER_SPEED_MPS = 3.0 m/s` never fires on an `8:15/km` `~2.0 m/s` run; chord heading `:622-647` requires `acc <= 15 m`, committed `RUN_*`/`WALKING_TRANSITION` mode, and `>= 5 m` on lagged `fusedEast/fusedNorth`), `ekf.predict()` dead-reckons along hardcoded `PI/2` (North) and builds positive `P[pos_N, v]`. Moving South/East/West causes `updateGnssPosition()` (`K[2][0..1] · y < 0`) to actively crush `x[2]` back toward `0` while coarse `speedVar = (acc * 0.25)^2` (`22.3 (m/s)^2` at `18.9 m`) and `R_pos = acc^2` (`357 m^2` vs `P_pos = 25 m^2`, `K_pos ≈ 0.065`) starve convergence.
     * After `20+` lagged fixes, `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:521-555` hits `consecutiveMovingOutliers >= 3` (`mahalanobis > 3.0`), inflates `p[0][0] = y0^2; p[1][1] = y1^2`, and teleports `[E, N]` by `12.20 m` (`t = 31 s`).
  3. **ERTS Position-Before-Speed Leak & Backward RTS Catch-Up Velocity Overshoot (`ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.kt:216-264, 434-454` & `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt:841-851`)**:
     * In forward replay (`:438-453`), `updateGnssPosition()` runs **before** `updateSpeed()` (opposite of live `REQ-FIL-023.1`), while `ZUPT_SPEED_VARIANCE = 2.0` leaves `P[pos, v] >= 2.0` across `t = 23 -> 24 s`. A `9.34 m` / `12.20 m` teleport injects a huge velocity kick via `K[2][0..1] · y`, and coarse `sVar = max(0.5, (acc * 0.25)^2)` (`6.25–14.1 (m/s)^2`) + Huber down-weighting prevents the true `~2.0 m/s` speed measurement from correcting it.
     * In backward smoothing (`:216-264`), `C_k[2, 0..1]` couples the forward-directed position correction `deltaX[0..1]` across the teleport jump directly into `smoothedStates[k][2]`, synthesizing a `5.73 m/s` catch-up sprint at `t = 30–34 s` which `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt:842` writes to `ExtremumValues.MAX(SPEED_mps)`. Coarse `sVar` + position-before-speed on curved 1-Hz heading error also contributes to the remaining ERTS `-2.2%` (`~120 m`) distance shortfall beyond the `~38 m` cold-start freeze loss.
* **Independent Peer Review & Verification (Blind Spots, Mathematical Invariants & Architecture)**:
  * **Cold-Start Unpin vs. Indoor Spiderweb Suppression (`REQ-TRK-048`/`077`, `REQ-FIL-023.1`)**: Unpinning under coarse `10–20 m` accuracy MUST NEVER bypass `mIsIndoorMode`, `extStationary`, `accelAtRest`, or stationary `gpsSpeed <= 0.1 m/s` (`TST-FIL-023.1` 25.2 m convergence jump). Releasing strictly on **corroborated sustained motion** (`!mIsIndoorMode && !extStationary && !accelAtRest` + `>= 2–3` consecutive fixes with trusted speed `>= ~0.8–1.0 m/s` or active IMU steps/non-stationary carriage + consistent raw GNSS progression) eliminates the 23 s freeze while keeping indoor/café/cold-stationary pinning 100% intact.
  * **ERTS Catch-Up Velocity Gating vs. Genuine Sprint Acceleration**: In a genuine sprint (`2.0 -> 6.0 m/s`), live speed measurements (`speedMps`/`wheelSpeedMps`) themselves rise smoothly alongside matching `|Δpos| ≈ v·dt`. In a post-unpin teleport, `|Δpos|` jumps far beyond `localSpeed·dt` (or a single-epoch geometric spike stands isolated above surrounding live speeds). Reordering `updateSpeed` before `updateGnssPosition` (with realistic Doppler speed variance + single-tick teleport-speed gating), decoupling `P[0..1, 2]` / `F[0..1, 2]` across detected position-step discontinuities (reusing `copyAndDecoupleVelocityPosition` in `smoothSnapshots()`), and bounding backward position-to-speed innovation prevents synthetic `5.73 m/s` spikes while preserving genuine sprints and `TST-FIL-017`/`025`/`027`/`032` curves/tunnels.
  * **FOSS / Zero-GMS Boundary**: `ATT_fused` (`REQ-SEN-028`) intentionally uses `LocationManager.GPS_PROVIDER` + hardware IMU/wheel without Google Play Services; using Doppler speed + raw GNSS chord + IMU motion energy satisfies the `"and/or"` AC cleanly without introducing a GMS dependency.
  * **Call-Site & Monolith LoC Audit (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest.kt:116-134`)**: `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` is **`1,459 / 1,500` LoC (`41` LoC headroom!!)** and NOT in `MAX_MONOLITH_FILE_LOC_BASELINE`. Extracting pure cold-start release & raw-chord heading-seed helpers into a dedicated pure-Kotlin helper file (`AttFusedColdStartHelper.kt` in `banalservice.odometry`) keeps `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` safely **`<= 1,485 / 1,500` LoC**. Other candidate files have ample headroom: `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java` (`810 / 1,500`, optional minimal protected hook only so `GPS`/`GoogleFused` default behavior stays byte-identical), `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt` (`810 / 1,500`), `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.kt` (`742 / 1,500`), `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt` (`1,054 / 1,500`). All protected monoliths remain **`0` LoC touched**.
