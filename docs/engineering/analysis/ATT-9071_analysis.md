## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
`python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 qa-audit` emitted false-positive `NON-COMPLIANT` findings and spurious corrective-action tickets (`ATT-9052`–`ATT-9054` in `sprint_2026-11-18_03_nacht.md`; `ATT-9116`–`ATT-9120` in `sprint_2026-11-19_01_früh.md`) across consecutive Schichten due to premature WIP/Stage-6 sampling, non-code SUP.9 bug penalties, and strict `_plan.md` filename matching.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
1. **Stale Pre-`ATT-8207` Status Filter** (`tools/jira_util.py:5199-5203`, `tools/hoechstmass/tools/jira_util.py:1993-1997`): `completed_parents` includes `"final review (agent)"`, even though `ATT-8207` (`3b390f63`, `REQ-PRO-125`) defers `--merge-ticket` until Stage 6 `PASS`. Sampling `"final review (agent)"` audits unmerged `feature/<KEY>` work against `sprint/<SPRINT>` (e.g. `ATT-8895`).
2. **Unsafe WIP Fallback** (`tools/jira_util.py:5204`, `tools/hoechstmass/tools/jira_util.py:1998`): `pool = completed_parents if len(completed_parents) >= sample_size else parent_issues` falls back to ALL sprint tickets (`Implementation`, `Analysis`, `Zu erledigen`) when fewer than `sample_size` tickets have merged.
3. **SUP.9 Non-Code Resolutions & Missing `labels` Fetch** (`tools/jira_util.py:5139,5170`, `tools/hoechstmass/tools/jira_util.py:1958`): `qa_audit()` omits `"labels"` from `fetch_all_sprint_issues` / `fallback_fields` and does not recognize `NON_FIX_RESOLUTION_LABELS` (`resolution-duplicate`, `resolution-rejected`, `resolution-cannot-reproduce` at `tools/jira_util.py:2991-2995`), penalizing `ATT-8729` (`resolution-duplicate` of `ATT-8998`) for 0 commits/subtasks/deliverables.
4. **Plan Filename Variant** (`tools/jira_util.py:5259`, `tools/hoechstmass/tools/jira_util.py:2033`): `deliv_checks["plan"]` checks strictly `{key}_plan.md` (unlike `test_spec` accepting `_test_spec.md` or `_spec.md`), failing `ATT-8848` (`ATT-8848_impl_plan.md`).
- **Independent Peer Review & Verification (4-Augen-Prinzip)**:
  - **Blind Spot 1 (`labels` Field Omission)**: `fetch_all_sprint_issues` (`L5139`) and `fallback_fields` (`L5170`) do not request `labels`, and Höchstmaß (`L1958`) omits both `labels` and `description`. Without adding `labels`, SUP.9 `resolution-*` detection fails on live Jira payloads.
  - **Blind Spot 2 (Population `N` & Verifier Compatibility)**: `tools/jira_util.py:5197,5510` computes `min_sample` and `total_tickets` from `len(parent_issues)` instead of `len(completed_parents)` (`REQ-PRO-091.2`), and `_validate_single_qa_audit()` in `tools/verify_requirement_governance.py:752-768` (and Höchstmaß `L490-506`) rejects `< 2` sampled tickets unless zero/low-completion sprints (`N/A (0 merged tickets in sprint)` or `< 2` merged) are handled cleanly.
  - **Blind Spot 3 (`SUP.9` Pool Retention vs Exclusion)**: Exempting SUP.9 non-code tickets via `N/A (SUP.9 non-code resolution)` on code checks (while verifying `resolution-*` label / `[SUP.9 RESOLUTION:` comment & PO provenance) or excluding from code sampling must preserve `>= 2` audit coverage when a sprint contains a `resolution-*` ticket.

## 3. User Scope Grounding (ATT-1250)
- **In-Scope Goals (`2 SP`)**: (1) Restrict `completed_parents` to post-Stage-6 merged statuses (`final review (human)`, `final review`, `erledigt`, `closed`, `done`); (2) Eliminate WIP `parent_issues` fallback and handle `0` merged tickets cleanly; (3) Fetch `labels` and exempt SUP.9 `resolution-duplicate`/`resolution-rejected`/`resolution-cannot-reproduce` tickets from code-lifecycle failures; (4) Accept `{key}_plan.md` or `{key}_impl_plan.md` in `qa_audit()`; (5) Mirror fixes in `tools/hoechstmass/tools/jira_util.py` + verifier compatibility if needed + offline unit tests (`Rule 5`).
- **Out-of-Scope Non-Goals**: No changes to `app/src/main/`, Stage 3 `SKILL.md` naming guidance (`ATT-9071_plan.md` remains canonical), `sprint_metrics.py`, or the 4 user-owned dirty `tools/` files.

## 6. Architectural Strategy & High-Level Solution
1. Include `"labels"` in `qa_audit()` Jira issue queries (root + Höchstmaß) and restrict `completed_parents` strictly to `("final review (human)", "final review", "erledigt", "closed", "done")`.
2. Remove the `else parent_issues` WIP fallback; sample `min(sample_size, len(completed_parents))` from `completed_parents` and emit explicit `N/A (0 merged tickets in sprint)` COMPLIANT summary without CA creation when empty (and allow valid `0`/`< 2` merged-ticket audits in `_validate_single_qa_audit()`).
3. Detect SUP.9 non-code resolutions (`resolution-duplicate`, `resolution-rejected`, `resolution-cannot-reproduce`) and mark code-lifecycle checks `N/A (SUP.9 non-code resolution)` (verifying SUP.9 metadata + PO approval provenance) without raising non-compliances or CA tickets.
4. Accept both `docs/engineering/plans/{key}_plan.md` and `docs/engineering/plans/{key}_impl_plan.md` in `deliv_checks["plan"]`, and add mocked regression tests covering all 4 criteria in both root and Höchstmaß suites.
