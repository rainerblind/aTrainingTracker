## Context & Problem Statement
Two domain-calculation defects in `PowerPhysiologyCalculator.kt` distort the Performance Management Chart (PMC) on **Fitness & Physiologie -> Fitness & Ermüdung**:
1. **Low-CTL ACWR denominator explosion**: `computePerformanceManagementChart()` (`:1073`) computes `acwr = atl / ctl` whenever `ctl > 0.01`. During cold starts or post-break ramp-ups (e.g., `CTL = 7.0`, `ATL = 22.7`), `ACWR` spikes to `3.26` and displays a false red **Risikobereich** alarm on modest load. Gating `ACWR` on `CTL >= 15.0` (`MIN_CTL_FOR_ACWR`) and displaying `—` + a neutral localized hint (*Building base* / *Aufbauphase*) eliminates false alarms.
2. **Tier-4 Session-RPE 4–5x scale inflation**: `resolveWorkoutTrainingLoad()` (`:990-998`) feeds raw Foster Session-RPE (`RPE * minutes`, `360–480` AU for 1h @ RPE 6–8) directly into the TSS-scaled PMC alongside Tier 1–2 power `TSS` and Tier 3 `hrTSS` (`100` for 1h @ threshold). Tier 4 must normalize RPE + duration onto the TSS scale (`calculateRpeTss`, calibrated to 1h @ RPE 8 = `100` TSS) while keeping `calculateSessionRpe()` (`REQ-WKO-038`) 100% untouched for `WorkoutRpeFeelSection.kt`.
