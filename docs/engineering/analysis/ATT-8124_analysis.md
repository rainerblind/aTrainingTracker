## 2. Architectural Resolution & Compatibility Boundaries
* **Preserve `ATT-6178` & `ATT-9154` & `ATT-6034`**: Keep `TrackerService.onDestroy()` non-blocking (`ATT-6178`), keep `endWorkout()` -> `EditWorkoutScreen` `< 1 s` via `WorkoutPostProcessingCoordinator` (`ATT-9154`), and keep `TrackerService.java <= 3,559` LoC (currently `3,550`, est. `+3` net -> `3,553/3,559`) by extracting post-backfill physiological recomputation into a cohesive Kotlin helper `PostWorkoutPhysiologyRecomputer.kt` (`ADR-001` / `ATT-6034` alignment).
* **Single-Pass Physiology & HRV**: When `PolarH10OfflineController.isRecordingArmed()` is `true`, `TrackerService.finalizeLiveSession()` skips pre-backfill EF/Decoupling/`PowerHrDynamicsCalculator` (`L3275–3296`) so `WorkoutPostProcessingCoordinator`'s `RECOMPUTE` stage executes `PostWorkoutPhysiologyRecomputer.recomputeAfterHrBackfill()` (HR `MIN`/`MAX`/`AVG`, EF, Aerobic Decoupling, `PowerHrDynamicsCalculator`) and `HrvPostWorkoutRecomputer.recomputeWorkout()` **strictly once** after H10 backfill (with `PolarH10OfflineController.stopAndBackfillWorkout(..., runPostBackfillRecompute = false)` eliminating the duplicate HRV call). `syncPendingWorkout()` also invokes `PostWorkoutPhysiologyRecomputer.recomputeAfterHrBackfill()` on deferred syncs.
* **GATT Yield Preservation (`TST-CON-051.3`)**: `MyBTLEDevice.pauseGattOperations()` disconnects/closes `mBluetoothGatt` via `disconnectFromGattInternal(null, false, /* preserveBackgroundReconnect = */ true)` so `mIsBackgroundReconnecting` survives pause/resume for `TST-CON-051.3` / `TST-SET-125.2`.
