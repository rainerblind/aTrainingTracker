**Ticket**: `ATT-8880` (`3 SP`, `severity-major`) | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-166` | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0`

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
* Pixel 10 cycling telemetry (`Zum Bäcker #57`, `workoutID = 2191`, `607 s`, smartphone in front jeans pocket) exposed 5 compounding defects across `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer`, `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother`, `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother`, `UnicycleOdometryEkf`, and `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused`:
  1. SQLite `time` (`DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`) batch-flush quantization (`dt = 0 s / 2 s` alternation on ~40% of rows) causes `dtSec = 0.001 s` EKF stalls, `-5.3 m` turn-braking innovations (`-88.65 m` distance loss), `1-Hz` yaw anti-aliasing bypasses, `72` `DISTANCE_m` freezes, and `81` `VERTICAL_SPEED` `0 m/h` sawtooth drops.
  2. `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` overwrites `mWorldYawRateSensor` with the last 20-ms gyro sample, logging stroboscopic `1.2–1.5 Hz` thigh-pedaling aliases (`±0.5..4.81 rad/s`) into `WORLD_YAW_RATE_radps` and live wheel calibration.
  3. Indoor bakery stop (`140 s`, `acc = 47.4..50.0 m`, `speed = 0.0 m/s`) omits ZUPT and stationary cross-covariance decoupling, letting backward RTS pull post-stop departure motion backwards (`+31.66 m` phantom indoor drift, pre-stop `SPEED_mps` collapsed to `0.0 m/s`).
  4. `ALLOWED_CANONICAL_COLUMNS` omits `DISTANCE_m_LAP` (desynced vs. `Laps.db`), and `updateWorkoutSummaries()` encodes unsimplified `1-Hz` `mapPolyline` / `distanceStream` / `altitudeStream` instead of matching `TrackerService.finalizeLiveSession()`.
  5. `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother` sums every `deltaAlt > 0.0` without `2.0 m` hysteresis (`+22.88 / -23.66 m` vs. `+14.57 / -14.56 m`), and `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer` reads filtered canonical `ALTITUDE` instead of pre-filtering raw barometer altitude through `BarometricAltitudeFilter.filterSlewOnly()`.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Independent Hypothesis Verification
* **Defect 1 — Batch Timestamp Quantization (`ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt:172, 195-200`)**:
  * `readWorkoutSamples()` parses only `WorkoutSamplesDbHelper.TIME` (`DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`), ignoring `SensorType.TIME_TOTAL`. Duplicate UTC seconds yield `dtSec = max(0.001, 0.0) = 0.001` in `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.smoothSamples()` (`:299`) and `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.smooth()` (`:127`), bypassing `useAntiAliasedYaw` (`dtSec >= 0.5`, `:303`), producing `0.0 m` in `smoothSnapshots()` (`:230`), and emitting `0.0` (`0 m/h`) in `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.smooth()` (`:303-308`).
  * **Fix path**: Reconstruct monotonic `timestampMs` in `readWorkoutSamples()` from `TIME_TOTAL` anchored to the first valid `time` epoch, falling back to `parseTimestampMs(timeStr)` with strictly monotonic `max(parsedTs, prevTs + 1000L)` when `TIME_TOTAL` is absent/null/non-increasing (preserving pause gaps and existing test schemas lacking `TIME_TOTAL`).
* **Defect 2 — Stroboscopic 50-Hz Gyro Aliasing (`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt:230, 361, 464`)**:
  * `onSensorChanged()` (`:230`) and `ingestImuMeasurement()` (`:464`) call `mWorldYawRateSensor?.newValue(worldYawRate)` on every 20-ms sample. Because `TrackerService` (`:2714`) polls `getAllSensorData()` at `1 Hz` without calling a reset hook, AND `onLocationChanged()` (`:361`) reads `mWorldYawRateSensor?.value` for `processWheelCalibrationStep()`, both `WORLD_YAW_RATE_radps` logging and wheel calibration receive raw 20-ms pedal-stroke peaks.
  * **Peer Check**: `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFusedTest.kt:126-139` asserts immediate emission (`0.1 rad/s`) after a single `ingestImuMeasurement()` call. A **rolling `1.0 s` time-weighted mean window** ($\sum \omega_i \Delta t_i / \sum \Delta t_i$, keeping at least the latest sample) publishes immediately on sample 1, cancels cyclic pedaling oscillations regardless of poll phase, and must reset (alongside `mLastGyroTimestampNs = 0L`) in `resetOriginAndEkf()` (`:475`) and `shutDown()` (`:491`).
* **Defect 3 — Indoor Stop Backward RTS Drift (`ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.kt:174-204, 360-370` & `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:351-358, 411-612`)**:
  * `smoothSamples()` calls `ekf.updateGnssPosition()` without `isStationary` (`false` default, `:361`) and never calls `ekf.applyZeroVelocityUpdate()`. At `acc = 50.0 m > MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS (20.0)`, position updates are rejected while `ekf.updateSpeed(0.0, sVar = 156.25)` (`:367`) lets `P[2][2]` and `P[0..1][2]` grow across 140 s; backward RTS (`smoothSnapshots()`, `:174-217`) then drags post-stop departure motion into the stop and drives pre-stop speed negative.
  * **Peer Check**: `applyZeroVelocityUpdate()` (`UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt:354`) intentionally keeps `p[2][2] = max(p[2][2], ZUPT_SPEED_VARIANCE (2.0))` for live `UnicycleOdometryEkfTest` re-acceleration, and `smoothSamples()` constructs its own `snapshots` (`:313-323, 396`). Therefore: (a) detect stationary epochs strictly as `speedMps != null && speedMps <= 0.05 && (wheelSpeedMps == null || wheelSpeedMps <= 0.05)` so canopy Doppler-dropout tests with `wheelSpeedMps = 10.0` (`ExtendedRtsOdometrySmootherTest.kt:140`) are unaffected; (b) invoke `ekf.applyZeroVelocityUpdate()` and `isStationary = true`; (c) decouple stationary `[0..1][2]` transition-Jacobian/covariance coupling in `snapshots` / `smoothSnapshots()` and clamp `smoothedStates[k][2] = 0.0` on stationary epochs without mutating live `UnicycleOdometryEkfTest` contracts.
* **Defect 4 — `DISTANCE_m_LAP` Desync & Summary Stream Parity (`ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt:64-76, 286-343, 420-452`)**:
  * `ALLOWED_CANONICAL_COLUMNS` (`:64-76`) and `updateSamplesDatabase()` (`:288-294`) omit `SensorType.DISTANCE_m_LAP.name`. Adding it and resetting `lapCumulativeDist = 0.0` on `LAP_NR` transitions before adding `odom.deltaDistanceMeters` keeps row-level `DISTANCE_m_LAP` exact and consistent with `LapsDatabaseManager.syncLapDistancesAndSpeeds()` (`:486`).
  * `updateWorkoutSummaries()` (`:430, 438, 449`) encodes raw `1-Hz` `validLatLngs`, `distances`, and `altitudes`, missing `PolyUtil.simplify(validLatLngs, 10.0)` (`TrackerService.java:3203`) and `WorkoutSummaries.ENCODING_STEP_SIZE` (`20`-sample) subsampling (`LiveWorkoutSession.java:267`, `WorkoutSummariesDatabaseManager.java:2491, 3117`; short `< 20`-sample workouts should retain at least start/sampled/end points cleanly).
  * **Peer Check (Test Impact)**: `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputerTest.kt:218-219` and `:327-331` assert exact unsimplified point counts (`30` and `25`) on collinear straight-line routes; `PolyUtil.simplify(..., 10.0)` reduces collinear tracks to `2` endpoints, requiring explicit Stage 2/4 test contract updates (`TST-FIL-017.3`, `TST-FIL-019.3`).
* **Defect 5 — Altitude Hysteresis & Raw Barometer Pre-Filtering (`ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt:286-319` & `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt:180-217`)**:
  * `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.smooth()` (`:311-319`) sums every `deltaAlt > 0.0`. Adopting `ASCENT_DESCENT_HYSTERESIS_METERS = 2.0` (`AltitudeAndSlopeDevice_ATTFused.kt:68, 430-442`, `REQ-FIL-020.2`) eliminates micro-ripple inflation while preserving `0.0 m` standstill ascent (`ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmootherTest.kt:106`).
  * `readWorkoutSamples()` (`:180, 208`) reads filtered `ALTITUDE` instead of raw barometer altitude. **Peer Check (9-Locale Parity)**: `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.java:927-965` resolves barometer columns across all 9 locales (`"Altitude from Pressure"`, `"Höhe aus Luftdruck"`, etc. + legacy names, excluding `DEM`/`Topografische`/`GPS`). `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer` MUST match all 9-locale barometer column names (not German-only), reconstruct monotonic `timestampMs` FIRST, and pre-filter non-null barometer values through `BarometricAltitudeFilter.filterSlewOnly()`, falling back to canonical `ALTITUDE` only when absent/null.
