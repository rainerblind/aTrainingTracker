## 2. Chesterton's Fence Archaeology (`REQ-PRO-022`)

- **Original Requirement ID & Target**: Extends `REQ-MIG-053` (*Lossless FIT Round-Trip for Workout Metadata, HRV Protocol Tags & Bike Radar Developer Fields*) and `REQ-DAT-036` (*SQLite Schema Evolution & `onCreate`/`onUpgrade` Parity*) across `WorkoutSummariesDatabaseManager.java`, `WorkoutData.kt`, `WorkoutExportHeader.kt`, `WorkoutDataMapper.kt`, `WorkoutRepository.kt`, `EditWorkoutViewModel.kt`, `FitFileWriter.kt`, `ParsedWorkoutData.kt`, `FitWorkoutParser.kt`, and `LegacyImportEngine.kt`.
- **Historical Origin & Commit Trace**: `ATT-2005` introduced `WorkoutSummaries.RACE` (v23), `ATT-7747` introduced FIT `APP_METADATA_DEVELOPER_DATA_ID` fields `0..7` (`REQ-MIG-053`), `ATT-8127` hardened `matchesAppId` scoping (`REQ-MIG-058`), and `ATT-7919` bumped `WorkoutSummaries.db` to v31.
- **Root Reason for Existing Formulation**: `COMMUTE` and `TRAINER` are mutually exclusive routine contexts, while `RACE` is an independent classification flag serialized as a `UINT8` developer field (`fieldDefinitionNumber = 7`) under `APP_METADATA_DEVELOPER_DATA_ID` (`developerDataIndex = 1`) for lossless FIT round-trips without breaking third-party FIT readers.
- **Preservation of Core Invariants**: Existing `COMMUTE`/`TRAINER` mutual exclusion, `RACE` semantics, FIT developer fields `0..7` & radar fields (`developerDataIndex = 0`), third-party `.fit` backward compatibility (`isEpic = false` when absent), `StravaUploader` payload, `onCreate`/`onUpgrade` SQLite parity, and `Rule 20` cursor mapping symmetry MUST remain 100% intact.
