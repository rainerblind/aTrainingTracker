## 1. Requirement Specification (`REQ-CON-054`)

- **Priority**: High | **Type**: Functional | **Verification Method**: Unit, Integration
- **Localization Parity**: `N/A - no new or modified UI strings`.

### 1.2 Functional & Architectural Requirements
1. **`REQ-CON-054.1` (Complete Unpair/Delete & Alias-Scoped GATT/Polar Teardown)**:
   - `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator` SHALL expose `forceDisconnectDevice(context, macAddress, polarId)` and `disconnectUnpairedSessions(context, pairedIdentifiersProvider)` (with an injectable paired-identifier lookup for deterministic pure-JVM testing) that disconnect every known MAC and 8-hex canonical Polar ID alias on the shared `PolarBleApi`, clear `connectedDeviceIds`, `activeAccOwners`, and matching `activeSessions` entries for that peripheral, and MUST NOT shut down `sharedApi` or disconnect sibling paired Polar sensors when other leases/sessions remain active.
   - `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.shutDown()` SHALL disconnect across all known identifiers (`streamId`, `coordKey`, `polarId`, `macAddress`) via `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.forceDisconnectDevice(...)` while preserving ref-counted lease release; `BTLEHeartRateDevice.shutDown()` SHALL invoke `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.forceDisconnectDevice(mContext, getAddress(), null)` alongside raw GATT teardown.
   - `MyBTLEDevice.disconnectFromGatt()` SHALL execute `gatt.disconnect()` + `gatt.close()` directly when already executing on `mHandler`'s looper (while preserving async `mHandler.post(...)` when invoked off-looper or under mocked loopers), and `MyBTLEDevice.onConnectionStateChange()` SHALL immediately disconnect and close late `STATE_CONNECTED` callbacks strictly when `mIsShutDown == true` (preserving non-shutdown null-GATT behavior).
   - `DeviceDataRepository.updateDevice()` (on `isPaired: true -> false`), `DeviceDataRepository.deleteDevice()` (capturing MAC address before SQLite deletion), and `DeviceManager.pairingChanged()` (even when `deviceId` is absent from `mMyRemoteDevices`) SHALL invoke `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.forceDisconnectDevice(...)` so unpairing or deleting immediately releases lingering Polar/BLE sessions.
2. **`REQ-CON-054.2` (Connected-GATT Discovery Fallback with Scoped Service Validation)**:
   - `BTSearchForNewDevicesEngine` SHALL retain `BluetoothManager` and, on `startAsyncSearch()`, release unpaired Polar SDK sessions when searching `DeviceType.HRM`, then enumerate `BluetoothManager.getConnectedDevices(BluetoothProfile.GATT)` (guarded by `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` permission and defensive `SecurityException`/`RuntimeException` handling).
   - Connected-GATT enumeration SHALL skip null/duplicate MACs and devices already paired in `DevicesDatabaseManager` (`isBluetoothDeviceInDB && isPaired`), track connected-GATT probe addresses in `mConnectedGattProbes`, and verify target GATT service UUID presence (`BluetoothConstants.getServiceUUID(mDeviceType)`, plus `UUID_SERVICE_GARMIN_MEMBER` for `BIKE_RADAR` and CSC feature matching for cycling sensors) strictly for `mConnectedGattProbes` before emitting `newDeviceFound()`, immediately disconnecting/closing non-matching probe GATT handles while keeping existing scan-path behavior and unit tests intact.
3. **`REQ-CON-054.3` (Silent Connected BLE HRM Watchdog & CCCD Retry)**:
   - Extract a shared pure-JVM `BleHrmSilentConnectionWatchdog` (`DEFAULT_WATCHDOG_TIMEOUT_MS = 12_000L`, injectable for deterministic unit tests) with deterministic state transitions (`IDLE -> ARMED_INITIAL -> ARMED_AFTER_RESUBSCRIBE -> RECOVERING`) returning `Action.REENABLE_CCCD` on first silent timeout, `Action.DISCONNECT_AND_RECONNECT` on second silent timeout, and `Action.NONE` once disarmed by any HR notification packet or reset.
   - `BTLEHeartRateDevice` SHALL arm the watchdog upon service configuration, disarm on the first received `0x2A37` HR packet, preserve `0x2A24`/`0x2A25` DIS reads before the initial HR CCCD write, immediately retry a failed `0x2A37` `onDescriptorWriteCompleted(..., status != GATT_SUCCESS)` once, defer/skip watchdog escalation while `isGattPaused()` is true (PFTP offline yield safety), and on watchdog trigger first re-enable `0x2A37` notification + CCCD write and, if still silent, cleanly unregister sensors, disconnect/close GATT, and schedule a clean reconnect.
   - `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` SHALL arm the same `12 s` (injectable) silent-HR watchdog on connect, disarm on first non-empty `PolarHrData` sample packet, cancel on disconnect/`blePowerStateChanged(false)`/`shutDown()`, and on watchdog trigger first cancel & re-launch the `HR` stream slot (`startHrCollection()`) and, if still silent, perform clean alias-scoped disconnect and backoff reconnect rather than remaining connected with `--`.
4. **`REQ-CON-054.4` (Monolith Ceilings & Non-Regression Invariants)**:
   - `DeviceManager.java` MUST remain `<= 1,708` LoC (`+0` net LoC = `1,707 / 1,708`); `TrackerService.java` and `DevicesDatabaseManager.java` MUST have `0` edits; `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt`, `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.kt`, `MyBTLEDevice.java`, `BTLEHeartRateDevice.java`, `BTSearchForNewDevicesEngine.java`, and `DeviceDataRepository.kt` MUST each remain `<= 1,500` LoC; zero new `src/main` reads in unit tests; zero new UI strings (`Localization Parity: N/A`).

## 3. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)
- *Given* a BLE/Polar HRM is connected via `BTLEHeartRateDevice`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge`, or `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator` (optionally alongside a second paired Polar sensor), *When* the user unpairs or deletes it (including when absent from `DeviceManager.mMyRemoteDevices`), *Then* both raw `BluetoothGatt` (including late post-shutdown callbacks) and all MAC/8-hex `PolarBleApi` session aliases for that sensor tear down immediately while any sibling paired Polar sensor remains connected and streaming.
- *Given* a compatible BLE HRM (e.g., Polar Verity Sense) remains GATT-connected to the phone while unpaired or deleted, *When* `BTSearchForNewDevicesEngine.startAsyncSearch()` runs, *Then* unpaired Polar SDK sessions are released and `getConnectedDevices(BluetoothProfile.GATT)` probes surface the matching HRM without power-cycling, while already-paired DB sensors are skipped and non-matching connected peripherals are ignored and closed.
- *Given* a BLE HRM connects via `BTLEHeartRateDevice` or `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` (and is not GATT-paused for PFTP) but receives `0` HR notification packets for the `12 s` watchdog window (or receives a non-`GATT_SUCCESS` `0x2A37` CCCD write status), *When* the watchdog or retry triggers, *Then* HR CCCD (`0x2A37`) / `startHrStreaming` is re-enabled once and, if still silent for a second window, the device performs a clean disconnect/close and reconnect cycle instead of staying connected with `--`; receiving any HR packet disarms the watchdog immediately.
- *Given* all Stage 4 changes are complete, *When* unit and architecture contract tests run, *Then* `DeviceManager.java` stays at `1,707 / 1,708` LoC (`+0` net LoC), `TrackerService.java` and `DevicesDatabaseManager.java` have `0` edits, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt <= 1,500` LoC, `0` new `src/main` reads exist in tests, and all existing `REQ-CON-049`/`050`/`053`/`SET-118` tests remain green.

## 4. Test Specification (`TST-CON-054`)
- **`TST-CON-054.1` (`PolarOfflineGattCoordinatorCoexistenceTest`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridgeTest`, `MyBTLEDeviceLifecycleTest`, `BTLEHeartRateDeviceTest`, `DeviceManagerTeardownTest` — Unpair/Delete & Alias-Scoped Teardown)**: Verify `forceDisconnectDevice()` & `disconnectUnpairedSessions()` (with injectable paired lookup) disconnect MAC + 8-hex aliases and clear `connectedDeviceIds`/`activeAccOwners` without harming a sibling paired Polar sensor; verify `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.shutDown()` disconnects all aliases and `BTLEHeartRateDevice.shutDown()` invokes `forceDisconnectDevice()`; verify `MyBTLEDevice.disconnectFromGatt()` runs synchronously when on handler looper and `onConnectionStateChange()` closes late `STATE_CONNECTED` strictly when `mIsShutDown == true` (preserving `MyBTLEDeviceLifecycleTest:323`); verify `DeviceManager.pairingChanged(id, false)` and `DeviceDataRepository.deleteDevice()`/`updateDevice()` release lingering Polar sessions even when absent from `mMyRemoteDevices`.
- **`TST-CON-054.2` (`BTSearchForNewDevicesEngineTest` — Connected-GATT Discovery Fallback)**: Verify `startAsyncSearch()` releases unpaired Polar SDK sessions for `HRM`, probes matching connected-GATT HRM peripherals from `BluetoothManager.getConnectedDevices(GATT)` and invokes `onNewDeviceFound()`, skips already-paired DB sensors, ignores & closes non-matching connected-GATT peripherals (missing target service UUID), handles missing `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` without throwing, and preserves existing scan-path tests.
- **`TST-CON-054.3` (`BleHrmSilentConnectionWatchdogTest`, `BTLEHeartRateDeviceTest`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridgeTest` — Silent-HR Watchdog & CCCD Retry)**: Verify `BleHrmSilentConnectionWatchdog` state machine (`REENABLE_CCCD -> DISCONNECT_AND_RECONNECT -> NONE`, disarm on packet, reset); verify `BTLEHeartRateDevice` preserves DIS `0x2A24`/`0x2A25` before HR CCCD order (`REQ-CON-049`), retries failed `0x2A37` CCCD status once, re-enables CCCD after `12 s` silence then cleanly disconnects/reconnects if still silent, disarms on first HR packet, and defers while `isGattPaused()`; verify `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` re-subscribes `HR` slot after `12 s` silence, disconnects aliases + schedules reconnect if still silent, and disarms on first HR sample.
- **`TST-CON-054.4` (Monolith Ceilings & Contract Guards)**: Verify `ArchitectureConformanceContractTest` (`DeviceManager.java <= 1,708`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt <= 1,500`, `0` edits to `TrackerService.java`/`DevicesDatabaseManager.java`), `InterfaceSignatureDriftContractTest`, `DeadCodeEliminationContractTest`, `SystemLiveTrackingRequirementsContractTest`, and `SourceTextReadingAuditContractTest` (`0` new `src/main` reads).
