## 3. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)

- **Given** `PhoneCarriageClassifier` is in `STATIONARY` and the cyclist accelerates (`2.42 -> 3.88 -> 4.60 m/s` / `3.5 -> 5.5 -> 6.1 m/s`, `acc <= 20 m`, non-quiet IMU, real displacement `> 10 m`), **When** `rawCandidate` shifts across `WALKING_TRANSITION -> BIKE_JERSEY_BACK -> BIKE_FRONT_POCKET`, **Then** `committedMode` exits `STATIONARY` within `<= 1 s` of crossing unambiguous motion speed and never stays `STATIONARY` for `3 s` at `> 5 m/s`.
- **Given** `PhoneCarriageClassifier` is in `STATIONARY` indoors or at a stop with `quietImu == true`, **When** a transient `1–2 s` coarse (`acc > 20 m`), invalid-accuracy (`NaN`, `Infinity`, `0.0`, `< 0.0`), or stagnant-position GNSS speed spike (`v = 5–7 m/s`, with or without coordinate jump) arrives, **Then** coarse/invalid coordinates are excluded from `positionSamples` (`isPositionStagnant` stays unpoisoned) and `committedMode` remains `STATIONARY` with zero false exits.
- **Given** `PhoneCarriageClassifier` is in `STATIONARY` and experiences a brief non-stationary blip followed by an `UNKNOWN` dropout gap (or returns to `STATIONARY`), **When** non-stationary evidence later resumes, **Then** `nonStationarySinceMs` restarts fresh from `effectiveNowMs` with zero accumulated stale dwell.
- **Given** a device lacking a barometer, wheel sensor, or `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` permission (or rigid handlebar with `>= 10 m` fresh trusted displacement), **When** accelerating smoothly out of `STATIONARY`, **Then** fast exit and multipath rejection behave deterministically without regressions to existing hysteresis tests.
