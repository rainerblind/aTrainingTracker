## 1. Requirement Specification (`REQ-PER-028`)

### 1.1 Problem Statement & Rationale
Visual UI inspection of the Periods view (`audit_03_zeitraeume.png`) under German locale revealed two localization defects:
1. Weekly card headers display hardcoded US month-first formatting `"Nov. 2 - Nov. 8"` instead of German day-first order (`2. Nov. – 8. Nov.` / `2. Nov. - 8. Nov.`).
2. `PeriodBarGraph` top-bar labels display hardcoded `Locale.US` decimal points (`1.7h`, `5.7h`, `5.4km`, `5.3mi`) instead of localized decimal separators (`1,7h`, `5,7h`, `5,4km` in German).

### 1.2 Functional & Architectural Requirements
1. **Active Locale Weekly Date Range Formatting (`REQ-PER-028.1`)**: Weekly period date ranges (`periodDateRange`) SHALL be formatted according to the active `Locale` (`Locale.getDefault()`). In day-first locales (e.g. German `de_DE`), the date range SHALL display day before month, and in month-first locales (e.g. US English `en_US`), month before day.
2. **Active Locale Dynamic Resolution for Cached Periods (`REQ-PER-028.2`)**: To ensure locale changes take immediate effect without database recreation, UI rendering of `periodDateRange` in `PeriodSummaryCard` and `PeriodMapScreen` SHALL dynamically resolve localized date ranges using `startTimestampS` and `endTimestampS` when available, falling back to stored `periodDateRange`.
3. **Active Locale Chart Decimal Separator Formatting (`REQ-PER-028.3`)**: Top-bar metric value labels generated by `formatMetricValue` in `PeriodBarGraph.kt` SHALL accept `locale: Locale = Locale.getDefault()`. Decimals for durations (`1h..10h`, e.g. `1,7h` in German vs `1.7h` in US English) and distances (`km`/`mi`, e.g. `5,4km` / `5,3mi` in German vs `5.4km` / `5.3mi` in US English) SHALL use the locale's decimal separator via `DecimalFormatSymbols.getInstance(locale).decimalSeparator` or localized floating-point formatting.
4. **Preservation of System Invariants (`REQ-PER-028.4`)**: Non-decimal duration formats (`<1m`, `${m}m`, `${h}h`), integer kilometer/mile values, `PeriodSummaries.db` table schema and version, period aggregation boundaries, and 100% clean-room test pass rate SHALL be preserved.

### 1.3 Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)
* **AC-1 (Date Range Day-First in German)**: *Given* German locale (`de_DE`), *When* a weekly period header renders, *Then* it displays day before month (e.g. `2. Nov. – 8. Nov.` / `2. Nov. - 8. Nov.`).
* **AC-2 (Chart Decimal Comma in German)**: *Given* German locale (`de_DE`), *When* `PeriodBarGraph` renders duration (1h..10h) or fractional distance, *Then* labels display comma decimal separators (`1,7h`, `5,4km`, `5,3mi`).
* **AC-3 (US Locale Parity)**: *Given* US English locale (`en_US`), *When* rendered, *Then* date range displays month before day (`Nov 2 – Nov 8`) and chart labels display dot decimal separators (`1.7h`, `5.4km`).
* **AC-4 (Cached Dynamic Locale Adaption)**: *Given* cached periods in `PeriodSummaries.db` generated under English, *When* device locale switches to German, *Then* card headers render German day-first date ranges without database migration.
