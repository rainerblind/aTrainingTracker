## 1. Requirements Summary (`REQ-CON-056` — Priority: `High`, Type: `Functional`, Verification: `Unit, Integration`)

| Sub-ID | Normative Contract (SHALL / MUST) |
|---|---|
| `REQ-CON-056.1` | `TuningPreferencesDataStore` SHALL store radar tail-light preferences exclusively under two canonical keys: `KEY_RADAR_LIGHT_CONTROL_MODE` (`RadarLightControlMode`, unified fresh-install default `SMART_AUTOMATION`) and `KEY_RADAR_LIGHT_DEFAULT_MODE` (`RadarLightMode`, default `DAY_FLASH`). A one-time `DataMigration<Preferences>` wired into `user_settings` DataStore SHALL migrate all legacy radar-light keys (`KEY_RADAR_LIGHT_AUTO_MODE`, `KEY_RADAR_AUTO_LIGHT_CONTROL`, `KEY_RADAR_SMART_LIGHT_AUTOMATION`) while preserving existing users' effective control mode (`KEY_RADAR_LIGHT_CONTROL_MODE` > `KEY_RADAR_LIGHT_AUTO_MODE` > `KEY_RADAR_SMART_LIGHT_AUTOMATION` > `KEY_RADAR_AUTO_LIGHT_CONTROL`), remove those 3 legacy keys + `LEGACY_MAP_CAMERA_KEYS`, and migrate legacy `SharedPreferences` cluster tolerances into DataStore. All `TuningConfig` boolean projections (`radarLightAutoMode`, `radarAutoLightControl`, `radarSmartLightAutomation`) SHALL be pure derived properties of `radarLightControlMode` with zero contradictory combinations. Per-startup `purgeLegacyMapCameraPreferences()` IO launches, per-save `LEGACY_MAP_CAMERA_KEYS` loops, and `TrainingApplication.setClusterTol*` dual-write mirrors SHALL be removed, with `TrainingApplication.getClusterTol*` delegating to DataStore when initialized. |
| `REQ-CON-056.2` | `RadarLightController` SHALL remove all local preference caches (`cachedAutoMode`, `cachedAutoLightControl`, `cachedLightDefaultMode`, `cachedControlMode`, `hasLoadedInitialPreferences`) and dead `updateCachedPreferences(...)` overloads, read live config directly from `TuningPreferencesDataStore`'s in-memory `StateFlow` (zero `runBlocking`), and dispatch `persistSelectionAsync` on a single long-lived controller `CoroutineScope(SupervisorJob() + Dispatchers.IO)`. `TrackerService` SHALL delete `mCachedAutoPauseConfig`, `mLastAutoPauseConfigFetchMs`, `AUTO_PAUSE_CONFIG_CACHE_TTL_MS`, and `invalidateAutoPauseCache()`/`invalidateAutoPauseConfigCache()`, reading directly via `SettingsDataStoreJavaHelper.getAutoPauseConfig(this)` (`StateFlow.value`, zero `runBlocking`) so every Auto-Pause toggle (`bike`/`run`/`other`/`startInAutoPause`/`resetToDefaults`) takes effect immediately while keeping `TrackerService.java <= 3,220` LoC and `TrainingApplication.java <= 2,050` LoC. |
| `REQ-CON-056.3` | `RadarStateRepository` SHALL split monolithic `RadarState` into three orthogonal `StateFlow`s: `threatState: StateFlow<RadarThreatState>` (multi-source arbitration `BLE > ANT_PLUS > SIMULATOR`, `CONNECTED > CONNECTING`, `sourceStates <= 4`), `lightState: StateFlow<RadarLightState>`, and `snoozeState: StateFlow<RadarAudioSnoozeState>` carrying monotonic `snoozeUntilElapsedRealtimeMs: Long?` instead of a 1-Hz background ticker. `lightState` and `snoozeState` updates SHALL execute atomically via `MutableStateFlow.update { ... }`. `RadarSnoozeController` SHALL replace its 1-Hz polling loop with a single one-shot expiry timer (`delay(remainingMs)`), emitting `snoozeState` only on state transitions and expiry. `SmartRadarLightEngine` SHALL observe ONLY `threatState`; `RadarAlertManager` SHALL observe `threatState` and `lightState.map { it.lightMode }.distinctUntilChanged()` (zero `snoozeState` subscription), guaranteeing **0** wakeups or cooldown extensions on timed snooze countdown progression. Combined `RadarState` SHALL be derived via `combine` strictly at UI/ViewModel boundaries (`RadarEdgeOverlay` derives badge minutes locally from `snoozeUntilElapsedRealtimeMs`). |
| `REQ-CON-056.4` | The AB-BA `synchronized` deadlock cycle between `RadarLightController` and `SmartBrakeLightDetector` SHALL be eliminated by construction: `SmartBrakeLightDetector.isEnabled` and `isAutoBypassed` MUST use lock-free `AtomicBoolean`, and neither `RadarLightController` (`registerRadarDevice`, `unregisterRadarDevice`, `triggerBrakeLight`, `checkAndRestorePreBrakeMode`) nor `SmartBrakeLightDetector` (`onLinearAccelerationChanged`, `stopListening`, `resetMotionState`) SHALL hold its internal monitor while invoking any method on the other class, `RadarLightDeviceClient`, `SmartRadarLightEngine`, or `RadarStateRepository`. |

## 3. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)

- **Given** a fresh installation or an existing profile containing any combination of the 5 legacy radar-light keys, `LEGACY_MAP_CAMERA_KEYS`, or legacy SharedPreferences cluster tolerances, **When** DataStore initializes and settings or Auto-Pause toggles are changed, **Then** migration runs once preserving prior behavior, all queries agree on one canonical `RadarLightControlMode` + `RadarLightMode`, and changes take immediate effect in `RadarLightController` and `TrackerService` without manual cache invalidation.
- **Given** an active 15-minute or 30-minute audio snooze running concurrently with 8-Hz radar vehicle packets, **When** time advances during snooze and threats arrive, **Then** snooze never overwrites vehicle targets, `lightState`/`snoozeState` update atomically via `MutableStateFlow.update`, `SmartRadarLightEngine` and `RadarAlertManager` receive **0** 1-Hz countdown wakeups, and snooze expires cleanly at `snoozeUntilElapsedRealtimeMs`.
- **Given** concurrent mode/device registration changes in `RadarLightController` and deceleration/stop events in `SmartBrakeLightDetector` across parallel threads under load, **When** both paths execute simultaneously, **Then** neither monitor is ever acquired inside the other (`findDeadlockedThreads() == null`) and zero deadlocks or ANRs occur.
