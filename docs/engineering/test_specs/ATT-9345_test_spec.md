## 1. Requirement Summary (`REQ-SET-126` [Planned])
- **`REQ-SET-126.1` (Verity Sense Workout PPI Gating & Explicit Firmware Stop)**: `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` SHALL gate Polar Verity Sense `startPpiStreaming()` strictly to non-workout resting modes (`mode != TrackingMode.TRACKING && mode != TrackingMode.PAUSED`), preserving resting `READY` / Morning HRV checks (`ATT-9176`/`ATT-9177`). On `TrainingApplication.TRACKING_STATE_CHANGED` entering `TRACKING`/`PAUSED`, the bridge SHALL cancel `StreamSlot.PPI` and explicitly call `api.stopStreaming(sessionId, PmdMeasurementType.PPI)` without interrupting `HR`, `ACC`, or `GYRO`; returning to `READY` resumes resting PPI cleanly.
- **`REQ-SET-126.2` (Stale/Flatline HR & RR Clearing + Recovery)**: Pure-JVM `PolarHrStreamHealthMonitor` SHALL prevent `silentHrWatchdog` from disarming on `sample.hr <= 0` or lost contact (`contactStatusSupported && !contactStatus`), clear `mHeartRateSensor.newValue(null)` and `mRrIntervalSensor?.newValue(null)` on `hr <= 0`/no-contact persistence (`>= 10 s`) or HR packet silence (`>= 15 s`), clear `mRrIntervalSensor?.newValue(null)` on sustained `ARTIFACT_GAP`-only PPI/RR bursts (`>= 10 s`), detect sustained constant-value HR flatline (`>= 60 s` identical `hr > 0`) or rolling `>= 95%` optical HRV artifact saturation, trigger cooldown-bounded recovery (stopping Verity `PPI` + re-subscribing `HR` without disturbing `ACC`/`GYRO`), and trigger `MySensorManager.reevaluatePrimaryHrSensor()`.
- **`REQ-SET-126.3` (Health- & Modality-Aware Primary `HR` Arbitration)**: Pure-JVM `PrimaryHrSensorArbitrator` + lock-free `HrSensorHealthProvider` in `MySensorManager` SHALL order `SensorType.HR` candidates deterministically by `(healthTier [HEALTHY (0) < DEGRADED (1) < STALE_OR_NULL (2)], modalityTier [ECG (0) < ANT+ (1) < Optical PPG (2) < Other (3)], registrationOrder)`. When ALL HR sensors are `STALE_OR_NULL`, highest-modality sensor remains bound to `ProxySensor` (no spurious `mSensorsSensor` drop while stale sensors emit `null`). All health reads inside `synchronized (MySensorManager.this)` MUST be lock-free, and `ProxySensor.setSourceSensor(...)` MUST execute outside the lock.
- **`REQ-SET-126.4` (Monolith Ceilings & Detailed Design)**: `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt <= 1,500` LoC (`<= 1,495`, unchanged public JVM signatures); `MySensorManager.java <= 1,500` LoC; `0` edits to `DeviceManager.java`, `TrackerService.java`, `DevicesDatabaseManager.java`, `PolarVeritySenseOfflineController.kt`; `docs/architecture/detailed_design/my_btle_device.md` updated; `0` UI strings.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)
1. **Verity Workout PPI Gating**: *Given* Polar Verity Sense with `polarLiveImuEnabled = true` in `READY`, *When* connected, *Then* `startPpiStreaming` runs for resting HRV; *When* `TRACKING_STATE_CHANGED` enters `TRACKING`/`PAUSED`, *Then* `StreamSlot.PPI` cancels and `stopStreaming(PPI)` runs while `HR`/`ACC`/`GYRO` continue uninterrupted.
2. **Stale/Flatline HR & RR Clearing**: *Given* an HRM emitting `hr <= 0`/lost contact, going silent (`> 15 s`), flatlining at constant `79 bpm` (`>= 60 s`), or emitting only `ARTIFACT_GAP` PPI beats (`>= 10 s`), *When* evaluated by `PolarHrStreamHealthMonitor`, *Then* `silentHrWatchdog` is not disarmed on `hr <= 0`, stale HR/RR clear to `null`, flatlined/saturated streams mark degraded + run bounded recovery, and primary `HR` re-arbitrates.
3. **Health- & Modality-Aware Primary `HR`**: *Given* Verity Sense (PPG) registered before Polar H10 (ECG), *When* both are healthy, *Then* canonical `HR` selects Polar H10; *When* H10 drops/stales while Verity is healthy, *Then* canonical `HR` fails over to Verity; *When* Verity flatlines (`79 bpm`) or hits `100%` artifact rate while H10 is healthy, *Then* canonical `HR` selects H10; *When* all HRMs are stale, *Then* `ProxySensor` retains highest-modality binding emitting `null`.
