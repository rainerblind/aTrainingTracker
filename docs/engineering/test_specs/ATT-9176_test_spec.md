### 1.2 Functional & Architectural Requirements
1. **`REQ-HRV-051.1` (Pure-JVM Optical PPI Processing, Hardware Quality Gating & `mRrIntervalSensor` Precision)**:
   - Extract a stateful pure-JVM helper `PolarPpiStreamProcessor` (`banalservice/devices/bluetooth_le/polar/`) processing `PolarPpiData.PolarPpiSample` batches (`ppi`, `errorEstimate`, `hr`, `blockerBit`, `skinContactStatus`, `skinContactSupported`, `timeStamp`) into monotonic `RrBeatEvent(..., isOpticalPpi = true)` instances via `RrBeatNormalization`.
   - **Zero/Negative Drop**: Samples with `ppi <= 0` SHALL be dropped completely (never passed to `RrBeatNormalization` or emitted).
   - **Hardware Artifact Stamping (`RrArtifactFilter` Preservation)**: Samples with `ppi > 0` where `blockerBit == true` or `(skinContactSupported && !skinContactStatus)` SHALL preserve timeline monotonicity via `RrBeatNormalization` and be stamped `artifactFlag = RrBeatEvent.ARTIFACT_GAP`, `gapBeats = 0` (when `ppi` is within physiological range), and `isOpticalPpi = true` so `RrArtifactFilter.isGapOrOutOfPhysiologicalRange` (`L312-315`) never overwrites them, `computeBiasFreeRmssd` excludes all adjacent pairs from clean `rMSSD` calculations, and hardware motion/contact artifacts remain visible to `HIGH_ARTIFACTS (> 5.0%)`.
   - **Clean Reference Tracking & `mRrIntervalSensor` Guard**: `lastNormalRrMs` SHALL update strictly from clean (`!isHardwareArtifact && !isOutOfPhysiologicalRange`) samples. `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` SHALL emit `mRrIntervalSensor?.newValue(...)` ONLY for non-`ARTIFACT_GAP` beats (matching `ANTHeartRateDevice.java:282`), while dispatching all processed optical PPI `RrBeatEvent`s to `perDeviceHrvController` and registered `RrBeatListener`s.
   - **Rule 51 Reset**: `PolarPpiStreamProcessor.reset()` SHALL clear monotonic timestamp/reference/counter state in BOTH `unregisterSensors()` and `onAccumulatorsReset()`.
2. **`REQ-HRV-051.2` (Decoupled Verity Sense PPI Lifecycle, Atomic Re-Entrancy Guard & PMD Stability)**:
   - In `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt` (`<= 1,310/1,500` LoC), PPI streaming SHALL activate ONLY for optical PPI-capable devices (`isVeritySense == true`) when `FEATURE_POLAR_ONLINE_STREAMING` is ready AND `api.getAvailableOnlineStreamDataTypes(sessionId).contains(PolarBleApi.PolarDeviceDataType.PPI)`. H10/ECG chest straps (`isVeritySense == false`) SHALL NEVER probe or start PPI streaming.
   - **Decoupled Startup Paths**: PPI launch SHALL execute independently across all 3 readiness hooks (`bleSdkFeatureReady(FEATURE_POLAR_ONLINE_STREAMING)`, `syncAlreadyOpenSessionIfReady()`, and `maybeStartVerityStreamsIfReady()` after DIS model or `FEATURE_POLAR_OFFLINE_RECORDING`) — NEVER nested in `else if` behind `accJob`/`gyroJob` or gated on `isGyroStreamingActive`.
   - **Atomic Pre-Launch Guard & Clean Teardown**: `startPpiCollection(api)` SHALL claim `isPpiStreamingActive.compareAndSet(false, true)` **before** `scope.launch` (resetting in `finally` on exit/cancellation/failure) to prevent duplicate `startPpiStreaming()` / PMD `ERROR_ALREADY_IN_STATE` races during rapid readiness callbacks. `cancelActiveStreamJobs()`, `deviceDisconnected`, `blePowerStateChanged(false)`, and `shutDown()` SHALL cancel `ppiJob`, reset `isPpiStreamingActive`, and in `shutDown()` invoke `api.stopStreaming(streamId, PmdMeasurementType.PPI)` inside `runCatching` without disrupting `HR`/`ACC`/`GYRO` or `PolarVeritySenseOfflineController` offline `HR` recording.
3. **`REQ-HRV-051.3` (Dual Live Workout `DFA alpha-1` Isolation & Optical Signal Quality Mode)**:
   - In `HrvRollingEngine.evaluate(nowMs)` (shared by both global `HRVMetricsDevice` and per-device `PerDeviceHrvController`), compute `val activeWindowHasOpticalPpi = allBeats.any { it.beatTimestampMs >= dfaCutoff && it.isOpticalPpi }`.
   - When `activeWindowHasOpticalPpi == true`, `alpha1Value` SHALL be suppressed to `null` (so both `"Bester Sensor"` `HRVMetricsDevice` and pinned `[Polar Verity Sense]` `HRV_DFA_A1` tiles emit `null` / `--.--`), while `rmssdValue`, `sdnnValue`, and `artifactRate` continue evaluating normally and `HrvSignalQualityAnalyzer.analyzeBeats(...)` receives `isOpticalPpi = activeWindowHasOpticalPpi` (bypassing ECG-only `SMOOTHED_STREAM`/`QUANTIZED_SYNTHETIC` false warnings while enforcing `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` and `HIGH_ARTIFACTS > 5%`).
   - Suppression SHALL be **non-sticky**: switching to an ECG chest strap (`isOpticalPpi = false`) automatically resumes `HRV_DFA_A1` once optical PPI beats age out of the active `120 s` window or after `resetSession()`.
4. **`REQ-HRV-051.4` (`WorkoutRr.db` & Post-Workout `HrvPostWorkoutRecomputer` Optical Isolation)**:
   - `TrackerService.mRrBeatListener.onRrBeat(event)` (`TrackerService.java:293`) SHALL ignore optical PPI beats via a 1-line in-place guard (`if (!event.isOpticalPpi() && mAutoPauseManager.getCurrentState() == AutoPauseState.TRACKING && mWorkoutID > 0)` — **`+0` net LoC**, keeping `TrackerService.java` at `3,538/3,559`), ensuring optical PPI beats are never persisted into `WorkoutRr.db` and `HrvPostWorkoutRecomputer` never computes or plots optical `HRV_DFA_A1` curves on Verity-only workouts while ECG/ANT+ streams remain completely untouched.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)
- *Given* a Polar Verity Sense connected via `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` reporting `PolarDeviceDataType.PPI` support, *When* valid `PolarPpiSample` packets arrive (`ppi > 0`, `blockerBit == false`, and `skinContactStatus == true` when `skinContactSupported == true`), *Then* monotonically timestamped `RrBeatEvent(..., isOpticalPpi = true)` events are dispatched to `perDeviceHrvController` and `RrBeatListener`s alongside `HR`/`ACC`/`GYRO`, and `mRrIntervalSensor` receives clean intervals.
- *Given* `PolarPpiSample` packets with `ppi <= 0`, `blockerBit == true`, or `(skinContactSupported && !skinContactStatus)`, *When* processed by `PolarPpiStreamProcessor` and `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge`, *Then* `ppi <= 0` samples are dropped, hardware-flagged samples are stamped `ARTIFACT_GAP` (`gapBeats = 0` when physiological, `isOpticalPpi = true`), never update `lastNormalRrMs` or `mRrIntervalSensor`, never enter clean `rMSSD` pairs in `RrArtifactFilter`/`HrvRollingEngine`/`MorningHrvCheckViewModel`, and count toward `artifactRatePercent` (`HIGH_ARTIFACTS > 5%`).
- *Given* `HrvRollingEngine` / `HRVMetricsDevice` / `PerDeviceHrvController` receives `isOpticalPpi = true` beats, *When* periodic HRV metrics evaluate, *Then* `HRV_DFA_A1` emits `null` while `HRV_RMSSD`/`HRV_SDNN` and optical signal quality evaluate normally; when ECG beats (`isOpticalPpi = false`) replace the active `120 s` window, `HRV_DFA_A1` resumes automatically.
- *Given* a Polar Verity Sense disconnects, reconnects, receives rapid duplicate readiness callbacks, or shuts down (or a Polar H10 connects), *When* `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` manages streams, *Then* H10 never launches PPI, Verity launches PPI independently of `ACC`/`GYRO` with atomic `compareAndSet` re-entrancy protection (zero `ERROR_ALREADY_IN_STATE`), `ppiJob` cancels cleanly, `stopStreaming(PPI)` runs on `shutDown()`, and `PolarPpiStreamProcessor` resets on both `unregisterSensors()` and `onAccumulatorsReset()`.
- *Given* an active workout (`AutoPauseState.TRACKING`, `mWorkoutID > 0`) receiving optical PPI vs ECG beats, *When* `TrackerService.mRrBeatListener` runs, *Then* optical PPI beats are never queued into `WorkoutRr.db` (preventing optical `HrvPostWorkoutRecomputer` `DFA alpha-1` curves), whereas ECG beats persist normally.

## 4. Test Specification (`TST-HRV-051`)
- **`TST-HRV-051.1` (`PolarPpiStreamProcessorTest.kt` — Pure-JVM Filtering, Timestamps & Reset)**: Verify valid PPI samples reconstruct monotonic UTC timestamps with `isOpticalPpi = true` & `ARTIFACT_NORMAL`; verify `ppi <= 0` samples are dropped; verify `blockerBit == true` and `(skinContactSupported && !skinContactStatus)` samples are stamped `ARTIFACT_GAP` (`gapBeats = 0` when physiological, `isOpticalPpi = true`), never mutate `lastNormalRrMs`, are preserved as artifacts through `RrArtifactFilter.filterLiveCausal` & `filterBidirectionalBeats`, and never enter clean `rMSSD` pairs; verify `skinContactSupported == false` with `skinContactStatus == false` is NOT rejected; verify `reset()` clears timestamp/reference/counter state.
- **`TST-HRV-051.2` (`PolarOnlineStreamingBridgeTest.kt` — Decoupled Lifecycle, H10 Guard, Re-Entrancy & `mRrIntervalSensor`)**: Verify Verity Sense probes `getAvailableOnlineStreamDataTypes` and launches `startPpiStreaming` independently of `accJob`/`gyroJob` across all 3 readiness paths (`bleSdkFeatureReady`, `syncAlreadyOpenSessionIfReady`, late DIS/offline feature identification); verify H10 (`isVeritySense == false`) NEVER calls `startPpiStreaming` or `stopStreaming(PPI)`; verify rapid duplicate readiness callbacks trigger only ONE `startPpiStreaming` call via atomic `compareAndSet`; verify `ARTIFACT_GAP` optical beats dispatch to `RrBeatListener`/`perDeviceHrvController` WITHOUT updating `mRrIntervalSensor`; verify `deviceDisconnected`, `blePowerStateChanged(false)`, `onAccumulatorsReset()`, and `shutDown()` cancel `ppiJob`, reset `PolarPpiStreamProcessor`, and invoke `stopStreaming(id, PmdMeasurementType.PPI)` on `shutDown()` without disrupting `HR`/`ACC`/`GYRO`.
- **`TST-HRV-051.3` (`HrvRollingEngineTest.kt`, `HRVMetricsDeviceTest.kt`, `PerDeviceHrvControllerTest.kt`, `HrvGoldenOracleTest.kt` — Live `DFA alpha-1` Isolation)**: Verify optical PPI beats suppress `HRV_DFA_A1` to `null` in `HrvRollingEngine`, global `HRVMetricsDevice`, and pinned `PerDeviceHrvController` while computing `HRV_RMSSD`, `HRV_SDNN`, `HRV_ARTIFACT_RATE`, and optical `HrvSignalQualityAnalyzer` status; verify non-sticky recovery when ECG beats (`isOpticalPpi = false`) replace the active `120 s` window; verify `HrvGoldenOracleTest` remains bit-identical.
- **`TST-HRV-051.4` (`TrackerServiceRrOpticalIsolationTest.kt` + Contract Suites — `WorkoutRr.db` Isolation & Governance)**: Verify `TrackerService.mRrBeatListener` drops `isOpticalPpi == true` beats during `AutoPauseState.TRACKING` (`mWorkoutID > 0`) while queuing ECG beats (`isOpticalPpi == false`) unchanged into `WorkoutRr.db`; verify `ArchitectureConformanceContractTest` (`DeviceManager.java` `1,707/1,708`, `DevicesDatabaseManager.java` `1,669/1,671`, `TrackerService.java` `3,538/3,559`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt <= 1,500`), `SystemLiveTrackingRequirementsContractTest`, `DeadCodeEliminationContractTest`, `InterfaceSignatureDriftContractTest`, and `SourceTextReadingAuditContractTest`.
