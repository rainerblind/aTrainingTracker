## 1. Formal Requirement Specification (`REQ-FIL-046`)

1. **Tier-4 Auto-Resolved `BSportType` Propagation & Anti-Circular Isolation (`REQ-FIL-046.1`)**:
   - `BANALService` SHALL maintain volatile `mResolvedBSportType` (default `BSportType.UNKNOWN`, accepting only `BIKE` or `RUN`, else `UNKNOWN`) via `setResolvedBSportType(@NonNull BSportType)` (exposed on `BANALService`, `BANALServiceComm`, and static `updateActiveResolvedBSportType`), cleared on `clearInferredSportTelemetry()` (`RESET_ACCUMULATORS_INTENT`, `onDestroy()`, `resetOriginAndEkf()`), with zero per-sample SQLite lookups and zero `SPORT_TYPE_CHANGED_BY_USER_INTENT` broadcasts.
   - `BANALService` SHALL expose `getEffectiveBSportType()` / static `getEffectiveActiveBSportType()` enforcing strict precedence: Tier 1 `mHaveUserSelectedSportType` (`mUserSelectedBSportType`, preserving explicit `OTHER`/`UNKNOWN` locks) > Tier 1.5 `mMultisportBSportType` > Tier 2 `DeviceManager.getSportType()` > Tier 3 `mInferredBSportType` > Tier 4 `mResolvedBSportType` > `BSportType.UNKNOWN`, while leaving `getAuthoritativeBSportType()` (`REQ-FIL-030.2`) and `getBSportType()` (`REQ-FIL-030.3`) completely unchanged.
   - `TrackerService` SHALL propagate its resolved `BSportType` (`getSportTypesRepository().getBSportType(sportTypeId)`) to `BANALService` whenever `createNewWorkout()`, `onSearchingFinished()`, or `applyResumedState()` resolves session sport state (alongside `SmartphoneRawTelemetryRecorder.updateSportState()`).
   - `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator` SHALL gate `BIKE_*` carriage-mode cycling votes (`+1.2 * dt`) on `speedMps >= MIN_BIKE_CARRIAGE_VOTE_SPEED_MPS (1.8 m/s)` so walking-speed `BIKE_FRONT_POCKET` flickers never latch Tier 3 `BSportType.BIKE` over Tier 4 `RUN`, while preserving steep `>= 2.0 m/s` uphill MTB climbs (`REQ-FIL-030.1`).
2. **Sustained Unclassified Walking Fallback & Strict Cycling/`OTHER` Guardrails (`REQ-FIL-046.2`)**:
   - `SmartphoneImuCadenceEstimator` SHALL map `WALKING_TRANSITION` to `ModeCategory.RUNNING_STEP` immediately when `sportContext == SportContextHint.RUNNING`, and when `sportContext == SportContextHint.UNKNOWN` strictly after `UNKNOWN_WALK_CONFIRM_DWELL_MS = 25_000L` of continuous monotonic `WALKING_TRANSITION` (resetting the dwell timer on any non-walking mode prior to confirmation, on `BIKE_*`/`UNKNOWN` after confirmation, on backwards timestamp regression, on `sportContext != UNKNOWN`, and in `reset()`/`suppressAll()`).
   - `WALKING_TRANSITION` under `SportContextHint.CYCLING` or `SportContextHint.OTHER` MUST remain strictly `ModeCategory.SUPPRESSED` (`null` cadence, `0` strides). In `PhoneCarriageMode.STATIONARY`, `resolveCategory()` SHALL suppress immediately if `sportContext` contradicts `activeCategory` (`CYCLING` vs `RUNNING_*`, `RUNNING` vs `BIKE_*`, or `OTHER`).
   - `SmartphoneImuCadenceEstimator` SHALL expose lock-free `@Volatile var isRunningOrWalkingActive: Boolean` (`true` iff `activeCategory == RUNNING_STEP || activeCategory == RUNNING_ARM`), functioning identically with hardware `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR` or permission-free 50-Hz `ACC` `1.2–4.0 Hz` impact bandpass fallback.
3. **`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` Effective Sport Sync & Stride Gating (`REQ-FIL-046.3`)**:
   - `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.resolveActiveBSportType()` SHALL consume `BANALService.getEffectiveBSportType()` / `getEffectiveActiveBSportType()`. `resolveSportContextHint()` SHALL map user-locked `BSportType.UNKNOWN` (`isUserSportLocked() == true`) to `SportContextHint.OTHER` while unselected `UNKNOWN` maps to `SportContextHint.UNKNOWN`.
   - On 1-Hz `updateCarriageClassification()` ticks, `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` SHALL detect changes in `resolveSportContextHint()` (`mLastObservedSportHint`, reset in `resetOriginAndEkf()`) and trigger `handleSportTypeChanged(timestampMs)` so Tier 4 updates activate `CADENCE` & `STRIDES` within `<= 1.0 s`.
   - `applyCadenceAndStrideSensors()` and `handleSportTypeChanged()` SHALL allow `SensorType.STRIDES` registration/accumulation iff `sportHint == RUNNING || (sportHint == UNKNOWN && cadenceEstimator.isRunningOrWalkingActive)`, immediately unregistering `STRIDES` on `CYCLING` or `OTHER` while preserving non-retroactive stride baseline synchronization (`TST-FIL-029.3`) and monolith LoC caps (`TrackerService <= 3,220`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused <= 1,500`, `BANALService <= 1,500`, `SmartphoneImuCadenceEstimator <= 1,500`).

## 2. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)

- **AC1 (Phone-only walk records continuous cadence & strides)**: *Given* a phone-only walk (`2026-11-23_093732` or `2026-11-23_171814`, `sportId = 4 Spaziergang`, `WALKING_TRANSITION`, no external footpod, with or without `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR`), *When* processed by `TrackerService`/`BANALService`/`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused`/`SmartphoneImuCadenceEstimator`, *Then* walking `CADENCE` (`~45–60 strides/min`) and cumulative `STRIDES` emit continuously across `> 80%` of active walking.
- **AC2 (Zero-paired-sensor unselected walk & Tier-3 bike override)**: *Given* an unselected walk with `mResolvedBSportType == UNKNOWN` in continuous `WALKING_TRANSITION` (`>= 25.0 s`), *When* processed without `TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR`, *Then* `CADENCE` and `STRIDES` activate cleanly; if the athlete subsequently rides (`BIKE`), Tier 3 `BIKE` overrides Tier 4 and immediately unregisters `STRIDES`.
- **AC3 (Cycling bakery stop & explicit `OTHER` suppression)**: *Given* an active cycling session (`SportContextHint.CYCLING`, e.g., `2026-11-22_081402` bakery stop + `< 25 s` pre-ride driveway bike-pushing) or explicit user-selected `OTHER` (`mHaveUserSelectedSportType && UNKNOWN`), *When* entering `WALKING_TRANSITION`, *Then* `ModeCategory.SUPPRESSED` holds and `0` walking strides are emitted.
