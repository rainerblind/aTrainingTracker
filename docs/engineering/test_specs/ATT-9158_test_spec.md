### 1.2 Functional & Architectural Requirements
1. **Corroborated Cold-Start Pinning Release, Unbypassable Veto Precedence & Anti-Oscillation Guard (`REQ-FIL-036.1`)**:
   - `SpeedAndLocationDevice.java` **SHALL** expose a minimal `protected boolean shouldReleaseColdStartStationaryPin(Location location)` hook returning `false` by default (keeping `SpeedAndLocationDevice_GPS` and `SpeedAndLocationDevice_GoogleFused` 100% behaviorally identical). In `onNewLocation()`, this hook **MUST** be evaluated strictly **AFTER** `if (mIsIndoorMode)` and strictly **BEFORE** `if (shouldUnpin && (extStationary || accelAtRest)) { shouldUnpin = false; }`, guaranteeing that indoor mode, stationary external wheel/footpod sensors, and IMU rest-variance vetoes remain structurally unbypassable.
   - Pure helper `AttFusedColdStartHelper` (`com.atrainingtracker.banalservice.odometry`, keeping `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt <= 1,485 / 1,500` LoC) **SHALL** evaluate cold-start pin release for `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` under coarse initial GNSS accuracy (`10–20 m`) ONLY when BOTH hold:
     (a) `>= 2` consecutive fresh corroborated motion fixes (`hasTrustedGpsSpeed && gpsSpeed >= 0.8 m/s`, OR `isWheelMoving`, OR active running/walking carriage/step evidence) with `!isStationaryByClassifier`, AND
     (b) net directed raw-GNSS progression (non-oscillating: net horizontal displacement from the start of the motion window `>= 3.0 m` AND straightness ratio `netDist / pathDist >= 0.55` over `>= 2` moving intervals), explicitly rejecting indoor handheld oscillating multipath fixes that bounce back and forth without directed progress.
   - When cold-start release triggers on sustained outdoor movement (`1.0–2.3 m/s`, `acc = 9.2–18.9 m`), `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` **SHALL** release standstill pinning promptly (`<= 5 s` instead of freezing `20+ s`), publish non-zero speed and progressive coordinates/distance, and avoid `10+ m` unpin teleportation or geometric `calcSpeed` spikes.
2. **Cold-Start Raw-Chord Heading Seeding, Unanchored Velocity-Crush & Post-Outage Snap Decoupling, and Bounded Doppler Speed Variance (`REQ-FIL-036.2`)**:
   - While EKF heading has not yet been anchored by Doppler or chord updates, `AttFusedColdStartHelper` **SHALL** seed `UnicycleOdometryEkf` heading via `updateOfflineHeading()` as soon as directed raw-GNSS displacement reaches `>= 4.0 m` (or trusted moving GPS bearing at `gpsSpeed >= 1.0 m/s` corroborated by non-stationary motion) even when `speed < MIN_DOPPLER_SPEED_MPS (3.0 m/s)` and `acc <= MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS (20.0 m)`, preventing hardcoded North (`PI/2`) dead-reckoning against South/East/West starts.
   - `UnicycleOdometryEkf.updateGnssPosition()` **SHALL** support `@JvmOverloads decoupleVelocity: Boolean = false` and **MUST** zero `k[2][0..1]` and decouple `P[0..1, 2]` whenever (a) `decoupleVelocity == true` (e.g., while live heading is still unanchored or during ERTS discontinuity steps) OR (b) `confirmedPostOutage` inflates `p[0][0] = max(p[0][0], y0^2); p[1][1] = max(p[1][1], y1^2)`, preventing both unanchored-heading velocity crushing (`K[2] · y < 0`) and post-outage snap velocity spikes while preserving `TST-FIL-023.1` & `REQ-FIL-009` tunnel recovery.
   - For trusted GNSS fixes (`acc > 0.0 && acc <= UnicycleOdometryEkf.MAX_GNSS_ACCURACY_METERS`), Doppler `speedVar` in both `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` and `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother` **SHALL** use a calibrated bounded variance (`((acc * 0.08)^2).coerceIn(0.25, 2.25)` instead of unbounded `(acc * 0.25)^2 = 22.3 (m/s)^2` at `18.9 m`), while preserving full distrust when `acc > 20.0 m` (`testDopplerDropoutReplay_maintainsContinuousSpeed`).
3. **Post-Workout ERTS Speed-Before-Position Ordering, `dt_gnss`-Normalized Teleport Decoupling, 1-Tick Glitch Pre-Gate & Backward Catch-Up Velocity Bound (`REQ-FIL-036.3`)**:
   - In `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.smoothSamples()`, moving speed updates (`validWheelSpeed` / `validSpeed`) **SHALL** execute **BEFORE** `ekf.updateGnssPosition()` (matching `REQ-FIL-023.1` live ordering) with calibrated bounded Doppler variance for trusted fixes (`acc <= 20.0 m`).
   - `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.smoothSamples()` **SHALL** pre-gate isolated 1-tick geometric speed glitches (an uncorroborated non-wheel spike exceeding `max(2.0 * localMedianSpeed, localMedianSpeed + 2.5)` bounded by normal neighbors) without damping genuine multi-second sprint accelerations (`2.0 -> 3.5 -> 5.0 -> 6.0 m/s`).
   - `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother` **SHALL** detect GNSS step discontinuities normalized by actual valid-GNSS elapsed time `dt_gnss` (`dt_gnss in (0.0, 5.0]` and `stepDist / dt_gnss > max(6.0, localSpeed * 2.2 + 2.5)`, so `45 s` curved tunnels with large `dt_gnss` never false-trigger). Across any detected discontinuity epoch (including post-stationary unpin teleports), BOTH forward `updateGnssPosition(..., decoupleVelocity = true)` AND backward RTS (`smoothSnapshots()` via `copyAndDecoupleVelocityPosition` on `discontinuityMask`) **SHALL** decouple `[0..1, 2]` position-velocity covariance, and backward RTS velocity `smoothedStates[k][2]` **SHALL** be bounded against artificial position-driven catch-up overshoot above the local moving speed envelope so canonical `SPEED_mps` and `ExtremumValues.MAX(SPEED_mps)` remain free of synthetic `5.73 m/s` spikes while preserving genuine sprints and accurate workout `DISTANCE_m`.

### 1.3 Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)
* **AC-1 (`REQ-FIL-036.1`, `REQ-FIL-036.2`)**: *Given* an outdoor running start (`1.0–2.3 m/s`, heading South/East/West) where raw GPS has cold-start accuracy (`9.2–18.9 m`) and active running IMU motion/steps (`!mIsIndoorMode`, `!extStationary`, `!accelAtRest`), *When* `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` processes the first `30 s`, *Then* standstill pinning releases within `<= 5 s` (not freezing for `20+ s`), heading seeds from directed raw-GNSS progression without crushing speed, no `>= 10 m` teleport jump occurs upon release, and recorded distance tracks raw GPS progression within tight tolerance.
* **AC-2 (`REQ-FIL-036.1`)**: *Given* (a) indoor mode active (`mIsIndoorMode == true`), (b) stationary external wheel sensor (`0.0 m/s`), (c) motionless IMU (`accelAtRest == true`), (d) cold stationary `25.1 m -> 16.0 m` convergence at `0.0 m/s`, OR (e) indoor handheld oscillating multipath fixes (`acc = 12–18 m`, fake `1.2–1.8 m/s` Doppler bouncing back and forth around an anchor with low straightness), *When* `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` processes the fixes, *Then* stationary pinning MUST remain engaged (`0.0 m/s`, `0.0 m` distance creep, zero indoor spiderweb release).
* **AC-3 (`REQ-FIL-036.3`)**: *Given* a recorded track containing `23 s` of pinned standstill followed by `9.34 m` (`t = 24 s`) and `12.20 m` (`t = 31 s`) unpin teleports (with or without an isolated 1-tick geometric speed glitch while normal live speeds are `1.8–2.5 m/s`, `< 3.3 m/s`), *When* `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother` / `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer` recomputes canonical columns and `ExtremumValues`, *Then* smoothed `SPEED_mps` and `ExtremumValues.MAX(SPEED_mps)` stay bounded (`< 3.5 m/s`, eliminating the `5.73 m/s` catch-up overshoot) and raw device-attributed columns remain untouched.
* **AC-4 (`REQ-FIL-036.2`, `REQ-FIL-036.3`)**: *Given* (a) a genuine multi-second sprint acceleration (`2.0 -> 3.5 -> 5.0 -> 6.0 m/s` over `6 s` with matching GPS displacement), (b) a `45 s` curved tunnel GNSS outage (`testCurvedTunnelOutageReplay_eliminatesExitStepDiscontinuity`), and (c) an `acc > 20 m` Doppler dropout (`testDopplerDropoutReplay_maintainsContinuousSpeed`), *When* `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother` / `UnicycleOdometryEkf` runs, *Then* the genuine sprint peaks accurately (`>= 5.5 m/s`), tunnel exit continuity holds with zero false discontinuity trigger, and coarse `acc > 20 m` fixes remain distrusted.
