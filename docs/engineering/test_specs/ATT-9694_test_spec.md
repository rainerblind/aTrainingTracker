## 1. Formal Requirement Specification (`REQ-FIL-047`)

1. **Bounded Pure-JVM `SportSpeedPriors` & Off-Main-Thread `SportTypes` Loading (`REQ-FIL-047.1`)**:
   - Define immutable pure-JVM value object `SportSpeedPriors(maxWalkSpeedMps, minBikeSpeedMps, maxRunSpeedMps)` (defaults: `2.0`, `2.5`, `5.5` m/s; zero Android/SQLite imports) clamping `maxWalkSpeedMps` to `[1.5, 2.5]`, `minBikeSpeedMps` to `[1.8, 3.5]`, and `maxRunSpeedMps` to `[3.5, 6.5]` m/s (non-finite inputs fall back to defaults).
   - `SportSpeedPriors.fromBands(bands)` SHALL ignore placeholder/invalid rows (`!minSpeed.isFinite() || !maxSpeed.isFinite() || maxSpeed <= 0.0 || minSpeed < 0.0 || maxSpeed < minSpeed`) and derive: `maxWalkSpeedMps` from the highest `maxSpeed` among walking `RUN` bands (`(minSpeed <= 1.0 && maxSpeed <= 2.8)` or walk/hike `gcName`/`stravaName`/`tcxName`), `minBikeSpeedMps` from the lowest positive `minSpeed` (`minSpeed > 0.0 && maxSpeed > minSpeed`) among `BIKE` bands, and `maxRunSpeedMps` from the highest `maxSpeed` among `RUN` bands, falling back to canonical defaults per missing dimension.
   - `SportTypeDatabaseManager` SHALL expose read-only `getActiveSportTypeSpeedBands()` (`IS_PLACEHOLDER = 0`, try-with-resources `Cursor`, zero raw `Cursor` leaks). `SportSpeedPriorsLoader.loadAsync(context, onLoaded)` SHALL execute off the main thread on Android/Robolectric (`Build.VERSION.SDK_INT > 0`), silently falling back to `SportSpeedPriors.DEFAULT` on error, and MUST synchronously no-op without launching background threads when `Build.VERSION.SDK_INT == 0` (`Coding Standards §5.5`). `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` SHALL trigger async loading on `init` and `onAccumulatorsReset()` (skipping stale callbacks when `mIsShuttingDown`), applying priors to `carriageClassifier`, `sportDiscriminator`, and `multisportDetector` with zero per-sample SQLite lookups and staying `<= 1,500` LoC.
2. **Low-Speed `BIKE_*` Suppression & Bounded Priors in `PhoneCarriageClassifier` & `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator` (`REQ-FIL-047.2`)**:
   - `PhoneCarriageClassifier` SHALL accept `@Synchronized fun setSportSpeedPriors(priors: SportSpeedPriors)` (preserved across `reset()`). When `!wheelActiveMoving` and `effectiveSpeed != null`:
     - Compute `bikeLowSpeedFloorMps`: `1.8 m/s` (`MIN_BIKE_SPEED_FLOOR_MPS`) if `confirmedNoFootstrikes && (committedMode.isCyclingMode() || sportHint == SportContextHint.CYCLING)`; else `minOf(priors.minBikeSpeedMps, maxOf(1.8, priors.maxWalkSpeedMps))` if `confirmedNoFootstrikes`; else `priors.minBikeSpeedMps`.
     - Suppress `BIKE_*` (`BIKE_HANDLEBAR`, `BIKE_JERSEY_BACK`, `BIKE_FRONT_POCKET`) whenever `!wheelActiveMoving && effectiveSpeed < bikeLowSpeedFloorMps`; when suppressed without `hasActiveRunningCadence` or `easyRunSuppressWalk`, resolve directly to `PhoneCarriageMode.WALKING_TRANSITION` (preventing all-`-Infinity` `RUN_WAIST_HIP` fallthrough). Raise `suppressWalkForNoFootstrikeClimb` threshold from `1.6 m/s` to `bikeLowSpeedFloorMps` (`2.0 m/s` default), preserving `2.0 m/s` steep uphill MTB (`TST-FIL-034.4`) and `1.8 m/s` established cycling climbs.
     - Derive `runCyclingMaskSpeedMps = maxOf(5.5, priors.maxRunSpeedMps)` so workout-average `RUN` settings (`4.0 m/s`) never break `5.0 m/s` downhill sprints or `TST-FIL-035.1` while honoring higher configured running ceilings up to `6.5 m/s`.
   - `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator` SHALL accept `@JvmOverloads` `sportSpeedPriors` + `@Synchronized fun setSportSpeedPriors(priors)` (preserved across `reset()`), gating `BIKE_*` carriage votes on `speedMps >= minOf(priors.minBikeSpeedMps, maxOf(1.8, priors.maxWalkSpeedMps))` and using `highBikeSpeedMps = priors.maxRunSpeedMps.coerceIn(5.0, 6.5)`.
3. **Symmetric Sliding-Window `BIKE` Guardrails in `MultisportTransitionDetector` (`REQ-FIL-047.3`)**:
   - `MultisportTransitionDetector` SHALL accept `@JvmOverloads` `sportSpeedPriors: SportSpeedPriors = SportSpeedPriors.DEFAULT` + `@Synchronized fun setSportSpeedPriors(priors)` (preserved across `reset()`).
   - Phase 1 (`establishedSport = BIKE` over `establishWindowMs`) and Phase 2 (`RUN -> BIKE` over `confirmationWindowMs`) SHALL evaluate a bounded active-unpaused sliding window (`evaluateBikeWindow`, symmetric with `evaluateRunWindow`): bypassed iff `hardwareSignals.hasActiveBikeHardware == true || spanMs <= 0L`; otherwise requiring `windowActiveMs >= spanMs`, `windowActiveSec > 0.0`, finite `windowDistM >= priors.minBikeSpeedMps * (spanMs / 1000.0)`, and `windowAvgSpeedMps >= priors.minBikeSpeedMps`.
   - Phase 2 `RUN -> BIKE` SHALL advance `enterAnchor` to the qualifying sliding-window head while keeping `exitAnchor` intact (preserving pause immunity, slow-start recovery, and phone-only duathlon `RUN -> T1 -> BIKE` carryover with zero phantom `BIKE` locks on walking speeds `0.2–2.4 m/s`).

- **AC3 (Genuine cycling & triathlon `T1`/`T2` preserved)**: *Given* cycling errand `2026-11-22_081402`, a steep uphill MTB climb (`2.0–2.2 m/s` with `confirmedNoFootstrikes == true` or active bike sensor, or `1.8 m/s` established cycling climb), and triathlon `RUN -> T1 -> BIKE` (`>= 4.5 m/s` for `15 s`), *When* processed, *Then* cycling carriage modes, `BIKE` establishment, and `T1`/`T2` transitions classify accurately without regressions.
