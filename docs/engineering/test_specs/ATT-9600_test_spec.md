## 1. Requirements Summary (`REQ-FIL-042` — Priority: `High`, Type: `Functional`, Verification: `Unit`)

| Sub-ID | Normative Contract (SHALL / MUST) |
|---|---|
| `REQ-FIL-042.1` | `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` SHALL unify all internal telemetry consumers (`UnicycleOdometryEkf`, `PhoneCarriageClassifier`, `KinematicSportTypeDiscriminator`, `AttFusedColdStartHelper`, `SmartphoneImuCadenceEstimator`, chord/displacement buffers, and wheel freshness checks) onto Android's monotonic `elapsedRealtime` millisecond clock via `AttFusedTimestampNormalizer`. GNSS `Location` timestamps SHALL derive from `location.elapsedRealtimeNanos / 1_000_000L` (ignoring `location.time` when `elapsedRealtimeNanos > 0L`), falling back cleanly to platform `elapsedRealtime` (or synthetic `< 1.5e12` / plain-JVM `location.time` when `elapsedRealtimeNanos <= 0L`). External UTC boundaries (`MultisportTelemetryAnchor.timestampMs` via NTP-immune `toWallClockEpochMs()`, `SmartphoneRawTelemetryRecorder` `epochMs`) MUST remain UTC wall-clock. |
| `REQ-FIL-042.2` | `AttFusedTimestampNormalizer` SHALL normalize `SensorEvent` (`ACC`, `GYRO`, `ROTVEC`, `BARO`, `STEP`) and external `Wheel` timestamps onto the same monotonic `elapsedRealtime` domain across all Android OEM HALs: (a) standard `CLOCK_BOOTTIME` sensors (`|sensorMs - elapsedMs| <= 5_000 ms`) SHALL use `event.timestamp / 1_000_000L` directly (`offset = 0`, preserving CHRE/FIFO `dt`); (b) divergent OEM HALs (`uptimeMillis` or epoch nanoseconds with `> 5_000 ms` offset from `SystemClock.elapsedRealtimeNanos()`) SHALL latch and drift-reanchor per-sensor-type offsets onto `elapsedRealtime` without freezing under paused Robolectric clocks; (c) wall-clock wheel callbacks (`>= 1.5e12` while platform monotonic is active) SHALL map onto `elapsedRealtime`; and (d) plain-JVM tests where `SystemClock` is unmocked SHALL safely degrade to `currentTimeMillis()` with zero `RuntimeException`. |
| `REQ-FIL-042.3` | `PhoneCarriageClassifier.classify(nowMs)` SHALL enforce a defensive monotonic guard clamping `effectiveNowMs = max(nowMs, lastEventTimestampMs)` so lagged or out-of-order GNSS/CHRE callbacks (`nowMs < lastEventTimestampMs`) NEVER seed `candidateSinceMs` in the past or bypass `dismountHysteresisMs` (`2.0 s`) / `mountHysteresisMs` (`4.0 s`), and `ingestGnssSpeed()` SHALL ignore older out-of-order GNSS speed/bearing timestamps. `UnicycleOdometryEkf` SHALL keep `currentTimestampMs` monotonically non-decreasing across interleaved IMU/GNSS updates, and `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt` MUST stay strictly `<= 1,500` LoC. |

## 3. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)

- **Given** GPS `Location.getTime()` lags `System.currentTimeMillis()` by `2.18 s` while `Location.getElapsedRealtimeNanos()` matches `SystemClock.elapsedRealtimeNanos()` within `< 200 ms`, **When** `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` processes interleaved 50-Hz IMU and 1-Hz GNSS callbacks, **Then** `PhoneCarriageClassifier` enforces the full `dismountHysteresisMs` (`2.0 s`) / `mountHysteresisMs` (`4.0 s`) dwell windows with zero sub-`2 s` `4 <-> 1` mode chatter and zero backward `-2.18 s` timestamp sawtooths.
- **Given** an Android device whose `SensorEvent.timestamp` uses `uptimeMillis` or epoch nanoseconds (`> 5 s` offset from `SystemClock.elapsedRealtimeNanos()`), **When** IMU, wheel, and GNSS events arrive concurrently (including after deep sleep or NTP clock jumps), **Then** `AttFusedTimestampNormalizer` maps all streams cleanly onto `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()` without breaking `dt` or sliding windows.
- **Given** a unit/replay test reproducing the `t = 4..20 s` window from `2026-11-22_081402.phone_raw.csv.gz` (`-2.18 s` GNSS offset) and out-of-order `PhoneCarriageClassifier.classify()` calls, **When** the test suite runs, **Then** zero sub-`2 s` mode-chatter flips occur and all existing `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused`, `BANALService` Robolectric integration, `PhoneCarriageClassifier`, `UnicycleOdometryEkf`, and replay harness tests pass.
