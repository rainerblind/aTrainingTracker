## 1. Requirement Specification (`REQ-FIL-027` `[Planned]`)
* **Attributes**: Priority: `High` | Type: `Functional` | Verification Method: `Unit` | System Requirement(s): `SYSREQ-TRK-003`, `SYSREQ-TRK-004`, `SYSREQ-TRK-005`
* **Implementation**: `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.kt`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused.kt`, `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.kt`, `UnicycleOdometryEkf.kt`, `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.kt`
* **`REQ-FIL-027.1` (Monotonic `TIME_TOTAL` Timestamp Reconstruction)**: `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.readWorkoutSamples()` SHALL reconstruct monotonic `timestampMs` via `SensorType.TIME_TOTAL` anchored to the first valid `time` epoch (with `max(parsedTs, prevTs + 1000L)` fallback when `TIME_TOTAL` is absent/null/non-increasing, preserving pause gaps) BEFORE `BarometricAltitudeFilter.filterSlewOnly()` and ERTS smoothing, ensuring `0` `dt = 0 s / 2 s` quantization, `0` `m/h` `VERTICAL_SPEED` sawtooth drops, and full `useAntiAliasedYaw` activation.
* **`REQ-FIL-027.2` (Rolling 1.0-s Time-Weighted Mean `WORLD_YAW_RATE_radps`)**: `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` SHALL publish a rolling `1.0 s` time-weighted mean ($\sum \omega_i \Delta t_i / \sum \Delta t_i$, retaining at least the latest sample) to `mWorldYawRateSensor` across `onSensorChanged()` and `ingestImuMeasurement()`, MUST preserve immediate single-sample emission (`0.1 rad/s`, `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFusedTest:126-139`), and MUST reset cleanly alongside `mLastGyroTimestampNs = 0L` in `resetOriginAndEkf()` and `shutDown()`.
* **`REQ-FIL-027.3` (Stationary Indoor-Stop Pinning & RTS Decoupling)**: `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmoother.smoothSamples()` SHALL classify stationary epochs strictly as `speedMps != null && speedMps <= 0.05 && (wheelSpeedMps == null || wheelSpeedMps <= 0.05)`, invoke `ekf.applyZeroVelocityUpdate()`, pass `isStationary = true` to `ekf.updateGnssPosition()`, decouple velocity/position transition-Jacobian/covariance in `snapshots` / `smoothSnapshots()` backward RTS pass, and clamp `smoothedStates[k][2] = 0.0`, while leaving `UnicycleOdometryEkf.ZUPT_SPEED_VARIANCE = 2.0`, `MIN_DOPPLER_SPEED_MPS = 3.0`, and `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmootherTest:140` (`wheelSpeedMps = 10.0`) intact.
* **`REQ-FIL-027.4` (`DISTANCE_m_LAP` & Summary Stream Subsampling Parity)**: `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer` SHALL add `SensorType.DISTANCE_m_LAP.name` to `ALLOWED_CANONICAL_COLUMNS` and `updateSamplesDatabase()`, resetting per-lap cumulative distance on `LAP_NR` transitions in parity with `Laps.db`; `updateWorkoutSummaries()` SHALL apply `PolyUtil.simplify(validLatLngs, 10.0)` (`2` collinear endpoints instead of `30`/`25` in `TST-FIL-017.3` / `TST-FIL-019.3`) and `WorkoutSummaries.ENCODING_STEP_SIZE` (`20`) subsampling parity (`TrackerService.finalizeLiveSession()`).
* **`REQ-FIL-027.5` (9-Locale Raw Barometer `filterSlewOnly()` & `2.0 m` Hysteresis)**: `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.readWorkoutSamples()` SHALL select raw barometer columns across all 9 locales matching `WorkoutSamplesDatabaseManager.resolveBarometricDeviceNames()` prefixes (pre-filtered via `BarometricAltitudeFilter.filterSlewOnly()`, falling back to `ALTITUDE` only when absent/null), and `ExtendedRtsAltitudeSlopeSmoother.smooth()` SHALL enforce `ASCENT_DESCENT_HYSTERESIS_METERS = 2.0` (`REQ-FIL-020.2`), keeping raw device columns in `WorkoutSamples.db` untouched, `WorkoutRepository.kt <= 5,117` LoC untouched, `0` new `src/main` reads (`SourceTextReadingAuditContractTest`), and `Localization Parity: N/A - no UI strings`.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)
* *Given* `WorkoutSamples.db` with `dt = 0 s / 2 s` batch-flush `time`, monotonic `TIME_TOTAL`, 9-locale barometer altitude (`-8.05/+6.87 m` spikes), `LAP_NR` segments, and an indoor stop (`speed <= 0.05`, `acc = 50 m`), *When* `ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputer.recomputeWorkout()` runs, *Then* `dtSec` reconstructs `1 Hz` (`0` `0 m/h` `VERTICAL_SPEED` drops, full `useAntiAliasedYaw`), raw baro pre-filters via `filterSlewOnly()` + `2.0 m` hysteresis, stop clamps to `0.0 m/s` without collapsing pre-stop speed, `DISTANCE_m_LAP` matches `Laps.db`, `mapPolyline` simplifies via `PolyUtil.simplify(..., 10.0)` (`2` collinear endpoints), and streams subsample at `ENCODING_STEP_SIZE` (`20`).
* *Given* `SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFused` receiving 1 sample (`0.1 rad/s`) vs. `50-Hz` cyclic pedaling oscillations (`1.2–1.5 Hz`), *When* `ingestImuMeasurement()`/`onSensorChanged()`, `resetOriginAndEkf()`, and `shutDown()` run, *Then* sample 1 emits `0.1 rad/s` immediately, cyclic oscillations cancel over `1.0 s`, and reset/shutdown clear all gyro-window state.

* **`TST-FIL-027.2` (`SpeedAndLocationDevice_ATTFusedTest`)**: Immediate 1st-sample `0.1 rad/s` (`:126-139`), rolling `1.0 s` time-weighted mean canceling `1.2–1.5 Hz` thigh-pedaling aliases, and `resetOriginAndEkf()`/`shutDown()` reset.
* **`TST-FIL-027.3` (`ExtendedRtsOdometrySmootherTest`, `UnicycleOdometryEkfTest`)**: `140 s` indoor stop (`speed <= 0.05`, `acc = 50 m`) clamped to `0.0 m/s` (`+0.0 m` drift) with moving pre-stop speeds intact, plus unchanged `ExtendedRtsOdometrySmootherTest:140` (`wheelSpeedMps = 10.0`), `ZUPT_SPEED_VARIANCE = 2.0`, and `MIN_DOPPLER_SPEED_MPS = 3.0`.
* **`TST-FIL-027.4` (`ErtsPostWorkoutRecomputerTest`, `TST-FIL-017.3`, `TST-FIL-019.3`)**: `DISTANCE_m_LAP` per-lap reset matching `Laps.db`, `PolyUtil.simplify(validLatLngs, 10.0)` (`2` collinear endpoints instead of `30`/`25`), and `ENCODING_STEP_SIZE` (`20`) stream subsampling (`< 20` & `>= 60` rows).
