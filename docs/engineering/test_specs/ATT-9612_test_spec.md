## 1. Requirement Specification (`REQ-SET-129`) & Given-When-Then Acceptance Criteria
1. **`REQ-SET-129.1` — Linearizable `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` Yield Quiescence (`gattActionLock`)**:
   - *Normative*: `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` SHALL serialize its GATT-mutating lifecycle actions (`onSearchTimeout()` `disconnectFromDevice()`, `connectPersistentSession()` `connectToDevice()`, and `onSilentHrWatchdogTimeout()` `DISCONNECT_AND_RECONNECT` `forceDisconnectDevice()`) under a dedicated `gattActionLock` that re-verifies `!isShutDown.get() && !isOfflineGattYielded()` before touching `polarApi`. `pauseForOfflineSync()` SHALL cancel `reconnectJob`, `searchTimeoutJob`, `silentHrWatchdog`, `hrStalenessTimer`, and `streamCoordinator` renegotiations and then traverse `synchronized(gattActionLock)` as a quiescence barrier before returning to `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.withGattYieldAndPolarApi()` so zero late bridge teardowns or competing connects can fire during an active offline sync.
   - *Given* an in-flight `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge` search timeout (`onSearchTimeout`), backoff reconnect wake-up, or silent-HR disconnect racing concurrently with `withGattYieldAndPolarApi()`, *When* `pauseForOfflineSync()` completes, *Then* any pre-existing teardown has fully finished before `withGattYieldAndPolarApi()` executes `block(api)` and zero bridge `disconnectFromDevice()` / `connectToDevice()` / `forceDisconnectDevice()` calls occur while the offline yield lease is held.
2. **`REQ-SET-129.2` — Disconnect-Recovering & In-Flight-Reusing `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.connectWithinTimeout()`**:
   - *Normative*: `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.multiplexerCallback` SHALL track alias-aware `connectingDeviceIds` (`isDeviceConnecting()`, set on `deviceConnecting`, cleared on `deviceConnected` / `deviceDisconnected` / `blePowerStateChanged(false)` / `forceDisconnectDevice` / `resetForTesting`). On managed APIs (`isManagedApi(api)`), `connectWithinTimeout(api, polarId, timeoutMs)` SHALL: (a) return `true` immediately without calling `api.connectToDevice()` when `isDeviceConnected(polarId)` is already `true`; (b) register a transient `finally`-cleaned multiplexer callback listener for `deviceConnected` and `deviceDisconnected`; (c) await an already in-flight handshake without issuing a duplicate `connectToDevice()` when `isDeviceConnecting(polarId)` is `true` on initial entry; and (d) whenever `deviceDisconnected` (or a transient non-cancellation disconnect error) arrives mid-wait (e.g., Polar SDK `15 s` disconnect guard or `30 s` connection watchdog tearing down a pre-yield bridge attempt), cancel the wedged `waitForConnection()`, wait `connectRetryBackoffMs` (`250 ms` default, `@VisibleForTesting` injectable), and immediately re-invoke `api.connectToDevice(polarId)` within the remaining `timeoutMs` budget while strictly rethrowing `CancellationException`. On unmanaged `apiOverride` mocks (`!isManagedApi(api)`), legacy single-shot behavior MUST be preserved.
   - *Given* a managed `PolarBleApi` whose stale pre-yield handshake fires `deviceDisconnected` mid-`connectWithinTimeout()` while `waitForConnection()` stays blocked, *When* `connectWithinTimeout()` observes `deviceDisconnected`, *Then* it cancels the stuck wait, retries `api.connectToDevice(polarId)` after `connectRetryBackoffMs`, and returns `true` on the subsequent `deviceConnected` within a single *Sync Now* attempt instead of timing out at `45 s`.
   - *Given* a managed `PolarBleApi` where the target sensor is already `isDeviceConnected` or `isDeviceConnecting` when *Sync Now* is tapped, *When* `connectWithinTimeout()` runs, *Then* it reuses/awaits the active session with `0` duplicate conflicting `connectToDevice()` calls.
3. **`REQ-SET-129.3` — Alias-Aware `20 s` `awaitFeaturesReady()` & Verity Sense / H10 Sibling Parity**:
   - *Normative*: `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.FEATURE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS` SHALL be `20_000L` ms (bounded inside caller timeouts) and `awaitFeaturesReady()` SHALL evaluate `api.isFeatureReady(alias, feature)` across all registered MAC/8-hex aliases (`collectAliasClosure(polarId)`). `PolarH10OfflineController` (`syncPendingWorkout`, `stopAndBackfillWorkout`, `executeArming`) and `PolarVeritySenseOfflineController` (`performArmingSequence`, `stopAndFetchOfflineHr`, `removeOfflineRecord`, `connectAndMatchSession`) SHALL route connection establishment through `connectWithinTimeout()`, and `PolarH10OfflineController` SHALL await required offline features via `awaitFeaturesReady()` on managed APIs (`isManagedApi(api)`), preserving unmanaged `apiOverride` test mocks.
   - *Given* a Polar Verity Sense or Polar H10 already connected and mid-GATT service discovery (`bleSdkFeatureReady` arriving `12–15 s` after tap, or keyed by MAC vs 8-hex alias), *When* Aftermath `syncPendingWorkout()` executes, *Then* both controllers await feature readiness cleanly and complete backfill on Attempt 1 without failing mid-discovery.
4. **`REQ-SET-129.4` — Monolith Ceilings, Public Signature Pin, Detailed Design & Zero String Changes**:
   - *Normative*: Keep `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.kt <= 1,500`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt <= 1,500` (unchanged constructor & public JVM signatures), `PolarVeritySenseOfflineController.kt <= 1,500`, `PolarH10OfflineController.kt <= 1,500`; keep `BACKFILL_TIMEOUT_MS` (`30_000L` H10 / `45_000L` Verity) and `STOP_CONNECTION_TIMEOUT_MS` (`5_000L`) unchanged; update `docs/architecture/detailed_design/my_btle_device.md` (`REQ-PRO-114`); `0` edits to `DeviceManager.java`, `TrackerService.java`, `DevicesDatabaseManager.java`, and `res/values*/strings*.xml` (`Localization Parity: N/A - 0 string changes`).

## 2. Requirement Archaeology & Chesterton's Fence Audit (`REQ-PRO-022`)
1. **Original Requirement ID & Target**: Refines `REQ-SET-120` (`ATT-9175`), `REQ-SET-119` (`ATT-8969`), `REQ-SET-118` (`ATT-8690`), `REQ-CON-050`, `REQ-CON-054` across `PolarOfflineGattCoordinator.kt`, `PolarOnlineStreamingBridge.kt`, `PolarVeritySenseOfflineController.kt`, `PolarH10OfflineController.kt`.

3. **Root Reason for Existing Formulation**: `ATT-9175` added cooperative yield cancellation without a quiescence barrier; `ATT-8969` introduced single-shot `connectWithinTimeout()`; `FEATURE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS = 10 s` predated 8-feature discovery durations.
