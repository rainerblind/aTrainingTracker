# Ticket Deliverable: ATT-9164 — Zone-Colored Heart Rate & Power Curves and Zone Boundary Y-Axis Labels

* **Risk Tier**: `tier-b` | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0` | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-68`

---

## 1. Problem, Scope & Change-Request Impact Analysis
* **Problem / Value Proposition**:
  * In `TelemetryMetricGraph.kt`, `HRV_DFA_A1` already renders its curve and area fill using a hard-stepped vertical zone-color gradient (`Brush.verticalGradient`) alongside intermediate threshold labels on the Y-axis.
  * By contrast, `HEART_RATE` and `POWER` graphs render zone background bands (`alpha = 0.10f`) and zone-colored marginal histograms, yet still draw their primary curve in a single flat accent color and display only raw unrounded `min`/`max` labels (plus `ATT-9170`'s `Ø` average label), making zone crossings and exact physiological thresholds harder to read at a glance.
* **In-Scope / Out-of-Scope**:
  * *In-Scope*:
    1. Theme-adaptive WCAG 2.1 AA (`>= 3.0:1`) zone stroke palette (`TTColor.zoneStrokeColor(zoneIndex, isDarkTheme)`) in `Color.kt` covering all 7 zones (`Z1`..`Z7`) across Light, Dark, and AMOLED surfaces (specifically darkening bright `Z1` chartreuse / `Z3` orange on Light surfaces and lifting `Z5` violet / `Z6` brown / `Z7` black to high-contrast silver-white on Dark/AMOLED surfaces).
    2. Threshold validity helpers (`isValidHeartRateThresholds`, `isValidPowerThresholds`), hard-stepped vertical gradient builder (`buildZoneBandsVerticalGradientStops`), point-to-zone stroke resolver (`resolveMetricZoneStrokeColor`), and dynamic round-number fallback tick generator (`calculateNiceIntermediateTicks`) in `TelemetryZoneMath.kt`.
    3. Unified zone-colored stroke/fill gradient, single-point dot, and scrub-marker dot coloring in `TelemetryMetricGraph.kt` for `HEART_RATE` and `POWER` whenever valid zones are configured, falling back to `accentColor` (`TelemetryGraphColorPreferences`) when zones are unconfigured.
    4. Multi-source overlay contrast preservation (`TelemetryOverlayComponents.kt`): when zone coloring is active on the primary series (`avoidZoneColors = true`), render secondary comparison overlays and legend/scrub dots in zone-orthogonal high-contrast colors (`CYAN`, `THEME_DEFAULT`, `MAGENTA`, `TEAL`) so multiple sensors (e.g., Polar H10 + Verity Sense) stay unmistakable across every zone.
    5. Zone-boundary Y-axis labels strictly within `(dataMin, dataMax)` for `HEART_RATE` and `POWER` (and round-number fallback ticks when unzoned) fed through `ATT-9170`'s `resolveTelemetryYAxisLabels` priority engine (`Y_AXIS_PRIORITY_AVERAGE > Y_AXIS_PRIORITY_THRESHOLD > Y_AXIS_PRIORITY_EXTREMA`) so `Ø` average always wins, zone boundaries suppress colliding raw edge `min`/`max` labels, and labels never overlap.
    6. Pure-JVM unit tests + Robolectric Compose UI & Light/Dark/AMOLED `Rule 8.5` PNG screenshot verification.
  * *Out-of-Scope*: `ElevationProfile.kt`, `SPEED`/`PACE`/`SLOPE`/`CADENCE`/`W_PRIME_BAL`/`HRV_RMSSD` curve coloring, database schema, and string XMLs (`0` new localized strings).
* **Chesterton's Fence / Impact**:
  * Preserves `REQ-UI-206` (`TelemetryMetricGraph`), `REQ-UI-230` (zone bands & `6.dp/6.dp` dashed threshold lines), `REQ-UI-530` (`TelemetryGraphColorPreferences` for non-zoned metrics and unzoned HR/Power fallback), `REQ-HRV-044`/`REQ-HRV-046` (`DFA α1` inverted 3-zone rendering), `REQ-UI-2797` (`ATT-9170` dotted grey average line & top-priority `Ø` gutter label), `Rule 64` plot alignment, `ThemeColorCentralizationAuditTest` (zero inline `Color(0x...)` outside `Color.kt`), and `TelemetryMetricGraph.kt <= 1,965` LoC (`ArchitectureConformanceContractTest`).
  * `SYS.2`/`SYS.3` impact: `NONE` (SW-only post-workout Compose visualization inside `TelemetryMetricGraph`).

## 2. Decision & Architecture Impact
* **Design Decision & Rationale**:
  * Centralize all contrast-tuned `Z1`..`Z7` stroke tokens in `TTColor.zoneStrokeColor(zoneIndex, isDarkTheme)` (`ui/theme/Color.kt`) so every zone achieves `>= 3.4:1` contrast across Light, Dark, and AMOLED while obeying `ThemeColorCentralizationAuditTest`.
  * Unify `hrvStrokeStops`/`hrvFillStops` with `zoneStrokeStops`/`zoneFillStops` in `TelemetryMetricGraph.kt` and keep all gradient math & round-tick selection pure-JVM in `TelemetryZoneMath.kt`, preserving `TelemetryMetricGraph.kt` well under `1,965` LoC.
  * Reuse `ATT-9170`'s `resolveTelemetryYAxisLabels` with `Y_AXIS_PRIORITY_THRESHOLD` for HR/Power zone boundaries and unzoned nice fallback ticks so `Ø` average (`100`) > zone boundaries/ticks (`50`) > raw min/max extrema (`20`) works uniformly across `HEART_RATE`, `POWER`, and `HRV_DFA_A1`.
  * Validate passed `hrZoneThresholds` / `powerZoneThresholds` via `isValid*Thresholds` so invalid/zero thresholds cleanly denote "no zones configured" without breaking any existing `TelemetryMetricGraph` caller signature.
* **SWE.2 Architecture / Files Touched**:
  * Production:
    * `app/src/main/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/theme/Color.kt`
    * `app/src/main/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/map/TelemetryZoneMath.kt`
    * `app/src/main/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/map/TelemetryOverlayComponents.kt`
    * `app/src/main/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/map/TelemetryMetricGraph.kt`
  * Tests:
    * `app/src/test/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/map/TelemetryMetricGraphZoneColorUiTest.kt` (new pure-JVM + Robolectric Compose UI & Light/Dark/AMOLED PNG verification suite)
    * `app/src/test/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/map/TelemetryMetricGraphColorTest.kt` (pass explicit unconfigured thresholds for unzoned accent-color fallback tests)
    * `app/src/test/java/com/atrainingtracker/trainingtracker/ui/map/TelemetryMetricGraphHrvUiTest.kt` (pass explicit unconfigured HR thresholds for solid-invariance check)
  * `docs/architecture.md`: unchanged (no new subsystem/service/DB/prefix family).
