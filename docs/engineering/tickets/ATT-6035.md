# Ticket Deliverable: ATT-6035 — GeoUtils, ViewModels & Hilfspakete: Ellipsoid- vs. Haversine-Distanz konsolidieren, BaseMappableListViewModel vereinheitlichen und util-Pakete aufräumen

* **Risk Tier**: `tier-b` | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0` | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-6033`

---

## 1. Problem, Scope & Change-Request Impact Analysis
* **Problem / Root Cause**:
  1. **Duplicated point-to-point distance formulas across spherical & ellipsoidal callers**: `GeoUtils.haversineDistanceMeters` (`routes/GeoUtils.kt`) already exists with zero Android imports (`R = 6371000.0`), yet 4 spherical callers (`RouteAutoDetector`, `CropWorkoutMath`, `IndirectTpmsEstimator`, `KnownLocationsGeometryMath` with `R = 6371009.0`) inline duplicate Haversine code, while 2 ellipsoidal callers (`WorkoutClusterEngine.distanceBetween` returning `Float`, `SpatialRegionEngine.distanceBetween` returning `Double`) duplicate `Location.distanceBetween(...)` + `catch (RuntimeException)` JVM Haversine fallback logic. Naively replacing ellipsoidal paths with spherical Haversine would silently shift production cluster scores, known-location radii, and spatial region splits by ~0.3–0.5%.
  2. **Divergent Mappable List ViewModels & duplicated `ui/utils` package**: `RoutesViewModel`, `SegmentListViewModel`, and `WorkoutSummariesViewModel` extend `BaseMappableListViewModel`, while `WorkoutClustersViewModel` duplicates `_sortOrder`/`setSortOrder`/`calculateDistance` and `KnownLocationsViewModel` runs 4 imperative `init` collectors with `.value` cross-reads (`allWorkouts.value`, `allClusters.value`, `locationsFlow.value`) instead of declarative `combine(...)` flows. Separately, `trainingtracker.ui.utils` (`CollapsingAppBarNestedScrollConnection.kt`, `EncodingUtils.kt`) duplicates `trainingtracker.ui.util`.
  3. **Duplicated calculation & accumulator pipelines**: `PowerPhysiologyCalculator` duplicates paired-sample filtering + first/second-half decoupling aggregation in `computeAerobicEfficiencyAndDecoupling` and step accumulation in `computeWPrimeBalanceSeries`; `BikeBellAudioSynthesizer` duplicates the 5-mode sine sum across `calculateStrikeSample` and `calculateRollStrikeSample`; `MyDoubleAccumulatorSensor` and `MyIntegerAccumulatorSensor` duplicate `add`/`sub` subclasses instead of passing `BinaryOperator<N>` to `MyAccumulatorSensor<N>`.
* **In-Scope / Out-of-Scope**:
  * *In-Scope*:
    * **Teilpaket A**: Add `@JvmOverloads` `earthRadiusMeters: Double = EARTH_RADIUS_METERS` to `GeoUtils.haversineDistanceMeters` (zero Android imports); add `AndroidGeoDistance.ellipsoidalDistanceMeters(...): Double` (`Location.distanceBetween` + `catch (RuntimeException)` Haversine fallback); delegate `WorkoutClusterEngine.distanceBetween` (`Float`), `SpatialRegionEngine.distanceBetween` (`Double`), `RouteAutoDetector.computeDistanceMeters` (`Float`), `CropWorkoutMath.haversineDistanceMeters` (`EPSILON` clamp), `IndirectTpmsEstimator.computeDistanceMeters`, and `KnownLocationsGeometryMath.calculateDistanceMeters` (`R = 6371009.0`); add Golden-Value tests (`< 1e-6 m` tolerance).
    * **Teilpaket B**: Unify `WorkoutClustersViewModel` (leaving line 100 `MyPreferenceManager(application)` untouched per `ATT-8121` parallel-lane hygiene) and `KnownLocationsViewModel` (`KnownLocationSortOrder : MappableSortOrder`) under `BaseMappableListViewModel`; migrate `KnownLocationsViewModel.init` to declarative `combine(...)` flows eliminating `.value` cross-reads while preserving `defaultDispatches` deduplication; delegate `BaseMappableListViewModel.calculateDistance` to `AndroidGeoDistance`; consolidate `trainingtracker.ui.utils` into `trainingtracker.ui.util` across all `app/src/main` & `app/src/test` callers (including `StreamSummaryCalculator.kt` from `ATT-6036` and `MapDetailLayoutCollapsingHeaderContractTest.kt`).
    * **Teilpaket C**: Deduplicate `PowerPhysiologyCalculator` (`computeAerobicEfficiencyAndDecoupling` & `computeWPrimeBalanceSeries`), `BikeBellAudioSynthesizer` (5-mode sine sum helper), and `MyDoubleAccumulatorSensor` / `MyIntegerAccumulatorSensor` (`BinaryOperator<N>` in `MyAccumulatorSensor<N>`) with numeric parity tests.
  * *Out-of-Scope*: Deleting `ui/util/Event.kt` (`ATT-8130`), polyline/segment projections (`ATT-8133`), `MyPreferenceManager(application)` singleton refactor in `WorkoutClustersViewModel.kt:100` (`ATT-8121`), and changing Null-Island thresholds or live-vs-crop distance semantics. `Localization Parity: N/A - 0 new or modified UI strings`.
* **Chesterton's Fence / Impact**:
  * Preserves `GeoUtils.kt` pure-JVM invariant (0 `android.*` / `com.google.android.*` imports).
  * Preserves WGS-84 ellipsoidal semantics (`WorkoutClusterEngine`, `SpatialRegionEngine`, `BaseMappableListViewModel`) vs. spherical Haversine (`R = 6371000.0` vs. `6371009.0` in `KnownLocationsGeometryMath`) within `< 1e-6 m`.
  * Preserves `WorkoutClusterEngine` `Float` return type (`14+` callers), `RouteAutoDetector` `Float` return type, `CropWorkoutMath.EPSILON` clamp, `KnownLocationsViewModel` `@JvmOverloads` `Application`-only constructor (`AndroidViewModelFactory`), `TestUnfinishedWorkoutChurn` zero-dispatch guard, `ArchitectureConformanceContractTest` 22-entry UI DB caller baseline, and `MyDoubleAccumulatorSensor` / `MyIntegerAccumulatorSensor` public constructors & types (`10+` device classes).
  * `SYS.2`/`SYS.3` impact: `NONE` (internal refactoring preserving external behavior).

## 2. Decision & Architecture Impact
* **Design Decision & Rationale**:
  * *Teilpaket A*: Keep `GeoUtils.kt` pure JVM (`@JvmOverloads fun haversineDistanceMeters(..., earthRadiusMeters: Double = EARTH_RADIUS_METERS)`), and introduce `AndroidGeoDistance.kt` in `com.atrainingtracker.trainingtracker.routes` for `android.location.Location.distanceBetween` with `catch (_: RuntimeException)` fallback to `GeoUtils.haversineDistanceMeters`.
  * *Teilpaket B*: `BaseMappableListViewModel` uses `AndroidGeoDistance.ellipsoidalDistanceMeters(...).toFloat()`. `WorkoutClustersViewModel` and `KnownLocationsViewModel` extend `BaseMappableListViewModel` (`ClusterSortOrder` and `KnownLocationSortOrder` implementing `MappableSortOrder`). `KnownLocationsViewModel` separates heavy spatial grouping (`combine(locationsFlow, allClusters, finishedWorkoutsFlow)`) from lightweight UI projection (`combine(spatialGroupingFlow, _sortOrder, _filterCriteria, locationFlow, _editDialogState)`), eliminating imperative cross-flow `.value` reads. `CollapsingAppBarNestedScrollConnection.kt` and `EncodingUtils.kt` (plus their unit tests) move from `ui.utils` to `ui.util`; `ui/util/Event.kt` stays untouched. `docs/architecture.md` has zero `ui.utils` references to update.
  * *Teilpaket C*: `PowerPhysiologyCalculator` extracts `collectPairedSamples` + `evaluateAerobicMetric` (parameterized by aggregator `(List<Double>) -> Double?`, efficiency calculator `(Double, Double) -> Double?`, and `isPowerBased`) and extracts `appendWPrimeStep`. `BikeBellAudioSynthesizer` extracts `calculateFiveModeSample(dt, fundamental, tau0..tau4)`. `MyAccumulatorSensor<N>` accepts `BinaryOperator<N> addOp, BinaryOperator<N> subOp` in its constructor so `MyDoubleAccumulatorSensor` and `MyIntegerAccumulatorSensor` supply method references/lambdas (`Double::sum`, `Integer::sum`, subtraction) without duplicating `add`/`sub` implementations.
* **SWE.2 Architecture / Files Touched**:
  * Production: `GeoUtils.kt`, `AndroidGeoDistance.kt` (new), `WorkoutClusterEngine.kt`, `SpatialRegionEngine.kt`, `RouteAutoDetector.kt`, `CropWorkoutMath.kt`, `IndirectTpmsEstimator.kt`, `KnownLocationsGeometryMath.kt`, `BaseMappableListViewModel.kt`, `WorkoutClustersViewModel.kt`, `KnownLocationsViewModel.kt`, `KnownLocationSortOrder.kt`, `ui/util/CollapsingAppBarNestedScrollConnection.kt`, `ui/util/EncodingUtils.kt`, callers of `ui.utils.*` (`StreamSummaryCalculator.kt`, `WorkoutRepository.kt`, `LegacyImportEngine.kt`, `WorkoutDataMapper.kt`, `WorkoutSummariesDatabaseManager.java`, `TrackerService.java`, `LiveWorkoutSession.java`, `LapEditBottomSheet.kt`, `TrackOnMapAftermathViewModel.kt`, `MapModels.kt`, `ElevationProfile.kt`, `MapDetailLayout.kt`, `RouteTabbedScreen.kt`, `EquipmentTabsScreen.kt`, `PeriodsTabsScreen.kt`, `WorkoutDetailTabbedContent.kt`, `WorkoutTabsScreen.kt`, `WorkoutSummariesListFragment.kt`, `SegmentsTabsScreen.kt`, `WorkoutClustersTabsScreen.kt`, `DevicesTabbedScreen.kt`, `SportTypesTabsScreen.kt`), `PowerPhysiologyCalculator.kt`, `BikeBellAudioSynthesizer.kt`, `MyAccumulatorSensor.java`, `MyDoubleAccumulatorSensor.java`, `MyIntegerAccumulatorSensor.java`.
  * Tests: `GeoUtilsTest.kt`, `AndroidGeoDistanceTest.kt` (new), `CropWorkoutMathTest.kt`, `KnownLocationsGeometryMathTest.kt`, `WorkoutClustersViewModelTest.kt`, `KnownLocationsViewModelTest.kt`, `PowerPhysiologyCalculatorTest.kt`, `BikeBellAudioSynthesizerTest.kt`, `MyAccumulatorSensorTest.kt`, `ui/util/CollapsingAppBarNestedScrollConnectionTest.kt`, `ui/util/NumericalEncodingUtilsTest.kt`, `MapDetailLayoutCollapsingHeaderContractTest.kt`, `LiveWorkoutSessionTest.kt`, and `ui.utils` import updates across test files.
