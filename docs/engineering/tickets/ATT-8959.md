# Ticket Deliverable: ATT-8959 — Full-Screen Radar: Top-down (GTA-1 style) vehicle markers selectable in Expert Settings

## 1. Problem, Scope & Change-Request Impact Analysis
* **Motivation & Gap**: Following `ATT-8733` and `ATT-8958`, Full-Screen Radar (`RadarEdgeSide.FULL_SCREEN`) renders approaching vehicles as enlarged side-view icon pills (`COMPACT_BADGE`). While highly legible, athletes requested an optional **top-down ("GTA-1 style") overhead vehicle marker** mode aligned with forward riding direction (bottom -> top) so approaching cars, heavy trucks/buses, and generic V1 vehicles are immediately recognizable in spatial overhead perspective — selectable directly in Expert Settings / Cycling Radar.
* **In-Scope / Out-of-Scope**:
  * *In-Scope*:
    1. Add `RadarFullScreenMarkerStyle` enum (`COMPACT_BADGE`, `TOP_DOWN_VEHICLES`; default `COMPACT_BADGE`) and DataStore persistence (`KEY_RADAR_FULL_SCREEN_MARKER_STYLE`, `radarFullScreenMarkerStyleFlow`, `updateRadarFullScreenMarkerStyle`) in `TuningPreferencesDataStore.kt` with defensive `safeEnumValueOf` deserialization and factory reset support.
    2. Expose a Material 3 `TuningSegmentedButtonRow` (`Compact Badge` vs. `Top-Down Vehicles`) inside `RadarSettingsSection.kt` whenever `tuningConfig.radarEdgeSide == RadarEdgeSide.FULL_SCREEN` (localized across all 9 `values*/strings_radar.xml` files).
    3. Create dedicated sibling Canvas renderer **`RadarTopDownVehicleMarker.kt`** (keeping `RadarEdgeOverlay.kt` well below `1,500` LoC) rendering crisp overhead top-down ("GTA-1 style") vehicle silhouettes pointing upward (`bottom -> top`), with distinct proportions for `UNKNOWN_OR_LIGHT` (compact overhead hatchback/generic car — **never** a motorcycle per `ATT-8958`), `CAR` (overhead 3-box sedan), and `TRUCK_OR_BUS` (overhead semi-truck cab + articulated cargo trailer with roof ribs), paired with an integrated bold `18–20.sp` tabular (`tnum`) distance tag directly below the rear bumper, 100% solid opaque threat color (`TTColor.RadarThreatAmber` / `TTColor.RadarThreatRed`), dual-rim contrast framing, and single-pass `.layout` Frame-1 bounds clamping.
    4. Centralize all overhead vehicle color tokens (`RadarTopDownWindshield`, `RadarTopDownTire`, `RadarTopDownHeadlight`, `RadarTopDownTaillight`, `RadarTopDownRoofHighlight`, `RadarTopDownTrailerRib`) in `Color.kt` (`0` inline `Color(0x...)` in composables).
    5. Wire `tuningConfig.radarFullScreenMarkerStyle` from `TrackingTabsScreen.kt` -> `RadarEdgeOverlay.kt` -> `RadarFullScreenThreatOverlay`, preserving 100% touch pass-through (`HorizontalPager` & `GoogleMap`), `ATT-8958` V1 lane-guide suppression, solid opaque threat fill, and per-target threat hysteresis.
  * *Out-of-Scope*: Modifying `LEFT`/`RIGHT` `RadarVerticalRail` dot geometry (`REQ-TRK-073.2`), BLE/ANT+ parsers (`BTLERadarDevice.java`, `ANTBikeRadarDevice.kt`), or audio alert logic (`RadarAlertManager.kt`).
* **Chesterton's Fence / Impact**: Net-new `REQ-TRK-098` / `TST-TRK-098` complementing `REQ-TRK-089` (`ATT-8733`, `ATT-8958`) and `REQ-TRK-062` (`SYSREQ-TRK-004`). Preserves backward-compatible default (`COMPACT_BADGE`), 100% gesture pass-through, clear-road badge suppression (`ATT-8679`), `ATT-8958` V1 non-motorcycle & no-lane-guide & non-oscillation invariants, `private` `@Preview` visibility, and `<= 1,500` LoC modularity (`RadarEdgeOverlay.kt` & `RadarTopDownVehicleMarker.kt`).

## 2. Decision & Architecture Impact
* **Design Decision & Rationale**:
  * **Dedicated Sibling Renderer (`RadarTopDownVehicleMarker.kt`)**: Isolating the overhead Canvas drawing logic, visual specification (`TopDownVehicleVisualSpec`), and Light/Dark/Map `@Preview` composables keeps `RadarEdgeOverlay.kt` (`~1,330` LoC) cleanly under `1,500` LoC and makes visual unit testing modular.
  * **Rigid Stacked Silhouette + Rear Distance Pill**: Rendering the overhead vehicle Canvas (`46×72`, `52×84`, `60×114 dp`) above a tightly coupled high-contrast stadium distance tag (`18–20.sp` `ExtraBold` `tnum`) inside one `.layout`-clamped container avoids text obscuring vehicle windshields/roofs and prevents 3-digit `"160m"` clipping while preserving crisp overhead car/truck silhouettes at handlebar glance distance.
  * **Conditional Binary Switcher (`docs/design_guidelines.md §1.1 & §1.4`)**: Showing `TuningSegmentedButtonRow` for `RadarFullScreenMarkerStyle` strictly when `radarEdgeSide == RadarEdgeSide.FULL_SCREEN` mirrors how `RadarClearMode` is hidden in `FULL_SCREEN` mode, keeping `RadarSettingsSection` clutter-free.
* **SWE.2 Architecture / Files Touched**:
  * Production: `RadarTopDownVehicleMarker.kt` (new), `RadarEdgeOverlay.kt`, `Color.kt`, `TuningPreferencesDataStore.kt`, `RadarSettingsSection.kt`, `TrackingTabsScreen.kt`, `app/src/main/res/values*/strings_radar.xml` (9 locales).
  * Tests: `RadarTopDownVehicleMarkerTest.kt` (new), `RadarEdgeOverlayTest.kt`, `RadarSettingsSectionTest.kt` (plus `TranslationParityTest`, `ThemeColorCentralizationAuditTest`, `ArchitectureConformanceContractTest`).
  * Living Docs: `docs/requirements/req_tracking_cockpit_part3.md` (`REQ-TRK-098`), `docs/tests/tst_tracking_cockpit_part2.md` (`TST-TRK-098`), master indexes (`docs/requirements.md`, `docs/tests.md`).
