### Requirement Archaeology & Chesterton's Fence Audit
* **Original Requirement ID & Target**: `REQ-SEN-028` (`REQ-SEN-028.1`) and `REQ-FIL-016` (`REQ-FIL-016.3`) targeting `strings_devices.xml` (9 locales), `DevicesDatabaseManager.java`, `DevicesDatabaseMigrationV16Helper.kt`, `Manufacturer.java`, and `DeviceData.kt`.
* **Historical Origin & Commit Trace**: Introduced in `ATT-7751` (`7d2836ef`, `Devices.db` v11) and `ATT-6279` (`75c1497e`, `Devices.db` v12) with internal `'ATT '` prefixes on Fused sensor names and `Build.BRAND` written to `MANUFACTURER_NAME` for all `Protocol.SMARTPHONE` rows.
* **Root Reason for Existing Formulation**: Initial prototyping reused `Build.BRAND` across `addLocationDevicesToDb` / `migrateAdd*` and prefixed sensor titles with internal `'ATT'` shorthand to distinguish them from Google Fused in developer testing.
* **Preservation of Core Invariants**: Both virtual sensors remain unpaired by default (`PAIRED = 0`); custom user-renamed sensor names in `Devices.db` are strictly preserved during v15 -> v16 migration; `Build.BRAND` remains intact for physical hardware sensors (`SPEED_AND_LOCATION_GPS`, `ALTITUDE_FROM_PRESSURE`), while virtual fused/DEM sensors display `"a Training Tracker"` (preserving lowercase `'a'`) and `SPEED_AND_LOCATION_GOOGLE_FUSED` displays `"Google"`.
