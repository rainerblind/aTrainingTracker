# Ticket Deliverable: ATT-8735 — Replace radar map pins with zoom-scaled closing-speed car icons supporting 1000+ passes

* **Risk Tier**: `tier-b` | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0` | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-1286` | **Localization Parity**: `9/9 locales unchanged (reuses existing radar_marker_*_title and radar_vehicle_size_* strings in values*/strings_radar.xml)`

## 1. Problem, Scope & Change-Request Impact Analysis
* **Motivation & Forensic RCA (`att5088_map.png` inspected via `read_file`)**:
  * In `ATT-5088` (`WorkoutRepository.getWorkoutMarkers()` + `MarkerLayer` / `createSensorMarker`), radar vehicle pass events are rendered with `R.drawable.bike_radar` inside a `36.dp` `ic_map_pin_base` inverted blue teardrop pin. Because `bike_radar.xml` starts with a full `96×96` rounded-square background path (`#000000`), `createSensorMarker`'s `sensorDrawable.setTint(Color.White)` turns the entire badge into an opaque white square pasted on top of a huge blue teardrop — obscuring `Hauptstraße` and the blue route polyline (`2–3×` wider than the street), using identical blue for every closing speed/close-pass hazard, and lacking any zoom scaling or viewport culling.
  * Multi-hour rides easily record **1,000+ vehicle passes**. Rendering 1,000+ uncached `Marker` composables or allocating bitmaps per marker/zoom frame causes Compose recomposition churn, frame drops, and memory pressure.
  * Two collateral defects exist in the current path: (1) `MapLayers.kt:369` keys markers strictly by `(title, iconResId, draggable)` — colliding whenever two radar passes share the same speed title; (2) `WorkoutRepository.kt:2811` checks cumulative `(sample.radarLargeVehicleCount ?: 0) > 0` instead of per-pass delta (`> prevLargeVehicles`), mislabeling every subsequent close-pass car as a truck after the first truck passes.
* **In-Scope / Out-of-Scope**:
  * *In-Scope*:
    1. Add compact symmetric front-facing vector drawables `ic_radar_map_car.xml` and `ic_radar_map_truck.xml` (24×24 viewport with `evenOdd` windshield/headlight cutouts) and render them centered (`anchor = Offset(0.5f, 0.5f)`) with a crisp dual-contrast outline (dark outer hairline + opaque white halo) legible on both Light and Dark Google Maps tiles and over route polylines.
    2. Centralize `TTColor.RadarClosePassBrown`, `TTColor.RadarMapIconOutlineLight`, and `TTColor.RadarMapIconOutlineDark` in `Color.kt` alongside `RadarClearGreen`, `RadarThreatAmber`, and `RadarThreatRed` (0 inline `Color(0x...)` outside `Color.kt`).
    3. Implement deterministic color priority (`Close Pass` Brown -> `>= 15.0 m/s` Red -> `[7.0, 15.0) m/s` Amber -> `< 7.0 m/s` Green), zoom-out cutoff (`currentZoom < 14.5f` renders `0` radar icons), 5 quantized zoom-bucket sizes (`8, 10, 12, 15, 18 dp`, staying `<=` half rendered Google Maps road width), hysteresis-buffered viewport culling for 1,000+ passes, and strictly bounded `O(1)` `RadarPassMarkerIconCache` keyed by `(colorCategory, isLargeVehicle, zoomBucket)` (`<= 40` bitmaps total; `<= 20` for V1 cars-only).
    4. Fix `MarkerLayer` `key(...)` collision and `WorkoutRepository.getWorkoutMarkers()` per-pass delta `radarLargeVehicleCount` detection.
    5. Update `REQ-RAD-006`, `TST-RAD-006`, `REQ-TRK-073.5`, and `docs/architecture.md` §4.9 (`RadarPassMarkerIconCache` ceiling).
  * *Out-of-Scope*: Changing live tracking `RadarEdgeOverlay` / `RadarTopDownVehicleMarker`, FIT/Strava exporters, or modifying `strings_radar.xml` localization keys.

## 2. Decision & Architecture Impact
* **Design Decisions**:
  * **Carry `RadarPassMarkerData?` on `LocationMarker`**: Keeps `WorkoutRepository.getWorkoutMarkers()` returning `List<LocationMarker>`, automatically bringing zoom-scaled, viewport-culled car/truck icons to all 3 map surfaces (`TrackOnMapScreen`, `FullScreenWorkoutMapOverlay`, `WorkoutClusterHeatmapScreen` / `MapScreenWithTrack`) per `coding_standards.md §1.11` with zero caller breakage.
  * **Front-Facing Symmetric Car & Truck Silhouettes**: Unlike side-profile silhouettes (which look inverted/awkward on arbitrary road headings), symmetric front-facing silhouettes read cleanly on North/South/East/West roads at `8–18 dp` without rotation artifacts, and dual-contrast haloing (dark outer hairline + opaque white halo + `evenOdd` cutouts) guarantees legibility on both light and dark map tiles.
  * **Hysteresis-Buffered `MapViewportBounds` (`@Immutable`)**: Wrapping `LatLngBounds` in an `@Immutable` data class with a plain `ViewportBoundsHolder` (Rule 66 — zero `MutableState` writes during composition) and `25%` expansion margin prevents Compose recomposition churn on every 1-px pan frame while eliminating edge pop-in and culling 1,000+ passes cleanly.
  * **Dedicated `RadarPassMarkerLayer` Invoked from `MarkerLayer`**: Skips drag-haptic `LaunchedEffect`/`snapshotFlow` coroutines for radar markers, keys each marker stably by `(passIndex, lat, lng)` across viewport-culling shifts, layers higher-severity passes on top (`zIndex = 60f..63f`), and looks up cached `BitmapDescriptor`s in `O(1)`.
* **SWE.2 Architecture / Files Touched**:
  * Production: `Color.kt`, `ic_radar_map_car.xml` (new), `ic_radar_map_truck.xml` (new), `MapModels.kt`, `RadarPassMarkerMath.kt` (new), `MapLayers.kt`, `MapContentScope.kt`, `ATrainingTrackerMap.kt`, `WorkoutRepository.kt`.
  * Tests: `RadarPassMarkerMathTest.kt` (new), `WorkoutRepositoryMarkerTest.kt`.
  * Living Docs: `docs/requirements/req_maps_navigation.md` (`REQ-RAD-006`), `docs/requirements/req_tracking_cockpit_part2.md` (`REQ-TRK-073.5`), `docs/tests/tst_maps_navigation.md` (`TST-RAD-006`), `docs/architecture.md`, synced master indexes.
