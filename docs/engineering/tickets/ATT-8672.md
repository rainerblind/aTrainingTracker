# Ticket Deliverable: ATT-8672 — Cycling Radar (ATT-1286): Remove RadarTrafficSimulator Completely from App and UI

* **Risk Tier**: `tier-b` | **Target Release**: `V5.0.0` | **Parent Epic**: `ATT-1286`

---

## 1. Problem, Scope & Change-Request Impact Analysis
* **Problem / Motivation**: During Ceremony 2 review of `ATT-8455`, the Product Owner requested that the built-in **Radar Traffic Simulator** (`RadarTrafficSimulator`, `RadarScenario`, `SimulatedRadarDevice`, `RadarSource.SIMULATOR`) be removed completely from the app and UI now that physical cycling radar hardware (`Garmin Varia RearVue 820`) and the external host BLE peripheral simulator (`tools/ble_radar_simulator.py`) are available. Leaving the in-app simulator wired into `RadarLightController`, `RadarStateRepository`, `BTLERadarDevice`, `ANTBikeRadarDevice`, `TrackerService`, `TrainingApplication`, `RadarSettingsSection`, `AdvancedTuningAccordion`, `AdvancedTuningScreen`, `TrackingTabsScreen`, and `EditDeviceDialog` adds dead code, UI clutter, and maintenance overhead across 19 localized strings.
* **In-Scope / Out-of-Scope**:
  * *In-Scope*:
    1. Physically delete `RadarTrafficSimulator.kt` (`RadarTrafficSimulator` + `RadarScenario`) and `SimulatedRadarDevice.kt`, plus simulator-only test suites `RadarTrafficSimulatorTest.kt` and `SimulatedRadarDeviceTest.kt`.
    2. Remove `RadarSource.SIMULATOR` from `RadarSource` (`RadarState.kt`) and update `RadarStateRepository.kt` documentation (`BLE > ANT_PLUS`, `sourceStates <= 3`).
    3. Remove all `RadarTrafficSimulator` hooks and `isSimulating` branches from `RadarLightController.kt`, `BTLERadarDevice.java`, `ANTBikeRadarDevice.kt`, `TrackerService.java`, and `TrainingApplication.java`.
    4. Remove all simulator UI controls, status formatting, and `isSimulating` parameters/branches from `RadarSettingsSection.kt`, `AdvancedTuningAccordion.kt`, `AdvancedTuningScreen.kt`, `TrackingTabsScreen.kt`, and `EditDeviceDialog.kt`.
    5. Remove the 19 obsolete `radar_simulator_*` / `radar_scenario_*` string resources identically across all 9 `app/src/main/res/values*/strings_radar.xml` files.
    6. Update affected unit/UI tests (`RadarStateRepositoryTest.kt`, `RadarLightControllerTest.kt`, `BTLERadarDeviceTest.kt`, `ANTBikeRadarDeviceTest.kt`, `TrackerServiceFinalizationTest.kt`, `RadarSettingsSectionTest.kt`, `RadarAccordionSectionTest.kt`, `EditDeviceDialogRadarLightTest.kt`, `RadarEdgeOverlayTest.kt`) and add explicit excision/UI-absence verification (`TST-CON-029`).
    7. Update living specifications (`docs/architecture.md`, `docs/requirements/req_tracking_cockpit_part2.md`, `docs/requirements/req_tracking_cockpit_part3.md`, `docs/requirements/req_ui_design_system_part5.md`, `docs/requirements/req_ui_design_system_part6.md`, `docs/tests/tst_tracking_cockpit_part2.md`, `docs/tests/tst_ui_design_system_part3.md`, `docs/tests/tst_process_stability_part2.md`).
  * *Out-of-Scope*: Real BLE (`BTLERadarDevice`), ANT+ (`ANTBikeRadarDevice`), `RadarLightController` hardware/Auto/brake logic, `RadarStateRepository` BLE/ANT+ arbitration, `RadarEdgeOverlay` live tracking rendering, post-workout `RadarTrafficSummary*` analytics, and external Python host simulators (`tools/ble_radar_simulator.py`, `tools/replay_workout.py`, `tools/run_simulator_self_tests.py`).
### Requirement Archaeology & Chesterton's Fence Audit
1. *Original Requirement ID & Target*: `REQ-CON-029` (Built-in Cycling Radar Traffic Simulator & Scenario Engine), `REQ-CON-042` (Radar Application State Preservation During Preemption & Hardware Packet Ingestion), plus simulator clauses in `REQ-CON-030`, `REQ-CON-031`, `REQ-CON-032`, `REQ-CON-041`, `REQ-CON-046`, `REQ-CON-056`, `REQ-TRK-062`, `REQ-UI-512`.
2. *Historical Origin & Commit Trace*: `REQ-CON-029` was introduced in `ATT-5087` (`69fec8e7`) and refined in `ATT-6704`; `REQ-CON-042` was introduced in `ATT-7574` (`a3783bd7`) to keep simulator preemption from wiping snooze/light state. Physical `Garmin Varia RearVue 820` hardware and `tools/ble_radar_simulator.py` (`ATT-6676`) now cover testing without an in-app simulator; PO requested complete removal during Ceremony 2 review of `ATT-8455`.
3. *Root Reason for Existing Formulation*: The in-app simulator (`RadarTrafficSimulator`, `SimulatedRadarDevice`, `RadarSource.SIMULATOR`) was built as a pre-hardware UI/audio/analytics test aid, and `REQ-CON-042.1`/`042.2` guarded physical preemption of that simulator.
4. *Preservation of Core Invariants*: Real BLE (`BTLERadarDevice`) and ANT+ (`ANTBikeRadarDevice`) acquisition, `BLE > ANT_PLUS` arbitration in `RadarStateRepository` (`sourceStates <= 3`), `REQ-CON-042.3` preservation of `audioSnoozeState` and `lightMode` across physical telemetry ingestion, `RadarLightController` public JVM signatures, post-workout `RadarTrafficSummary*`, external `tools/ble_radar_simulator.py`, and 100% 9-language localization parity are all preserved intact.

* **Change-Request Impact Summary**:
  * *Affected Requirements*: `REQ-CON-029`, `REQ-CON-042`, `REQ-CON-030`, `REQ-CON-031`, `REQ-CON-032`, `REQ-CON-041`, `REQ-CON-046`, `REQ-CON-056`, `REQ-TRK-062`, `REQ-UI-512` (`SYSREQ-TRK-002`, `SYSREQ-TRK-004`, `SYSREQ-TRK-006`, `SYSREQ-TRK-007` preserved).
  * *Affected Architecture Elements*: `RadarState` (`RadarSource` reduced from 4 to 3 values: `NONE`, `ANT_PLUS`, `BLE`), `RadarStateRepository` (`sourceStates <= 3`), `RadarLightController`, `BTLERadarDevice`, `ANTBikeRadarDevice`, `TrackerService`, `TrainingApplication`, `RadarSettingsSection`, `AdvancedTuningAccordion`, `AdvancedTuningScreen`, `TrackingTabsScreen`, `EditDeviceDialog`.
  * *Verification Impact*: Retire `RadarTrafficSimulatorTest` and `SimulatedRadarDeviceTest`; update 9 unit/Compose UI test suites and add explicit classpath/UI-absence assertions for `TST-CON-029`; verify 9-locale `TranslationParityTest` and `verify_requirement_governance.py`.
  * *Risk Assessment & Invariants*: Medium (`tier-b`). Zero SQLite schema changes; real BLE/ANT+ acquisition, failover, `audioSnoozeState` and `isAutoMode` preservation, `RadarLightController` public JVM signatures, and external `tools/ble_radar_simulator.py` remain 100% intact.

## 2. Decision & Architecture Impact
* **Design Decision & Rationale**: Perform a clean, complete excision rather than hiding UI controls behind feature flags. Removing `RadarSource.SIMULATOR` and simplifying internal predicates (`isRadarOverlayVisible`, `shouldShowRadarLightControlSection`, `TuningSubtitleFormatter.formatCyclingRadarSubtitle`) eliminates dead parameters and unreachable branches while preserving all real hardware control paths and `RadarLightController`'s pinned public API.
* **SWE.2 Architecture / Files Touched**:
  * *Deleted*: `RadarTrafficSimulator.kt`, `SimulatedRadarDevice.kt`, `RadarTrafficSimulatorTest.kt`, `SimulatedRadarDeviceTest.kt`.
  * *Modified Production*: `RadarState.kt`, `RadarStateRepository.kt`, `RadarLightController.kt`, `BTLERadarDevice.java`, `ANTBikeRadarDevice.kt`, `TrackerService.java`, `TrainingApplication.java`, `RadarSettingsSection.kt`, `AdvancedTuningAccordion.kt`, `AdvancedTuningScreen.kt`, `TrackingTabsScreen.kt`, `EditDeviceDialog.kt`, and all 9 `app/src/main/res/values*/strings_radar.xml`.
  * *Modified Docs*: `docs/architecture.md` (§4.9 `RadarStateRepository` `sourceStates <= 3` + Revision History) and living requirement/test shards (`req_tracking_cockpit_part2.md`, `req_tracking_cockpit_part3.md`, `req_ui_design_system_part5.md`, `req_ui_design_system_part6.md`, `tst_tracking_cockpit_part2.md`, `tst_ui_design_system_part3.md`, `tst_process_stability_part2.md`).
