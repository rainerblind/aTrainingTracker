1. ATT-9154 is merged to develop (`0ca9f805`, 2026-11-20 23:16), yet Jira shows "Final Review (Human)". Did the PO approve the sprint merge outside Jira, or does code reach develop without human acceptance? The merge message also says "+ 2 Stage-6 WIP carryover". 2. Who writes the Jira ticket descriptions (user stories and acceptance criteria): the human PO or an agent? If it is the human, that is the only real stakeholder-requirement input, and SWE.1 BP6 could be partly credited at ticket level.

4. Is there any plan to approve requirement or architecture baselines at release tags (`docs/releases/<tag>/`)? I found no approved baseline in the snapshot.
