**App Title**: aTrainingTracker – Sports Tracker (29/30)

**Short Description**:

**Full Description**:
<b>aTrainingTracker – The Intelligent Sports Tracker for Your Smartphone</b>

Focus on your training. We'll handle the technology.

aTrainingTracker is an intelligent sports tracker for athletes who value automation, accuracy and privacy. It automatically detects your sport, selects the correct equipment, and organizes your favorite routes. Just press Start—the app handles the rest in the background.

Perfect for cyclists, runners, hikers and other endurance athletes who want reliable tracking, intelligent sensor management and complete control over their data.

<b>SMARTER TRACKING</b>
Turn your smartphone into an intelligent bike computer that learns how you train. 
• <b>Automatic Detection</b>: Automatically recognizes your sport and selects the correct bike or shoes based on your connected sensors.
• <b>Custom Dashboards for Every Sport</b>: Experience a cockpit that adapts to you. Customize separate layouts for cycling, running, hiking and more. See the exact metrics you need for each activity.
• <b>Activity Intelligence</b>: Stop organizing your workouts manually. Frequently used routes are automatically recognized and named, keeping your training log organized without effort.

<b>INTELLIGENT SENSOR MANAGEMENT</b>
Connect multiple Bluetooth® LE and ANT+® sensors simultaneously—including heart rate, cadence, speed, power, temperature and more.
Unlike standard tracking apps, aTrainingTracker doesn't just connect to sensors—it intelligently selects the best data source so you don't have to:
• <b>Hardware Priority</b>: Dedicated speed sensors are automatically preferred over GPS for maximum precision.
• <b>Never Lose Speed Data</b>: If a wheel sensor disconnects, GPS seamlessly takes over to ensure no data gaps.
• <b>Always the Best Sensor</b>: The app automatically uses the most accurate sensor available.

<b>FAVORITE ROUTES</b>
Do you train on the same routes regularly? aTrainingTracker takes care of the organization. Frequently used routes are automatically recognized and grouped together, giving you a complete history of every ride on that route.
• <b>Personal Bests</b>: Automatically compare all your efforts on specific routes.
• <b>Visual Evolution</b>: View your training history through detailed heatmap overlays.
• <b>Route Statistics</b>: Get comprehensive data for your regular training loops without manual tagging.

<b>LIVE SEGMENTS & NAVIGATION</b>
Take your performance to the next level with real-time feedback and guidance.
• <b>Strava Live Segments</b>: See your progress against your Personal Best in real-time as you ride or run.
• <b>Route Following</b>: Import GPX files or Strava routes and follow them on the interactive live map.
• <b>Training Zones</b>: Stay in the right intensity with configurable heart rate and power zones.

<b>DETAILED ANALYSIS</b>
Once your session is done, find comprehensive local statistics ready for review:
• <b>Analytical Tools</b>: Interactive charts, heatmaps, route comparisons, and equipment statistics.
• <b>Environment Data</b>: Elevation gain, slope, and temperature.
• <b>Performance Metrics</b>: Distance, speed, power, heart rate, cadence.

<b>YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE</b>
You decide where your data goes. aTrainingTracker puts privacy first while offering the convenience of the cloud.
• <b>Privacy by Default</b>: No account required. No mandatory cloud. Your workouts stay on your device unless you choose otherwise.
• <b>Workout Import</b>: Easily migrate your training history from former devices by importing TCX files.
• <b>Optional Cloud Sync</b>: Automatically upload new activities to Strava or Dropbox for further analysis.
• <b>Popular Formats</b>: Export your workouts as GPX, TCX, CSV and other standard formats.

<b>BUILT FOR SERIOUS ATHLETES</b>
Developed continuously for more than 15 years with one goal: making activity recording as reliable and automatic as possible. No social noise, no distractions—just excellent tracking.

You focus on your training. aTrainingTracker takes care of the recording.
