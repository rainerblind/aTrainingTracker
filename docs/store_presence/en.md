**Full Description**:
<b>aTrainingTracker – The Intelligent Sports Tracker for Your Smartphone</b>

Focus on your training. We'll handle the technology.

aTrainingTracker turns your Android device into a professional, privacy-first bike computer and multi-sport tracker. Built for cyclists, runners, and endurance athletes who demand precision telemetry, intelligent automation, and complete data sovereignty. No mandatory cloud, no account required, and 100% ad-free.

<b>INTELLIGENT SENSOR MANAGEMENT & HARDWARE ARBITRATION</b>
Connect multiple Bluetooth® LE and ANT+® sensors simultaneously: heart rate, cadence, speed, cycling power, and temperature.
• <b>Intelligent Priority</b>: Dedicated wheel speed sensors are automatically preferred over GPS for millimeter precision.
• <b>Seamless Failover</b>: If a speed sensor disconnects, GPS immediately takes over without dropping a single meter.
• <b>Dual-Protocol Reliability</b>: Connect modern BLE sensors alongside established ANT+ devices without interference.

<b>ADVANCED SPORTS SCIENCE & PHYSIOLOGY</b>
Professional analytics formerly exclusive to specialized desktop software:
• <b>Coggan Power Suite</b>: Normalized Power (NP), Intensity Factor (IF), Training Stress Score (TSS), and Variability Index (VI).
• <b>Skiba W' Balance</b>: Real-time anaerobic battery tracking with 2-point Critical Power (CP/W') modeling.
• <b>Longitudinal PMC</b>: Track Chronic Training Load (CTL), Acute Training Load (ATL), Training Stress Balance (TSB), and ACWR.
• <b>Aerobic Efficiency</b>: Real-time Efficiency Factor (EF) and Aerobic Decoupling (Pw:Hr %).

<b>NAVIGATION, LIEBLINGSSTRECKEN & LIVE SEGMENTS</b>
• <b>Route Clusters (Lieblingsstrecken)</b>: Repeated training loops are recognized, clustered, and auto-named with automatic personal best comparisons.
• <b>Strava Live Segments & Climbs</b>: Real-time progress against your PR and KOM with live elevation profile scrubbers.
• <b>Track Guidance</b>: Import GPX or Strava routes and follow them on clear vector maps.
• <b>Barometric Precision</b>: Barometric altitude calibration anchored by Known Locations.
• <b>Electronic Bike Bell</b>: Integrated handlebar bell audible over audio cues and music.

<b>PERIODS 2.0 & MULTI-YEAR ANALYTICS</b>
• <b>Activity Heatmaps</b>: GitHub-style yearly contribution grids visualize workout consistency per sport.
• <b>Adaptive Dashboards</b>: Fully customizable Jetpack Compose cockpits with AMOLED Dark mode.

<b>DATA SOVEREIGNTY & CLOUD TRIAD</b>
You own your data. All workouts are stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
• <b>Native .FIT Support</b>: Industry-standard .FIT export and import alongside TCX, GPX, and CSV.
• <b>Cloud Backup Triad</b>: Automated, zero-touch sync to Google Drive, Dropbox, and Strava.
• <b>100% Private & Ad-Free</b>: No account creation, no analytics tracking, no paywalls on core tracking. Supported by an optional Pay-What-You-Want model.

Developed continuously for over 15 years by endurance athletes, for endurance athletes.
