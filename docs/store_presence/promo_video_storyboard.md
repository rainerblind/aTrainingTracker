## Custom Outdoor Handlebar Photo Replacement Guide

Per user sprint screening instruction: *"Maybe, we could also take a photo somewhere else, replay some workout, take a screenshot and put it into the photo?"*

The pipeline is completely modular:
1. **Take Your Outdoor Photo**: Take a clear landscape photograph ($1920 \times 1080$ or higher) showing your bicycle handlebars, stem mount, and scenery (gravel trail, mountain pass, or asphalt road).
2. **Save to Repository**: Place your image at:
   `docs/store_presence/video/custom_handlebar.jpg`
3. **Re-Run Automated Generator**:
   ```bash
   python3 tools/generate_promo_video.py --bg-photo docs/store_presence/video/custom_handlebar.jpg
   ```
4. The pipeline will automatically composite the Pixel 10 bezel and UI screens directly into your custom photo and output the updated showcase MP4!
