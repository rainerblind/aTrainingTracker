## Rule 1: Obey the Rules, Two-Tier Context Refresh & Constructive Resistance
* **Rule 1.1 (Context Refresh at Ticket / Ceremony Start)**: Before starting a new ticket, starting a sprint ceremony, or resuming after a context compaction, the agent SHALL read `.agents/rules/aspice_governance.md` and the relevant domain standard (`docs/design_guidelines.md` for UI tickets, `docs/coding_standards.md` for logic/service/DB tickets) via `read_file` or `view_file`.
* **Rule 1.2 (Intra-Ticket Stage Transitions)**: Transitions performed by `tools/jira_util.py` (`move <KEY> analysis` or `gate-verdict <SUBTASK> PASS`) automatically print the target stage `SKILL.md` and core governance rules to `stdout`, eliminating manual re-reading turns within a ticket unless a context compaction occurs.
* **Rule 1.3 (Constructive Resistance & Documented Deviations)**: Agents SHALL NOT initiate shortcuts. When a human user requests an exception from standard process, the agent SHALL first offer constructive resistance (*Pflicht zum Widerspruch*), explaining the guardrail's purpose and associated risk. If the human insists, the exception, its rationale, and exact changes MUST be cleanly documented in Jira and the deliverable/walkthrough (*Dokumentierte Abweichungserlaubnis*).

## Rule 2: Autonomous Continuous `Schicht` Cadence, Master Orchestration & Lifecycle Transitions
* **Rule 2.1 (Model Allocation — Master Orchestrator, Per-`Schicht` Coordinator & Interactive Checkpoints)**: Under continuous `Schicht` sprints (`ATT-8030` evolved, `.agents/skills/schicht-orchestrator/SKILL.md`), a lightweight **Master Orchestrator** conversation runs on **Gemini 4 Neon (Medium)** (`gemini-4-neon-medium`) and spawns **one fresh top-level conversation per `Schicht`** pinned to **Gemini 4 Neon (High)** (`agentapi new-conversation --model=gemini-4-neon-high --title="Schicht <YYYY-MM-DD_0x_Schichtname>"`, `nestingDepth: 0`). Running each `Schicht` Coordinator on `gemini-4-neon-high` eliminates cross-`Schicht` context compaction drift and guarantees that complex/graphical lane and Stage 6 subagents spawned with `Model: "inherit"` execute on Gemini 4 Neon (High) while routine lanes stay on `Model: "flash"` (`Rule 7.2`). Before spawning, the Master SHALL verify `gemini-4-neon-high` availability via `swarm(action="models", all=true)` and post-check `agentapi get-conversation-metadata` to guard against silent fallback to Flash. Automated sprint rollovers conclude cleanly into `develop` without requiring manual per-ticket review before merge. For standalone interactive sprint sessions, the human Product Owner switches models across phases (Planning on `Argon`/`Carbon`; Phase 2 on `Neon`/`Flash`; Ceremony 2 / Review on `Gemini 4 Carbon`).
* **Rule 2.2 (PO-Aligned 3-`Schicht` Cadence (`05:00` / `15:00` / `22:00`), Fresh Agent per `Schicht` & Sequential Handoff)**: Continuous automated sprints operate on three PO-aligned **`Schicht` windows**: `01_Früh` (`05:00–15:00`, 10h), `02_Spät` (`15:00–22:00`, 7h), and `03_Nacht` (`22:00–05:00`, 7h) local time using sequential naming **`YYYY-MM-DD_0x_Schichtname`** (e.g., `2026-11-16_02_Spät`). Each `Schicht` Coordinator owns **strictly one `Schicht`** and SHALL NOT spawn or start the next `Schicht` itself. At each `Schichtwechsel` (`05:00` / `15:00` / `22:00`), the Master Orchestrator triggers closeout via `agentapi send-message <SCHICHT_CONV_ID> "CLOSE_SCHICHT ..."`. The closing `Schicht` Coordinator freezes merges onto `sprint/<CLOSING_SCHICHT>`, terminates all active lane/Stage-6 subagents cleanly via `manage_subagents(Action="kill_all")`, keeps all completed Stage-6-verified `Final Review (Human)` items with the closing Schicht (archived post-close into `Bereit für PO Abnahme` ID `453` with a `sprint-<SCHICHT_ID>` label and zero artificial WIP cap), runs the Tier-2 Gate, deploys best-effort via `adb installDebug -PstampGitHash=true` if a physical device is attached (`REQ-TST-004` — never blocking rollover if unplugged), records Retro/metrics/gates/QA audit/risk review, **automatically rolls up metrics (`sprint-metrics --record --post-to-retro`) AND retro insights/proposals/QA findings (`post-weekly-rollup --retro-file docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_<KEY>.md`) into the rolling `Wochen Review & Retro` ticket**, merges `sprint/<CLOSING_SCHICHT>` `--no-ff` into `develop`, tags immutably (`sprint/<CLOSING_SCHICHT>` + SUP.8 manifest), notifies the Master via `agentapi send-message <MASTER_ID> "SCHICHT_CLOSED ..."`, and **stops cleanly**. Strictly **after** receiving `SCHICHT_CLOSED` and verifying clean `develop` / Jira state, the Master checks for active carryover WIP or eligible tickets in `Vom PO Freigegeben` (ID `447`) and — if work exists — spawns a brand-new `Schicht` Coordinator conversation (`--model=gemini-4-neon-high`) which branches `sprint/<NEW_SCHICHT>` off `develop`, carries active WIP (`carryover-wip`), refills open capacity across the 3 lanes from `447` `ORDER BY rank ASC` (`pack-sprint --lanes 3`), records Ceremony 1 Risk Review, and immediately starts Strategy A execution (if both `447` and WIP are empty, the Master skips spawning an empty `Schicht` and waits for the next `Schichtwechsel`).
* **Rule 2.3 (Rollover & In-Progress Priority)**: At Phase 2 start and when replenishing lanes, tickets already in active workflow columns (`Implementation`, `Analysis`, `Test Spec`, `Implementation Plan`, `Test`, `Final Review (Agent)`) SHALL be processed before untouched `Zu erledigen` tickets, ensuring Stage-6-bounced rollover tickets are healed promptly.
* **Rule 2.4 (Just-In-Time Transition to Analysis)**: Untouched sprint tickets SHALL remain in `Zu erledigen` during Ceremony 1. A ticket SHALL transition to `Analysis` (`python3 tools/jira_util.py move <KEY> "analysis"`) strictly just-in-time when Stage 1 begins for that specific ticket.
* **Rule 2.5 (Mid-Sprint Backlog Additions)**: Tickets added by the human user to an active sprint (`status = 'Zu erledigen'`, excluding `Review & Retro`) carry implicit human approval. The Coordinator SHALL transition them to `Analysis` and execute Stages 1–5 autonomously before concluding Phase 2.
* **Rule 2.6 (Autonomous Resume Post-Compaction)**: Upon resuming from context compaction, the agent SHALL query Jira and git state and immediately continue execution without asking the user for direction.
* **Rule 2.7 (Autonomous Schicht Retro Write-Allowlist — REQ-PRO-117)**: During autonomous `Schicht`-end Ceremony 2 (`chore(ceremony2)`) closes without interactive PO authorization, writes are **strictly restricted** to evidence and log paths: `docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_*.md`, `docs/engineering/gates/*`, `docs/engineering/qa_audits/*`, `docs/engineering/reviews/*`, `docs/engineering/test_evidence/*`, `docs/engineering/metrics/process_metrics.jsonl`, and appending Sprint Review Log rows to `docs/management/risk_register.md` (`REQ-STP-010.2`). Modifying protected governance/standard/tooling paths (`.agents/rules/**`, `.agents/skills/**`, `docs/coding_standards.md`, `docs/design_guidelines.md`, `docs/project_governance.md`, `docs/project_protocol.md`, `docs/engineering/rule_history_archive.md`, `tools/**`) during autonomous closes is **strictly prohibited** without an explicit `Interactive-PO:` session or dedicated PO-approved ticket; autonomous Retros wishing to change rules, skills, or standards SHALL record a structured proposal in `docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_<KEY>.md` AND execute `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as coordinator post-weekly-rollup --retro-file docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_<KEY>.md` so every finding and proposal automatically propagates into the rolling `Wochen Review & Retro` ticket before the per-Schicht retro ticket is closed.

## Rule 3: Human Decision Gate on Parent Tickets, Stage 6 Cold Review & Synchronous Progression
* **Rule 3.1 (Human Decision Gate & PO Ticket Review / Abnahme Sessions)**: Transitioning parent tickets to `Erledigt` is reserved exclusively for the human user. Agents SHALL end parent tickets at `Final Review (Human)` (assigned to `human`). During any interactive **PO Ticket Review / Abnahme session**:
  1. **Strict Review Queue (`Bereit für PO Abnahme`, ID `453`) — Never Ask Which Tickets**: Agents SHALL **NEVER** ask the human PO which tickets to review. Always query **exclusively** tickets in **`Bereit für PO Abnahme` (Sprint ID `453`)** (`python3 tools/jira_util.py search "project = ATT AND sprint = 453 AND status = 'Final Review (Human)' ORDER BY rank ASC"`) and begin the strict one-by-one review (`Rule 68` / `Rule 79`) starting with the highest-ranked ticket. Never pull tickets from a currently running `Schicht`, `Vom PO Freigegeben` (`447`), or `Bereit für PO Systemtest` (`454`).
  2. **Mandatory Latest `Schicht`-Stand on Phone Before Ticket #1**: Before presenting the first ticket, the agent SHALL verify that the **latest closed `Schicht`-Stand (`develop` / latest `sprint/*` tag merged into `develop`)** is installed on the attached physical phone (`Pixel 10`, skipping `emulator-*`): compare `adb -s <physical_serial> shell dumpsys package com.atrainingtracker.debug | grep versionName` against `git rev-parse --short develop`. If the phone does not already carry `-<develop_sha>`, compile and install the `develop` baseline (`ANDROID_SERIAL=<physical_serial> ./gradlew installDebug -PstampGitHash=true`, using a clean detached worktree at `develop` if the main checkout is dirty or running an active `Schicht` so active work is undisturbed) and launch `com.atrainingtracker.debug`. If no physical phone is attached, inform the PO immediately before starting UI ticket reviews.
  3. **Weekly PO Decision Session & Signed Baseline (`ATT-9545`)**: Replacing unsustainable per-Schicht Layer-4 human reviews, the PO conducts one prepared **30–60 minute weekly decision session** using `python3 tools/po_weekly_session.py generate-digest --week <YYYY-KWnn>` (REQ/SYSREQ diff, release scope changes, 2 randomly sampled tickets with evidence bundles, open deviations, weekly method scorecard, candidate baseline SHA) and records decisions via `python3 tools/po_weekly_session.py record-session --week <YYYY-KWnn> --baseline-sha <SHA> --minutes <N> --tag --post-jira` (persisting authentic `Human-Approved` baseline records and measured PO minutes in `docs/engineering/reviews/po_sessions.jsonl`). If a week passes without a PO session, Ceremony 2 SHALL record an explicit open deviation **`PO session not performed`** (`--not-performed`) — NEVER a synthetic approval.
  4. **Mandatory Follow-Up Linking vs. *Beifang* Isolation (`ATT-8223`)**: Whenever a reviewed ticket is accepted (`Erledigt`) with a **direct** follow-up defect or refinement ticket, the agent SHALL link the newly created ticket to the original reviewed ticket via `python3 tools/jira_util.py create-issue ... --follow-up-of=<ORIGINAL_KEY>` (creating a Jira `Relates` link + `po-review-followup` label + cross-reference comment on `<ORIGINAL_KEY>`). This allows independent auditors and `method_scorecard.py` to distinguish **Clean Acceptance** (`0` linked follow-ups), **Accepted with Refinement** (linked `Verbesserung`/`Feature`), and **Accepted with PO-Caught Defect Escape** (linked `Bug`) — preventing critical PO reviews from being misread as *"durchwinken"*. **Strict *Beifang* Rule**: Unrelated incidental findings or ideas spotted elsewhere during a review session (*Beifang*) SHALL **NEVER** be linked to the currently reviewed ticket (`--follow-up-of` MUST be omitted) so unrelated findings never pollute the reviewed ticket's acceptance or escape metrics.
  5. **Mandatory PO Review Comment Persistence on Every Reviewed Ticket (`ATT-8223`)**: Whenever the human PO gives a review comment or evaluation remark for a ticket during a review session (whether accepted `i.O.`, accepted with follow-up, deferred field test, or rejected `n.i.O.`), the agent SHALL post that PO comment verbatim/faithfully as a Jira comment on the reviewed ticket (`python3 tools/jira_util.py --as coordinator comment <KEY> "PO Review (<YYYY-MM-DD>): <PO comment & verdict>"`) before advancing to the next ticket, preserving an authentic timestamped human-review audit trail (`SUP.10`).
* **Rule 3.2 (Tiered Automated Gates & Sub-Task Progression — `ATT-9543`)**: Under Lean AAA v2, low-signal agent2 prose Gates 1–5 are replaced by deterministic machine checks (`python3 tools/auto_gate.py --ticket <KEY> --tier <tier-a|tier-b|tier-c> --advance-jira` — build, dependency-based unit tests, static safety linters, deliverable placeholder/link lint, and conflict-marker check), recording strictly tool-generated structured gate tables in Jira (no free-text agent opinion):
  * **`tier-b` & `tier-c`**: Passing `tools/auto_gate.py` advances the parent ticket directly to **`Final Review (Agent)`** (Stage 6) without agent2 prose Gate 1–5 subtasks.
  * **`tier-a` (High Risk)**: Retains **1 pre-implementation architectural/plan review** (`agent2` Gate 3 `gate-verdict <SUBTASK_KEY> PASS`) prior to Stage 4 coding, followed by `tools/auto_gate.py` and **Stage 6 Cold Review**.
  * Any legacy/Tier-A stage sub-task that passes gate review closes directly to `Erledigt` via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 gate-verdict <SUBTASK_KEY> PASS "<Audit Report>"`, synchronously advancing the parent ticket (`Test Spec` $\rightarrow$ `Implementation Plan` $\rightarrow$ `Implementation` $\rightarrow$ `Test` $\rightarrow$ `Final Review (Agent)`).
* **Rule 3.3 (Stage 6 Cold Final Review — Second-Vendor Claude on Tier A, 5-Bounce Healing, Emulator/Simulator Verification & Post-PASS Integration — `ATT-9546`)**: After Gate 5 / `tools/auto_gate.py` `PASS`, `feature/<KEY>` SHALL remain intact (NOT prematurely squashed onto `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>`). A freshly spawned Cold reviewer (`stage6-final-review/SKILL.md`) SHALL audit the ticket diff via `git diff sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>...feature/<KEY>`, inspect all created/modified/referenced `.png` screenshots via `read_file`, and — for all Product / UI / Sensor / Service / Database tickets — **deploy and verify the `feature/<KEY>` build live on `emulator-5554` (`ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5554 ./gradlew installDebug` after `python3 tools/agent_emulator.py ensure`) and/or sensor simulators (`tools/ble_radar_simulator.py`, seeded workout DB)**, exercising the interactive user flow via `adb`, verifying `adb -s emulator-5554 logcat` is free of crashes/exceptions/stalls (`ForgottenCoroutineScopeException`, ANRs), and visually inspecting live `screencap` output via `read_file` (ASPICE `SWE.6` / `SYS.5` independent target verification). Pure Python/markdown governance tickets without app runtime impact may skip APK deployment while still auditing all diffed files and images. **Second-Vendor Independence (`ATT-9546`)**: For **`tier-a`** tickets, the Coordinator SHALL spawn an independent **Anthropic Claude** Cold Reviewer (`agentapi new-conversation --model=claude-sonnet-5-5-high`, NO GPT models) passing diff + evidence bundle path; if Claude weekly quota is exhausted (`swarm(action="models", all=true)` reports `0%`), fall back to **Gemini 4 Neon (High)** with `--vendor google --model gemini-4-neon-high --allow-same-vendor-fallback --fallback-reason=claude_quota_exhausted` so `[STAGE6-PROVENANCE ...]` records Claude vs. Gemini-fallback defect catch rates empirically. The Cold reviewer executes `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 final-review-verdict <PARENT_KEY> PASS|BOUNCE ... --vendor <anthropic|google> --model <model_id>` (with up to **5 autonomous healing bounces** back to the implementing pair on `feature/<KEY>`). ONLY upon Stage 6 `PASS` SHALL `--merge-ticket` squash `feature/<KEY>` onto `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>` as a single atomic commit before advancing the ticket to `Final Review (Human)` (`sprint-<SPRINT_ID>` label, zero artificial WIP cap). Non-Tier-A model selection obeys `Rule 7.2` (Cold `Flash` for `tier-c` / routine; Cold `Pro` / Neon `inherit` for complex or graphical `tier-b` tasks or Bounce 2–5 escalations).

## Rule 5: Ticket Creation Stops Immediately
* **Rule 5.1 (Immediate Stop on Backlog Ticket Creation)**: When creating a backlog or follow-up ticket (`python3 tools/jira_util.py create-issue ...`, which automatically ranks it at the top of the backlog), the agent SHALL report the new issue key and stop immediately. Branching, creating sub-tasks, or starting implementation is prohibited unless explicitly instructed.

## Rule 6: Unattended Escalation via Jira
* **Rule 6.1 (Unattended Mid-Sprint Escalation)**: If a requirement ambiguity or design decision blocks a sub-task mid-sprint, the agent SHALL NOT block the console. The agent SHALL:
  1. `python3 tools/jira_util.py comment <SUBTASK_KEY> "Question for Human: ..."`
  2. `python3 tools/jira_util.py assign <SUBTASK_KEY> human`
  3. Immediately continue with the next unblocked ticket in the active sprint backlog.

## Rule 7: Asymmetric Zipper Principle & Persistent Subagent Execution
* **Rule 7.1 (Separated Persistent Ping-Pong Pair & Complexity/Visual Escalation)**: Each lane maintains two distinct persistent `TypeName: "self"` subagents (`author` `--as agent1` for `submit-stage`, `reviewer` `--as agent2` for `gate-verdict`) so no actor audits its own work (`REQ-PRO-107.1`). Routine Stages 1–5 use **`Flash`** (`Model: "flash"`) per `Rule 7.2`; **`Pro` / Gemini 4 Neon** (`Model: "inherit"` when the Coordinator session runs on Gemini 4 Neon, otherwise `Model: "pro"`) is used **only for complex or graphical tasks**:

| Stage | Doing (`--as agent1` `submit-stage`) | Gate Review (`--as agent2` `gate-verdict`) | Model Selection (`Rule 7.2`) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Stage 1–5** (`Analysis` $\rightarrow$ `Test`) | **`lane_author`** | **`lane_reviewer`** | **`Flash`** for routine tickets; **`Pro` / Neon (`inherit`)** only for complex (High-Risk / multi-component / concurrency / DB / sensor-protocol) or graphical (`Rule 8.5` UI/Canvas/Chart/Icon) tasks |
| **Stage 6: Cold Final Review (Agent)** (`Final Review (Agent)` $\rightarrow$ Gate 6) | *(Feature branch `feature/<KEY>`)* | **Fresh Cold Reviewer** | **`Flash`** for routine/low-risk/tooling/doc/single-component; **`Pro` / Neon (`inherit`)** only for **complex** or **graphical** tasks (or Bounce 2–3 escalations); audits `git diff sprint/<SPRINT>...feature/<KEY>`, performs incremental targeted tests + live verification on `emulator-5554` / simulator + `read_file` screenshot critique, and merges via `--merge-ticket` ONLY on `PASS` |

* **Rule 7.2 (3-Lane Execution & `Pro` / Neon Routing for Complex/Graphical Tasks Only)**: Execute **3 parallel lanes** across disjoint Parent Epics: **Lane A (`ATT-8138` ASPICE 4.0 Remediation / Tooling)** and **Lanes B & C (Disjoint Product Features & Bugfixes)** — each with a dedicated alternating independent **`Flash`** (`Model: "flash"`) subagent pair (`--as agent1` Doing vs `--as agent2` Gate Review). Use **`Pro` / Gemini 4 Neon** (`Model: "inherit"` when the Coordinator runs on Neon, otherwise `Model: "pro"`) **strictly for complex tasks** (High-Risk / multi-component architectural, concurrency/DB/sensor-protocol changes, or Stage 6 Bounce 2–3 escalations) **or graphical tasks** (UI/Compose/Canvas/Chart/Icon visual tasks subject to `Rule 8.5`).
* **Rule 7.3 (Proactive 3-Ticket Rotation & Error Replacement)**: When an Epic contains $\ge 4$ tickets in a sprint, the Coordinator SHALL rotate (terminate and respawn) the Epic-scoped subagent pair after every 3 completed tickets to prevent context window saturation. If a subagent enters an errored state or times out, terminate it immediately and spawn a fresh replacement.
* **Rule 7.4 (Separated Review Authorship & Concise Deliverables — REQ-PRO-107.1, REQ-PRO-107.4)**: Same-agent fused handoffs (where the Gate N reviewer immediately authors Stage N+1 on the same ticket) are formally deprecated per ASPICE 4.0 Finding M3. Subagent pairs SHALL alternate between Doing (`--as agent1`, `submit-stage`) and Gate Review (`--as agent2`, `gate-verdict`), ensuring that the Stage N+1 author and Gate N reviewer are distinct actors. Markdown deliverables (`analysis`, `test_spec`, `plan`, `walkthrough`) SHALL adhere to the concise cap ($\le 60\text{--}80$ lines).
* **Rule 7.5 (Two-Tier Unit Test & Lint Execution — Rule 85 Unthrottled)**: Agents SHALL run Tier 1 targeted unit tests **strictly** matching `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest --rerun --tests <NewOrModifiedTestClasses>` during Stages 4, 5, and 6 — **NEVER `--rerun-tasks`** (which wipes Gradle's incremental Kotlin/Java compile cache and burns ~6–8 min per run; `--rerun` keeps compilation incremental while still forcing fresh test execution in `~15–45s`). Run `TranslationParityTest` **only** if `values*/strings*.xml` changed and `:app:compileDebugAndroidTestKotlin` **only** if `androidTest/` changed. **Zero redundant Stage 5 / Gate 5 Gradle re-runs on unchanged code SHA**: if Stage 5 only promotes living docs (`[Implemented]` $\rightarrow$ `[Verified]`) and authors `walkthrough.md` without modifying production/test code since Gate 4, reuse Gate 4's verified test evidence directly. Full-repo contract tests (`ThemeColorCentralizationAuditTest`, `SourceTextReadingAuditContractTest`, `AspiceSweAuditContractTest`, `MeasuredNfrBenchmarkTest`, `System*ContractTest`, `TranslationParityTest`) and integration/AndroidTest-compile checks are deferred to the **Lean Tier 2 `Schichtwechsel` Gate** once per `Schicht` at `05:00`/`15:00`/`22:00` (`./gradlew :app:assembleDebug :app:compileDebugAndroidTestKotlin :app:testDebugUnitTest --rerun --tests <SprintTouchedSuites> --tests "com.atrainingtracker.trainingtracker.build.*" --tests "com.atrainingtracker.trainingtracker.integration.*" --tests "com.atrainingtracker.trainingtracker.localization.*" --max-workers=1 && python3 tools/verify_requirement_governance.py`, plus Python `tools/test_*.py` and safety linters; `export-test-evidence --no-kover`). The **full** `:app:testDebugUnitTest` suite alongside `:app:lintDebug`, `:app:detekt`, and `:app:koverXmlReportDebug` runs **once a week** (within the weekly Dual-Vendor Code Review / ASPICE Audit / `Wochen Review & Retro`, `Rule 3.5`) and on releases. Full-suite testing per-ticket is restricted strictly to High-Risk changes touching core shared infrastructure.
* **Rule 7.6 (Two-Tier Device Policy & Hardware Evidence Integrity — REQ-TST-004, REQ-PRO-098, REQ-STB-022)**: During Stages 1–5, autonomous agents SHALL deploy and verify exclusively on an emulated device (`emulator-5554`, `Pixel_8_Android_15` initialized via `python3 tools/agent_emulator.py ensure`). * **Rule 8.1 (Composite Stage CLI)**: Agents SHALL submit stages via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent1 submit-stage <PARENT_KEY> <1..5> <TEXT_OR_@FILE>` and record gate verdicts via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 gate-verdict <SUBTASK_KEY> <PASS|REVISION> <TEXT_OR_@FILE>`.

Story Points (`customfield_10016`) SHALL strictly use canonical Fibonacci numbers **`{0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21}`** (enforced in `tools/jira_util.py` `set-points` and `create-issue --points`; non-Fibonacci or fractional values are rejected). Per-ticket sizing obeys a 3-tier Complexity Split Guardrail (`REQ-PRO-029`): **`0–5 SP`** allowed directly; **`8 SP`** requires a **PO decision discussed at ticket creation time** — whenever an agent proposes/creates a new ticket estimated at `8 SP`, it SHALL first discuss with the PO whether to keep it as one cohesive `8 SP` ticket or split it into smaller Fibonacci tickets (`<= 5 SP`) before calling `create-issue` (once approved and placed by the PO into `Vom PO Freigegeben` ID `447` or an active sprint, execution proceeds without blocking; if Stage 1 uncovers `8 SP` scope on a ticket originally `<= 5 SP`, escalate via `Rule 6.1`); **`> 8 SP` (`13, 21 SP`)** mandates splitting into smaller Fibonacci tickets before entering `Analysis` or an active sprint. **Risk-Tier Classification (`ATT-9540`)**: Every product/process ticket SHALL carry a canonical Jira risk-tier label assigned at creation/refinement (`python3 tools/jira_util.py --as coordinator set-tier <KEY> <tier-a|tier-b|tier-c>`): **`tier-a`** (High Risk: sensors/BLE/ANT+, fusion/EKF, concurrency, persistence/DB migrations, data loss, crash/ANR, release work), **`tier-b`** (Standard: normal features and bugs), **`tier-c`** (Low Risk: UI-only tweaks, docs, tooling, test-only).
