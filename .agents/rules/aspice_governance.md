# Agile Agentic ASPICE (AAA) In-Sprint Core Governance Rules: aTrainingTracker

These **9 Core Rules** operationalize **Agile Agentic ASPICE (AAA)** (Tripwires, Infinite Context, and Constructive Resistance) and are strictly binding on all AI agents during in-sprint execution.

## Rule 1: Obey the Rules, Two-Tier Context Refresh & Constructive Resistance
* **Rule 1.1 (Context Refresh at Ticket / Ceremony Start)**: Before starting a new ticket, starting a sprint ceremony, or resuming after a context compaction, the agent SHALL read `.agents/rules/aspice_governance.md` and the relevant domain standard (`docs/design_guidelines.md` for UI tickets, `docs/coding_standards.md` for logic/service/DB tickets) via `read_file` or `view_file`.
* **Rule 1.2 (Intra-Ticket Stage Transitions)**: Transitions performed by `tools/jira_util.py` (`move <KEY> analysis` or `gate-verdict <SUBTASK> PASS`) automatically print the target stage `SKILL.md` and core governance rules to `stdout`, eliminating manual re-reading turns within a ticket unless a context compaction occurs.
* **Rule 1.3 (Constructive Resistance & Documented Deviations)**: Agents SHALL NOT initiate shortcuts. When a human user requests an exception from standard process, the agent SHALL first offer constructive resistance (*Pflicht zum Widerspruch*), explaining the guardrail's purpose and associated risk. If the human insists, the exception, its rationale, and exact changes MUST be cleanly documented in Jira and the deliverable/walkthrough (*Dokumentierte Abweichungserlaubnis*).

## Rule 2: Autonomous 12-Hour Sprint Cadence, PO Checkpoints & Lifecycle Transitions
* **Rule 2.1 (PO Coordinator Model-Switch Checkpoints)**: For interactive sprint phases, the human Product Owner switches the top-level Coordinator model across phases: Planning / Ceremony 1 on `Argon` or `Carbon`; Phase 2 Execution on `Flash`; Ceremony 2 / Review on `Gemini 4 Carbon`. Under continuous 12-hour sprints (`ATT-8030`), automated sprint rollovers conclude cleanly into `develop` without requiring manual per-ticket review before merge.
* **Rule 2.2 (Fixed 12-Hour Cadence, Hard Cutoffs & Start-Cutoff Reserve)**: Continuous automated sprints operate on fixed **12-hour windows closing at `04:00` and `16:00`** local time using sequential ISO-week naming (`YYYY-WW.N`). During Phase 2, the Coordinator executes `ORDER BY rank ASC` continuously without pausing to ask for continuation. At **`03:25` / `15:25`** (35-minute reserve), agents SHALL NOT start new `Zu erledigen` tickets when `remaining_time < est + 35 min`; untouched `Zu erledigen` sprint tickets SHALL be returned cleanly to the top of `Vom PO Freigegeben` (Sprint ID `447`), active WIP drained through Stage 6 Cold Review, the Tier-2 Gate completed, `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>` merged `--no-ff` into `develop`, tagged immutably, and installed via `adb` onto any attached physical device (`REQ-TST-004`). Completed work in `Final Review (Human)` carries a `sprint-<SPRINT_ID>` label with zero artificial WIP/overflow cap; if `Vom PO Freigegeben` has eligible tickets, the Coordinator starts the next 12h sprint, or pauses cleanly when empty.
* **Rule 2.3 (Rollover & In-Progress Priority)**: At Phase 2 start and when replenishing lanes, tickets already in active workflow columns (`Implementation`, `Analysis`, `Test Spec`, `Implementation Plan`, `Test`, `Final Review (Agent)`) SHALL be processed before untouched `Zu erledigen` tickets, ensuring Stage-6-bounced rollover tickets are healed promptly.
* **Rule 2.4 (Just-In-Time Transition to Analysis)**: Untouched sprint tickets SHALL remain in `Zu erledigen` during Ceremony 1. A ticket SHALL transition to `Analysis` (`python3 tools/jira_util.py move <KEY> "analysis"`) strictly just-in-time when Stage 1 begins for that specific ticket.
* **Rule 2.5 (Mid-Sprint Backlog Additions)**: Tickets added by the human user to an active sprint (`status = 'Zu erledigen'`, excluding `Review & Retro`) carry implicit human approval. The Coordinator SHALL transition them to `Analysis` and execute Stages 1–5 autonomously before concluding Phase 2.
* **Rule 2.6 (Autonomous Resume Post-Compaction)**: Upon resuming from context compaction, the agent SHALL query Jira and git state and immediately continue execution without asking the user for direction.

## Rule 3: Human Decision Gate on Parent Tickets, Stage 6 Cold Review & Synchronous Progression
* **Rule 3.1 (Human Decision Gate)**: Transitioning parent tickets to `Erledigt` is reserved exclusively for the human user. Agents SHALL end parent tickets at `Final Review (Human)` (assigned to `human`).
* **Rule 3.2 (Sub-Task Progression)**: Stage sub-tasks (1–5) that pass gate review SHALL be closed directly to `Erledigt` via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 gate-verdict <SUBTASK_KEY> PASS "<Audit Report>"`, which synchronously advances the parent ticket to the next stage column (`Test Spec` $\rightarrow$ `Implementation Plan` $\rightarrow$ `Implementation` $\rightarrow$ `Test` $\rightarrow$ `Final Review (Agent)`).
* **Rule 3.3 (Stage 6 Cold Carbon Review — Strictly Read-Only Zero-Build Invariant)**: Once squashed onto `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>`, a freshly spawned Cold `Gemini 4 Carbon` (`Model: "pro"`) reviewer (`stage6-final-review/SKILL.md`) SHALL audit the squashed commit strictly read-only on the Stage-5-verified SHA — **ZERO `./gradlew` invocations and ZERO `/tmp/att_build_merge.lock` acquisitions** in Stage 6 — and execute `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 final-review-verdict <PARENT_KEY> PASS|BOUNCE ...` (with up to 3 autonomous healing bounces back to the implementing pair) before advancing the ticket to `Final Review (Human)` (`sprint-<SPRINT_ID>` label, zero artificial WIP cap).
* **Rule 3.4 (Mandatory Stage 6 Review Questions)**: Every Stage 6 Cold Review verdict report MUST structure and answer: (1) `*Pros (Was ist gut/gelungen?)*`, (2) `*Cons / Auffälligkeiten / Defects Identified (Was ist kritisch, verbesserungsbedürftig oder unvollständig?)*`, and (3) `*Würdest du das freigeben? (Freigabe-Empfehlung)*` (`JA — Freigabe empfohlen` or `NEIN — BOUNCE`).
* **Rule 3.5 (Mandatory Weekly Dual-Vendor Full-Subsystem Reviews — REQ-PRO-095)**: In addition to in-sprint single-ticket Stage 6 diff reviews, agents and tooling SHALL enforce mandatory weekly holistic code & architecture reviews performed jointly by **both Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude** across full subsystem package slices (`tracker/`, `banalservice/`, `database/`, `exporter/`, `ui/tracking/`, `ui/aftermath/`, `ui/settings/`, `tools/`, `test/`) alongside recent delta commits. Subsystem audit coverage is tracked in `docs/engineering/code_reviews/coverage_matrix.md`; when `> 7 days` elapse or unreviewed packages remain, `python3 tools/jira_util.py ensure-weekly-reviews` automatically creates the review ticket under `ATT-232` and ranks it at `rank-top`.

## Rule 4: Code Lock Until Gate 3 Passes
* **Rule 4.1 (Gate 3 Pre-Implementation Verification)**: Before creating or modifying production source code in `app/src/...` during Stage 4, the agent SHALL execute:
  ```bash
  python3 tools/jira_util.py check-gate <Impl-Plan-Subtask-Key>
  ```
  and confirm exit code `0` (`GATE_PASSED`). Production code edits are strictly prohibited while Gate 3 is blocked.

## Rule 5: Ticket Creation Stops Immediately
* **Rule 5.1 (Immediate Stop on Backlog Ticket Creation)**: When creating a backlog or follow-up ticket (`python3 tools/jira_util.py create-issue ...`, which automatically ranks it at the top of the backlog), the agent SHALL report the new issue key and stop immediately. Branching, creating sub-tasks, or starting implementation is prohibited unless explicitly instructed.

## Rule 6: Unattended Escalation via Jira
* **Rule 6.1 (Unattended Mid-Sprint Escalation)**: If a requirement ambiguity or design decision blocks a sub-task mid-sprint, the agent SHALL NOT block the console. The agent SHALL:
  1. `python3 tools/jira_util.py comment <SUBTASK_KEY> "Question for Human: ..."`
  2. `python3 tools/jira_util.py assign <SUBTASK_KEY> human`
  3. Immediately continue with the next unblocked ticket in the active sprint backlog.

## Rule 7: Asymmetric Zipper Principle & Persistent Subagent Execution
* **Rule 7.1 (Asymmetric Model Zipper Principle)**: Subagents alternate between Doing (`--as agent1`, `submit-stage`) and Gate Review (`--as agent2`, `gate-verdict`) so no model tier audits its own work:

| Stage | Doing (`--as agent1` `submit-stage`) | Gate Review (`--as agent2` `gate-verdict`) | Context Synergy & Fused Handoff |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Analysis** (`[Analysis]` $\rightarrow$ Gate 1) | **`subagent_pro`** | **`subagent_flash`** *(pre-warmed)* | `subagent_flash` audits Gate 1 (`--as agent2`) and immediately executes Stage 2 Doing (`--as agent1`) in the same turn upon `PASS` |
| **Stage 2: Req & Test Spec** (`[Req & Test Spec]` $\rightarrow$ Gate 2) | **`subagent_flash`** | **`subagent_pro`** | `subagent_pro` audits Gate 2 (`--as agent2`) and immediately executes Stage 3 Doing (`--as agent1`) in the same turn upon `PASS` |
| **Stage 3: Implementation Plan** (`[Impl-Plan]` $\rightarrow$ Gate 3) | **`subagent_pro`** | **`subagent_flash`** | `subagent_flash` audits Gate 3 (`--as agent2`) and (for Standard tickets) immediately executes Stage 4 Doing (`--as agent1`) in the same turn upon `PASS` |
| **Stage 4: Implementation** (`[Implementation]` $\rightarrow$ Gate 4) | **`subagent_flash`** *(Standard)* / **`subagent_pro`** *(High-Risk & All Visual/UI)* | **`subagent_pro`** *(Standard)* / **`subagent_flash`** *(High-Risk & Visual/UI)* | `subagent_pro` executes Stage 4 Doing for Visual/UI/High-Risk tickets (with 4-point visual inspection before `submit-stage 4`), or conducts adversarial code review for Standard tickets |
| **Stage 5: Verification** (`[Test]` $\rightarrow$ Gate 5) | **`subagent_flash`** | **`subagent_pro`** | `subagent_flash` runs Tier 1 verification & walkthrough; `subagent_pro` visually inspects every image via `read_file` and executes Gate 5 `PASS` & merge |
| **Stage 6: Cold Final Review (Agent)** (`Final Review (Agent)` $\rightarrow$ Gate 6) | *(Integrated commit)* | **Fresh Cold `Gemini 4 Carbon` (`Model: "pro"`)** | Audits squashed commit and answers mandatory review questions via `final-review-verdict <KEY> PASS\|BOUNCE` |

* **Rule 7.2 (Epic-Scoped Persistent Subagents & Background Pre-Warming)**: Maintain a dedicated pair of persistent `self` subagents (`subagent_pro` with `Model: "pro"`, `subagent_flash` with `Model: "flash"`) per Parent Epic across the sprint. When starting the first ticket of an Epic, launch both subagents concurrently; `subagent_flash` pre-warms its context in the background during Stage 1. Subsequent tickets in the same Epic reuse the existing warm pair via `send_message`.
* **Rule 7.3 (Proactive 3-Ticket Rotation & Error Replacement)**: When an Epic contains $\ge 4$ tickets in a sprint, the Coordinator SHALL rotate (terminate and respawn) the Epic-scoped `(subagent_pro, subagent_flash)` pair after every 3 completed tickets to prevent context window saturation. If a subagent enters an errored state or times out, terminate it immediately and spawn a fresh replacement.
* **Rule 7.4 (Fused Same-Agent Handoffs & Concise Deliverables)**: Subagents executing Gate 1 $\rightarrow$ Stage 2, Gate 2 $\rightarrow$ Stage 3, and Gate 3 $\rightarrow$ Stage 4 SHALL execute the Gate Review and subsequent Stage Doing in the same turn upon `PASS`. Markdown deliverables (`analysis`, `test_spec`, `plan`, `walkthrough`) SHALL adhere to the concise cap ($\le 60\text{--}80$ lines).
* **Rule 7.5 (Two-Tier Unit Test & Lint Execution — Rule 85 Unthrottled)**: Agents SHALL run Tier 1 targeted unit tests **strictly** matching `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest --tests <NewOrModifiedTestClasses>` during Stages 4 & 5 (`TranslationParityTest` **only** if `values*/strings*.xml` changed; `:app:compileDebugAndroidTestKotlin` **only** if `androidTest/` changed; zero redundant Stage 5 re-runs on unchanged SHA). `:app:lintDebug`, `:app:detekt`, and full-repo contract tests (`ThemeColorCentralizationAuditTest`, `SourceTextReadingAuditContractTest`, `AspiceSweAuditContractTest`) are deferred strictly to the Tier 2 full-suite Sprint Integration Gate (`./gradlew testDebugUnitTest :app:lintDebug :app:detekt :app:compileDebugAndroidTestKotlin assembleDebug --continue && python3 tools/verify_requirement_governance.py`) once per sprint at the `04:00`/`16:00` cutoff (or end of Phase 2). Full-suite testing per-ticket is restricted strictly to High-Risk changes touching core shared infrastructure.
* **Rule 7.6 (Two-Tier Device Policy — REQ-TST-004)**: During Stages 1–5, autonomous agents SHALL deploy and verify exclusively on an emulated device (`emulator-5554`, `Pixel_8_Android_15` initialized via `python3 tools/agent_emulator.py ensure`). Autonomous agents are strictly prohibited from deploying to physical devices in Phase 2. At Ceremony 2 (Sprint Review), the integrated sprint build on `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>` SHALL be deployed to and evaluated on the physical device (`Pixel 10`, `ANDROID_SERIAL=<physical_serial> ./gradlew installDebug`). UI/Compose/service tickets SHALL verify on `emulator-5554` launching `com.atrainingtracker.debug` and dumping UI coordinates via `adb shell uiautomator dump` before tapping.
* **Rule 7.7 (Connected User Device Data Protection)**: Running `adb uninstall` or `adb shell pm clear` on `com.atrainingtracker` or `com.atrainingtracker.debug` without explicit human confirmation naming the exact package and first pulling a verified `.attbackup` archive is strictly prohibited.
* **Rule 7.8 (Reactive Turn Boundaries & Hub-and-Spoke Topology)**: Subagent dispatch (`invoke_subagent` or `send_message`) MUST be the final tool call of a Coordinator turn. Subagents SHALL report back strictly to the Coordinator; peer-to-peer `send_message` communication between subagents is strictly prohibited.
* **Rule 7.9 (Lost-Wakeup Watchdog & Coordinator Ownership)**: After each subagent dispatch, the Coordinator SHALL set exactly one watchdog timer via `schedule(DurationSeconds=1200, TimerCondition=<subagent id>)`. Subagents SHALL NEVER assume the Coordinator role.
* **Rule 7.10 (Subagent Lifecycle Cleanup)**: Standalone ticket subagents SHALL be terminated upon squash-merging to the sprint branch. Epic-scoped subagent pairs SHALL be terminated once all sprint tickets in that Epic reach `Final Review (Human)`.

## Rule 8: One Atomic Commit per Ticket & Engineering Hygiene
* **Rule 8.1 (Composite Stage CLI)**: Agents SHALL submit stages via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent1 submit-stage <PARENT_KEY> <1..5> <TEXT_OR_@FILE>` and record gate verdicts via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 gate-verdict <SUBTASK_KEY> <PASS|REVISION> <TEXT_OR_@FILE>`.
* **Rule 8.2 (Single Atomic Commit per Ticket)**: Each completed ticket SHALL be integrated into `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>` as strictly one squashed atomic commit (`git merge --squash feature/<KEY> && git commit -m "<KEY>: <Summary>" && git branch -D feature/<KEY>`).
* **Rule 8.3 (Jira Hygiene & Lösungsversion Governance)**: Sub-tasks SHALL NOT have a `FixVersion` (`Lösungsversion`). Parent tickets SHALL receive `FixVersion` only upon final human acceptance in Ceremony 2. The `Target Release` in Stage 1–5 deliverables MUST reference the active unreleased version reported by `python3 tools/jira_util.py versions`.
* **Rule 8.4 (Canonical Artifact Directory Layout)**: Artifacts in `docs/engineering/` SHALL reside exclusively within canonical subdirectories: `analysis/`, `test_specs/`, `plans/`, `walkthroughs/`, `retros/`, `metrics/`, `code_reviews/`, `test_results` symlink, and `rule_history_archive.md`. Stage 4 submits summaries directly to Jira without creating markdown files in `docs/engineering/`.
* **Rule 8.5 (Universal read_file Image Inspection & Visual Self-Inspection Checklist)**: Visual inspection via `read_file` by both `agent1` (Doing) and `agent2` (Gate Review) is mandatory for ALL created, modified, or referenced image assets (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.svg`). For graphical UI/Compose/Icon tickets, `subagent_pro` SHALL capture a PNG screenshot, inspect it via `read_file`, and verify the 4-point Visual Self-Inspection Checklist: (1) *Orientation & Geometry*, (2) *Color Mapping & M3 Track/Stop-Dot Artifacts*, (3) *Typography & Wrapping/Ellipsizing*, and (4) *Touch Ergonomics & Finger Occlusion*.
* **Rule 8.6 (Hard-Enforced ./gradlew Mutual Exclusion & Right-Sized Daemon Resources)**: `./gradlew` enforces POSIX `flock -w 600 /tmp/att_build_merge.lock` directly inside the wrapper script. `gradle.properties` sets `org.gradle.jvmargs=-Xmx4096m`, `kotlin.daemon.jvmargs=-Xmx3072m`, `workers.max=6`, and `daemon.idletimeout=1800000` (30-min warm daemon reuse) while `app/build.gradle` scopes `maxParallelForks = 2` strictly to unit `Test` workers.
* **Rule 8.7 (Documentation Code-Grounding & Verification Obligation)**: Every technical and user-facing statement, filename, symbol, and signature across documentation SHALL be verified against authentic production source code in `app/src/main/` (Documentation Code-Grounding). Cross-document allocations (`SYS.2` $\leftrightarrow$ `SYS.3`) MUST satisfy exact bidirectional set equality. island components and Repository exposure of `*DatabaseManager`/`Cursor` getters are strictly prohibited.
* **Rule 8.8 (Continuous SWE.2 Architecture Maintenance)**: Whenever a ticket introduces a new subsystem, foreground service, database manager, repository, or requirement prefix family, `docs/architecture.md` MUST be updated within that same ticket across Stage 3 and Stage 5 (Continuous SWE.2 Architecture Maintenance).
* **Rule 8.9 (Evidence-Based Living Requirement Status Model)**: Requirements and test specifications SHALL maintain status: `[Planned]`, `[Implemented]`, or `[Verified]`. Master tables enforce 100% mathematical parity across the 4 status columns.

## Rule 9: Parallel Sprint Execution Across Disjoint Epics & Three-Tier Jira Queue
* **Rule 9.1 (Multi-Lane Greedy Bin-Packing & Disjoint Epics Only)**: Concurrent ticket execution (`1..3` lanes) uses Multi-Lane Greedy Bin-Packing scanning **`Vom PO Freigegeben`** (permanent `future` pseudo-sprint ID `447`) strictly in `ORDER BY rank ASC` (never fixed `top[:X]`!) across completely disjoint Parent Epics with zero package and zero living-doc shard overlap. Agents are strictly forbidden from adding/promoting Product Backlog tickets into `Vom PO Freigegeben` (ID `447`); `--as coordinator` may pull approved tickets out of `447` into a starting sprint, return unstarted `Zu erledigen` sprint tickets cleanly to top of `447` at the `03:25`/`15:25` cutoff, and keep `447` positioned immediately above Product Backlog via `/rest/agile/1.0/sprint/{id}/swap`.
* **Rule 9.2 (Worktree Isolation & Submodule Hygiene)**: Parallel subagent pairs operate in isolated Git worktrees (`Workspace: "share"`) on detached `HEAD`. Subagents SHALL NEVER create filesystem symlinks at submodule paths (`ANT-Android-SDKs`) or stage them via `git add -A`.
* **Rule 9.3 (POSIX flock Mutual Exclusion & Deterministic --merge-ticket)**: Gradle builds, unit tests, device installs, and ticket integration merges SHALL be serialized under POSIX `flock -w 600 /tmp/att_build_merge.lock` via `python3 tools/parallel_sprint_lock.py` or `./gradlew`.
* **Rule 9.4 (Domain-Partitioned Localization)**: String resources MAY be partitioned into `app/src/main/res/values*/strings_<domain>.xml` to eliminate merge conflicts during concurrent additions.
* **Rule 9.5 (Deterministic Living Docs Recalculation)**: Master index recalculation (`python3 tools/verify_requirement_governance.py --sync-indexes`) executes automatically within `--merge-ticket` under the merge lock prior to squashing.
