## Rule 1: Obey the Rules, Two-Tier Context Refresh & Constructive Resistance
* **Rule 1.1 (Context Refresh at Ticket / Ceremony Start)**: Before starting a new ticket, starting a sprint ceremony, or resuming after a context compaction, the agent SHALL read `.agents/rules/aspice_governance.md` and the relevant domain standard (`docs/design_guidelines.md` for UI tickets, `docs/coding_standards.md` for logic/service/DB tickets) via `read_file` or `view_file`.
* **Rule 1.2 (Intra-Ticket Stage Transitions)**: Transitions performed by `tools/jira_util.py` (`move <KEY> analysis` or `gate-verdict <SUBTASK> PASS`) automatically print the target stage `SKILL.md` and core governance rules to `stdout`, eliminating manual re-reading turns within a ticket unless a context compaction occurs.
* **Rule 1.3 (Constructive Resistance & Documented Deviations)**: Agents SHALL NOT initiate shortcuts. When a human user requests an exception from standard process, the agent SHALL first offer constructive resistance (*Pflicht zum Widerspruch*), explaining the guardrail's purpose and associated risk. If the human insists, the exception, its rationale, and exact changes MUST be cleanly documented in Jira and the deliverable/walkthrough (*Dokumentierte Abweichungserlaubnis*).

## Rule 2: Autonomous Continuous `Schicht` Cadence, PO Checkpoints & Lifecycle Transitions
* **Rule 2.1 (PO Coordinator Model-Switch Checkpoints)**: For interactive sprint phases, the human Product Owner switches the top-level Coordinator model across phases: Planning / Ceremony 1 on `Argon` or `Carbon`; Phase 2 Execution on `Flash`; Ceremony 2 / Review on `Gemini 4 Carbon`. Under continuous `Schicht` sprints (`ATT-8030` evolved), automated sprint rollovers conclude cleanly into `develop` without requiring manual per-ticket review before merge.
* **Rule 2.2 (PO-Aligned 3-`Schicht` Cadence (`05:00` / `15:00` / `22:00`), Direct Next-`Schicht` Start & Naming)**: Continuous automated sprints operate on three PO-aligned **`Schicht` windows**: `01_Früh` (`05:00–15:00`, 10h), `02_Spät` (`15:00–22:00`, 7h), and `03_Nacht` (`22:00–05:00`, 7h) local time using sequential naming **`YYYY-MM-DD_0x_Schichtname`** (e.g., `2026-11-16_02_Spät`). At each `Schichtwechsel` (`05:00` / `15:00` / `22:00`), freeze merges onto `sprint/<CLOSING_SCHICHT>`, keep all completed Stage-6-verified `Final Review (Human)` items with the closing Schicht (archived post-close into `Bereit für PO Abnahme` ID `453` with a `sprint-<SCHICHT_ID>` label and zero artificial WIP cap), run the Tier-2 Gate, deploy best-effort via `adb installDebug` if a physical device is attached (`REQ-TST-004` — never blocking rollover if unplugged), record Retro/metrics/gates/QA audit/risk review, **automatically roll up metrics (`sprint-metrics --record --post-to-retro`) AND retro insights/proposals/QA findings (`post-weekly-rollup --retro-file docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_<KEY>.md`) into the rolling `Wochen Review & Retro` ticket**, merge `sprint/<CLOSING_SCHICHT>` `--no-ff` into `develop`, and tag immutably (`sprint/<CLOSING_SCHICHT>`). **Directly at the `Schichtwechsel`**, start the next `Schicht`: branch `sprint/<NEW_SCHICHT>` off `develop`, carry active WIP directly into the new Jira Schicht (`carryover-wip`), refill open capacity across the 3 lanes from `Vom PO Freigegeben` (ID `447`) `ORDER BY rank ASC` (`pack-sprint --lanes 3`), record Ceremony 1 Risk Review, and immediately continue/hand over Strategy A execution (or pause cleanly when `447` and WIP are empty).
* **Rule 2.3 (Rollover & In-Progress Priority)**: At Phase 2 start and when replenishing lanes, tickets already in active workflow columns (`Implementation`, `Analysis`, `Test Spec`, `Implementation Plan`, `Test`, `Final Review (Agent)`) SHALL be processed before untouched `Zu erledigen` tickets, ensuring Stage-6-bounced rollover tickets are healed promptly.
* **Rule 2.4 (Just-In-Time Transition to Analysis)**: Untouched sprint tickets SHALL remain in `Zu erledigen` during Ceremony 1. A ticket SHALL transition to `Analysis` (`python3 tools/jira_util.py move <KEY> "analysis"`) strictly just-in-time when Stage 1 begins for that specific ticket.
* **Rule 2.5 (Mid-Sprint Backlog Additions)**: Tickets added by the human user to an active sprint (`status = 'Zu erledigen'`, excluding `Review & Retro`) carry implicit human approval. The Coordinator SHALL transition them to `Analysis` and execute Stages 1–5 autonomously before concluding Phase 2.
* **Rule 2.6 (Autonomous Resume Post-Compaction)**: Upon resuming from context compaction, the agent SHALL query Jira and git state and immediately continue execution without asking the user for direction.
* **Rule 2.7 (Autonomous Schicht Retro Write-Allowlist — REQ-PRO-117)**: During autonomous `Schicht`-end Ceremony 2 (`chore(ceremony2)`) closes without interactive PO authorization, writes are **strictly restricted** to evidence and log paths: `docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_*.md`, `docs/engineering/gates/*`, `docs/engineering/qa_audits/*`, `docs/engineering/reviews/*`, `docs/engineering/test_evidence/*`, `docs/engineering/metrics/process_metrics.jsonl`, and appending Sprint Review Log rows to `docs/management/risk_register.md` (`REQ-STP-010.2`). Modifying protected governance/standard/tooling paths (`.agents/rules/**`, `.agents/skills/**`, `docs/coding_standards.md`, `docs/design_guidelines.md`, `docs/project_governance.md`, `docs/project_protocol.md`, `docs/engineering/rule_history_archive.md`, `tools/**`) during autonomous closes is **strictly prohibited** without an explicit `Interactive-PO:` session or dedicated PO-approved ticket; autonomous Retros wishing to change rules, skills, or standards SHALL record a structured proposal in `docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_<KEY>.md` AND execute `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as coordinator post-weekly-rollup --retro-file docs/engineering/retros/Sprint_Review_and_Retro_<KEY>.md` so every finding and proposal automatically propagates into the rolling `Wochen Review & Retro` ticket before the per-Schicht retro ticket is closed.

## Rule 3: Human Decision Gate on Parent Tickets, Stage 6 Cold Review & Synchronous Progression
* **Rule 3.1 (Human Decision Gate)**: Transitioning parent tickets to `Erledigt` is reserved exclusively for the human user. Agents SHALL end parent tickets at `Final Review (Human)` (assigned to `human`).
* **Rule 3.2 (Sub-Task Progression)**: Stage sub-tasks (1–5) that pass gate review SHALL be closed directly to `Erledigt` via `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 gate-verdict <SUBTASK_KEY> PASS "<Audit Report>"`, which synchronously advances the parent ticket to the next stage column (`Test Spec` $\rightarrow$ `Implementation Plan` $\rightarrow$ `Implementation` $\rightarrow$ `Test` $\rightarrow$ `Final Review (Agent)`).
* **Rule 3.3 (Stage 6 Cold Final Review — Independent Emulator/Simulator Verification, Visual Screenshot Audit & Post-PASS Integration)**: After Gate 5 `PASS`, `feature/<KEY>` SHALL remain intact (NOT prematurely squashed onto `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>`). A freshly spawned Cold reviewer (`stage6-final-review/SKILL.md`) SHALL audit the ticket diff via `git diff sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>...feature/<KEY>`, inspect all created/modified/referenced `.png` screenshots via `read_file`, and — for all Product / UI / Sensor / Service / Database tickets — **deploy and verify the `feature/<KEY>` build live on `emulator-5554` (`ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5554 ./gradlew installDebug` after `python3 tools/agent_emulator.py ensure`) and/or sensor simulators (`tools/ble_radar_simulator.py`, seeded workout DB)**, exercising the interactive user flow via `adb`, verifying `adb -s emulator-5554 logcat` is free of crashes/exceptions/stalls (`ForgottenCoroutineScopeException`, ANRs), and visually inspecting live `screencap` output via `read_file` (ASPICE `SWE.6` / `SYS.5` independent target verification). Pure Python/markdown governance tickets without app runtime impact may skip APK deployment while still auditing all diffed files and images. The Cold reviewer then executes `python3 tools/jira_util.py --as agent2 final-review-verdict <PARENT_KEY> PASS|BOUNCE ...` (with up to 3 autonomous healing bounces back to the implementing pair on `feature/<KEY>`). ONLY upon Stage 6 `PASS` SHALL `--merge-ticket` squash `feature/<KEY>` onto `sprint/<SPRINT_NAME>` as a single atomic commit before advancing the ticket to `Final Review (Human)` (`sprint-<SPRINT_ID>` label, zero artificial WIP cap). Model selection obeys `Rule 7.2` (use Cold `Flash` for routine / low-risk / `1–2 SP` / doc / contract-test / tooling / single-component fixes; use Cold `Pro` only for **complex** [High-Risk / multi-component architectural / concurrency / DB / sensor-protocol / Bounce 2–3 escalations] or **graphical** [UI/Compose/Canvas/Chart/Icon visual] tasks).
* **Rule 3.4 (Mandatory Stage 6 Review Questions)**: Every Stage 6 Cold Review verdict report MUST structure and answer: (1) `*Pros (Was ist gut/gelungen?)*`, (2) `*Cons / Auffälligkeiten / Defects Identified (Was ist kritisch, verbesserungsbedürftig oder unvollständig?)*`, and (3) `*Würdest du das freigeben? (Freigabe-Empfehlung)*` (`JA — Freigabe empfohlen` or `NEIN — BOUNCE`).
* **Rule 3.5 (Mandatory Weekly Dual-Vendor Process & Subsystem Reviews — REQ-PRO-095, REQ-PRO-096)**: In addition to in-sprint single-ticket Stage 6 diff reviews, agents and tooling SHALL enforce mandatory weekly reviews performed jointly by **both Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude** across two distinct pillars: (1) **ASPICE 4.0 Process & Evidence Cross-Audits** (`docs/engineering/aspice_audits/`, `REQ-PRO-096`), and (2) **Holistic Code & Architecture Reviews** across full subsystem package slices (`docs/engineering/code_reviews/`, `REQ-PRO-095`). When `> 7 days` elapse or unreviewed subsystems remain, `python3 tools/jira_util.py ensure-weekly-reviews` automatically creates the due review/audit ticket(s) under `ATT-232` and ranks them at `rank-top`. `:app:lintDebug`, `:app:detekt`, and `:app:koverXmlReportDebug` are coupled directly to these weekly reviews/audits (and releases) so findings are reviewed deliberately and turned into concrete action items (`kover` strictly as a scenario-gap diagnostic for untested edge cases — agents SHALL NEVER write trivial coverage-padding tests solely to inflate coverage metrics).

## Rule 4: Code Lock Until Gate 3 Passes
* **Rule 4.1 (Gate 3 Pre-Implementation Verification)**: Before creating or modifying production source code in `app/src/...` during Stage 4, the agent SHALL execute:
  ```bash
  python3 tools/jira_util.py check-gate <Impl-Plan-Subtask-Key>
  ```
  and confirm exit code `0` (`GATE_PASSED`). Production code edits are strictly prohibited while Gate 3 is blocked.

## Rule 5: Ticket Creation Stops Immediately
* **Rule 5.1 (Immediate Stop on Backlog Ticket Creation)**: When creating a backlog or follow-up ticket (`python3 tools/jira_util.py create-issue ...`, which automatically ranks it at the top of the backlog), the agent SHALL report the new issue key and stop immediately. Branching, creating sub-tasks, or starting implementation is prohibited unless explicitly instructed.

## Rule 6: Unattended Escalation via Jira
* **Rule 6.1 (Unattended Mid-Sprint Escalation)**: If a requirement ambiguity or design decision blocks a sub-task mid-sprint, the agent SHALL NOT block the console. The agent SHALL:
  1. `python3 tools/jira_util.py comment <SUBTASK_KEY> "Question for Human: ..."`
  2. `python3 tools/jira_util.py assign <SUBTASK_KEY> human`
  3. Immediately continue with the next unblocked ticket in the active sprint backlog.

## Rule 9: Parallel Sprint Execution Across Disjoint Epics & Three-Tier Jira Queue
* **Rule 9.1 (Multi-Lane Greedy Bin-Packing & Protected Human Pseudo-Sprints)**: Concurrent ticket execution runs **3 active parallel lanes** across disjoint Parent Epics (`1× ATT-8138 ASPICE/Tooling` + `2× Product Features/Bugs`) with zero package and zero living-doc shard overlap using Multi-Lane Greedy Bin-Packing scanning **`Vom PO Freigegeben`** (permanent `future` pseudo-sprint ID `447`) strictly in `ORDER BY rank ASC` (never fixed `top[:X]`!) and automatically skipping any `human-execution` tickets. Each lane accommodates tickets up to the per-lane capacity ceiling (`max_sp_per_lane`, default 8 SP per `Schicht` per `REQ-PRO-097`); excess tickets of saturated Epics remain in `447` without skipping higher-ranked eligible tickets. Agents are strictly forbidden from adding/promoting Product Backlog tickets into `Vom PO Freigegeben` (ID `447`) or pulling/modifying tickets inside the human-owned pseudo-sprints **`Bereit für PO Abnahme`** (ID `453`) and **`Bereit für PO Systemtest`** (ID `454`). `--as coordinator` may pull approved tickets out of `447` into a starting Schicht (`pack-sprint --lanes 3`), carry in-flight active WIP cleanly across Schicht cutoffs (`carryover-wip`), move completed `Final Review (Human)` items into `Bereit für PO Abnahme` (`453`) post-close (`archive-po-acceptance`), move `human-execution`/Deferred-Field-Test items to `454` via `defer-systemtest`, and keep `447` & `453` positioned below future Schichten via `/rest/agile/1.0/sprint/{id}/swap`.
* **Rule 9.2 (Worktree Isolation & Submodule Hygiene)**: Parallel subagent pairs operate in isolated Git worktrees (`Workspace: "share"`) on detached `HEAD`. Subagents SHALL NEVER create filesystem symlinks at submodule paths (`ANT-Android-SDKs`) or stage them via `git add -A`.
